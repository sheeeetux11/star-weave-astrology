It often delights me as to how Saturn and Venus seem like an odd match, yet they are often considered to be Friends in Astrology. Saturn brings arduous experiences in life which are amplified by difficult aspects to other placements, so it can be daunting when we see Venus in a particularly tense aspect like Opposition, Square, Inconjunct/Quincunx & a bit of Conjunction (of all, much less) with Saturn. Saturn being the Planet of maturity, constraints & long-term rewards; staying grounded, humble & devoted becomes the only way one can overcome the life lessons that he brings. Whereas Venus urges us to take things slow, enjoy the sweetness & comforts of life. These two though with different ways of expressing themselves, have quite a lot in common, making their bond an odd-yet-strong one. In tense aspects, more notably in Oppositions & Squares, you often see how it becomes harder to access your natural Venusian powers. Since Saturn tends to overpower Venus (or any other placement for that matter) bringing a sense of extreme control over its functions. 

This can be especially true if Venus is in Signs + Houses of :
Scorpio + 8H, Aries + 1H, Virgo + 6Hand if Saturn is in the Signs + Houses of :Aries + 1H, Cancer + 4H, Leo + 5Hwhere they are both in a difficult position hence exaggerating the negative impacts caused on each other. On the other hand, if Venus & Saturn are (both or either) in their most favored positions, one can have an easier time balancing the effects of the tense aspects.

Venus : Taurus + 2H, Libra + 7H, Pisces + 12H, Cancer + 4H, Aquarius + 11H • Saturn : Capricorn + 10H, Aquarius + 11H, Libra + 7H, Scorpio + 8H. Romance x Marriage x Home Life

You are likely to have a strong connection to your family, but depending on the placements & other aspects, this can be a good thing or really bad. Either your family had very strong opinions & restrictions, usually based on religious, cultural & societal views, and so you might have felt held back in expressing yourself truly, especially empathetically. Harsh circumstances & even recurring conflicts in your relationships can easily turn you cold & distant over-time. In many ways, (regardless of your gender) you might have had to hold your ground and be strong thereby not having the room to settle in & grow in your feminine side. Even if you respect your parents & elders, you might have a hint of fear or resentment towards them for how your relationship has evolved with them over time. 

Your moral-standards are strong and your ethical views keep getter more mature as you age as part of learning important life lessons, so in some ways, a cultural or religious upbringing or just certain societal expectations profoundly shaped your ideals & virtues in life.There is also blatant disappointment in your personal relationships; feeling unsupported, unloved & unappreciated no matter how hard you try, no matter what you sacrifice to do so. Your early years may have been difficult even with a good parental relationship, you might have had a hard time finding friends, to find common ground with them. Like-minded people likely came into your life later on in your early adulthood, whom you can depend on and truly trust. So, this tense combination can bless you with the strongest bonds in life, be it with family, community, friends, associates, authorities or in romance.

Romance can be a touchy subject due to past trauma, and Yes, I mean to be that dramatic. You likely grew up in a household with parents who didn't have an ideal relationship or were witness to toxic patterns in your family. You can easily spot similar negative patterns in your own romantic life. Since, this is one of the strongest aspect combinations that can indicate Generational Trauma/Curses especially relating to romantic & marital relationships. So, you might have had a hard time in your previous relationships or you might have had difficulty finding a partner. 

So, dissatisfaction in your romantic life can even (in extreme situations) can deter you from ever falling in love or to ever have a romantic relationship at all. Even with an ideal marriage/commitment, it is very likely (along with other placements & aspects) that you will feel the marriage is turning sour over time. This is especially true for Taurus 2H - Scorpio 8H, Aries 1H - Libra 7H & Cancer 4H - Capricorn 10H axis placements. So, serious breakup, divorce, estrangement and/or separation may be likely at least once in your life. But, this aspect can also be a positive combination where your relationship strengthens over time. 

Positives x Remedies x Karmic Lessons & RewardsThe only sort of romance that can work out with Saturn & Venus, is that of maturity (on both sides). So, if you are the mature, poise & well-balanced person, with a partner who is on equal footing with you, you are likely to see the magic of love blossom and take root becoming a strong tree over-time. Even with disappointments, occasional ups & downs, if you and your partner are invested in your commitment, you will be able to overcome all the difficulties and rebalance your relationship. This is where ‘knowing who you are, what you truly want,’ most importantly ‘what you deserve’ becomes extremely vital. 

Don’t accept less than what you deserve in relationships. Infidelity, manipulation, third-party situations, etc. that are both morally & personally devaluing, you will come across major decisive moments in your personal life where you will have to uphold your integrity & dignity. A pattern of noncommittal partners, or those that string along in promise of a wedding/official commitment, future faking, emotional & psychological manipulators (even abusers), etc. can come into your life as massive karmic tests and lessons. However, most people don’t understand that they will have to deal with you for a long while, even if it’s just a short interaction, you will most certainly make a lasting impression on them. 

So, nobody gets away with intentionally hurting/playing you, thats Saturn’s promise to you, to avenge you through karmic repercussions. Hence, your self-respect & self-love will usually have to be weighed against your love & desire for the relationship, to determine if the person is worth all the devotion, effort & wait or is it all just a waste of time. Since, if your loyalty & devotion (all the wonderful qualities of Venus x Saturn combination) is being misused intentionally, if the dynamic has turned toxic, you will be tested to uphold your integrity and leave (or depending on your situation to put your foot down firmly). 

This ability to discern between ‘a downright toxic & possibly abusive relationship’ with that of ‘a difficult yet (re/)buildable with time, patience & mutual effort - kind of relationship’, will be your Life’s Biggest Test(s) of Self-Love, Self-Worth & also in True Love. Also, generally time apart from your partner can be beneficial for the both of you. So, you can have a fairly successful partnership with someone who is equally mature, career-oriented & loyal where the both of you can experience life, happiness, love & companionship without being overwhelmed by the pressure of (usually high) expectations from these aspects of Saturn & Venus.

With all Saturn related Aspects, especially with Venus and in terms of Romance & Serious Commitments (Marriage & Business/Career related), it is always wise to take your time in ‘building your self-esteem, understanding your values & needs, along with gaining clarity over what relationships truly mean to you & what expectations do you set for a fulfilling connection, etc’. So, it is often recommended to wait until your First Saturn Return which will be after 29 years of age to get into a serious relationship, engaged and/or married (or even your Second Saturn Return, if you are wishing to start over). With all the tension, harsh lessons and even difficult circumstances that these aspects bring into your life, Saturn & Venus’s Incredible Combination can bring a wonderful committed relationship(s), with long-term fulfillment, as your Karmic Reward and something divinely guided & protected.
