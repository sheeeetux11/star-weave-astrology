Recent: 1989-90, 2002-04, 2016-17. Next: 2031

Your genius is a blessing indeed. But, you might need to temper your enthusiasm a little sometimes, since, you might seem a little rowdy or rude. You have excellent creativity. You are innovative, with a wicked sense of humor. You are social and simultaneously an introvert. You love to explore new studies, interests, places and even realms. You have a strong hold on your morality, but others may not always agree with your moral stand point. You are however just and uphold your integrity. You certainly don’t like to follow (all) transitions and rules, most of them seem unnecessary and frankly, boring. You are hence often deemed unconventional and unorthodox. You break rules with such finesse. 

You are bold and audacious, which can cause concerns from your loved ones. You express your opinions openly and courageously. You don’t hold back on the truth bombs, making you tactless sometimes. You are likely not someone who cares much about picking the words carefully or to be politically correct. This can be a fun aspect to have, but only until you are deemed as a trouble-maker. Your life takes interesting turns and you often don’t resist the changes. But, you might also secretly wish to create your own way in life.Your freedom and individuality should never be questioned, since then it’s war-time. You are extremely particular about your life choices, so you make them assuming all the responsibility for the consequences. 

You don’t like restrictions, but that’s all you seem to get often times, especially during your younger years. You are clever enough to trick people for a certain amount of time to make them think that you are finally over your rebellious phase and following the rules now. But, once you do get your way out, you are back to normal, back to yourself, unapologetically. So, in this way, you are quite adaptable, never losing your originality. Your wit and elegance make you quite charming, drawing a lot of admirers towards you. So, along with people who constantly seem to disagree with you, there are also low-key fans who love you for who you are. 

You are also more in-tune with the changing times, especially with technology. You have an open mind ready to look at all perspectives, but you are also wise, so you don’t get lost in the vastness of opinions in front of you. This aspect can make you rebellious, wanting your way or the high way, intuitive, and brutally honest. It is advised however to pace yourself and your self-expression according to the place & time, since that can go in your favor allowing you to express your opinions, win adoration, may be even get supporters for your brilliant ideals.
