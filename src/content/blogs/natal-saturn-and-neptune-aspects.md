Natal Saturn Trine Neptune 
Recent: 1975-78, 2001-02, 2011-13. Next: 2036.

This aspect can bring out the best of both Saturnian & Neptunian qualities. You are driven by your ideals, beliefs and faith. Your passions and life goals are well-structured. You know how to solidify your imaginations, so you can give form to your creative insights with ease. You always find practical solutions to even the most intricate problems. You take responsibilities very seriously and seldom give into excuses or laziness. You know the importance of being connected to your inner subconscious. You acknowledge your limits, but you don’t let them define you, you will always find a way to improve your skills/talents. So, in the process you also find new paths to success. You are always exploring the depths of your potential, since you understand the invaluable treasures hidden beneath. 

You are a practical dreamer. You are an optimist and a skeptic. It’s not easy to pull a wool over your eyes. You are firm in your opinions, it’s hard for anyone/anything to persuade you. You are righteous and honest. You are always finding new avenues to perfect your craft/gifts/skills. You are deeply spiritual. You might only place your faith on concrete religious' beliefs and be skeptical of anything ambiguous. Your approach to spirituality may be quite traditional, where you believe that structure, solid foundation and dedication are the keys to attaining inner peace. So, you might not always agree with the other side of spiritual practices which include escapism or other unconventional practices. Although, it is important not to become extremely self-righteous about your faith & religious beliefs, that you start to criticize the beliefs of others. Since, you might be prompted to feeling morally superior and, in the process, undermine others for their choices. This can cause conflicts especially when people start to find inconsistencies in your actions & words or start to criticize your approach to spirituality.

Your alone time is often spent doing things that bring you a sense of peace & relaxation. Charity, art, social service, activism, environmentalism, etc. are some of the things that you are actively involved in one form or the other. Your reliable and discerning personality can bring you a lot of respect and adoration from others. You might be sought after to guide/lead others through your expertise. Your dedication to your professional life will bring you a lot of recognition. You might choose to blend your artistic pursuits & spiritual beliefs into your career creating far more incredible projects/works. You work hard with long term success in mind, so it is likely that you don’t seek instant gratification. You know that the road to success demands the sacrifice of momentary joy & comfort. You have a strong will power and your inner strength is immense, which gives depth to your character. Your contributions to the world through both your career achievements and spiritual involvements will be well-regarded and respected.

Natal Saturn Conjunct Neptune
Occurs every 35-37 years. Recent: 1988-1990. Next: 2026.

This aspect in the Natal Chart can be one the most significant ones to consider. You might have had a harsh childhood, either due to unreliable parents/family, dangerous neighborhood, abusive family dynamics, etc. If you didn’t have a strong emotional foundation, you might have struggled a lot more growing up, and likely had to form your own emotional stability. You might have difficult relationships with father/father figures, mentors, and authority figures in general. You might have experienced hardships/abuse from them, or you might have been controlled/deceived by them, either way, you might have learnt it the hard way to never completely trust such archetypal people. Developing your self-confidence is a must with this aspect, since how you treat yourself will set a standard to how you allow others to treat you. If you lack in self-worth and setting healthy boundaries, you might end up in manipulative or even violent situations since it becomes easier for people with ill intentions to take advantage of you. 

Once you start to believe in yourself, make yourself a priority, and gain a sense of inner strength, you will notice a whole new world full of possibilities ahead of you. You have a gift of manifestation, since you can set your intentions high and work on them to make them a reality through practical planning. You can make your aspirations a reality, or bring your nightmares to life with the way you think. Positive mindset and thought process are essential in order to lead a fulfilling life away from the shadows of the past. You will have to make conscious effort to stay grounded with this aspect, since any escapist attitude/behaviors/habits will be shot down by rude awakenings. 

It’s extremely important that you are living in the moment and seeing things for what they are, since its highly likely that you can drift away in your own world (either of fantasy or horror) instead of facing the reality. Staying in that distorted reality can worsen your sense of self and potentially ruin your future. It’s vital that you take accountability for your actions and shoulder the responsibility for the consequences even when it’s hard to accept your (or others’) mistakes. Since, playing victim or shifting blames will only hide you for so long, if anything, with this aspect, you might get engulfed by lies and false narratives to such an extent that you won’t be able to dig yourself out of them. This can be vice versa as well, where you get involved with people who are always playing the victim and are good at escaping reality at all costs. So, it's important that you are grounded and not let any such behaviors/tendencies both from yourself and others to drag you into a place of darkness. 

It’s also important to read the fine print, look for red flags, double-check documents, etc. before getting into any kind of contracts or partnerships (including romantic ones). Making budgets, watching your spending/saving habits, securing reliable income streams, etc. will be a must since this can help in creating a sense of stability. Facing your fears/inner demons is a vital part of your life lesson. Self-sabotaging behaviors, defense mechanisms, additions, toxic patterns, etc. may come up to the surface repeatedly for you to take heed and resolve any inner child issues and traumas. You can break generational patterns, karmic cycles and toxic attachments with this powerful aspect.

Healing from the inside out is a positive manifestation of this aspect after all. It is also likely that you may possess strong intuitive & psychic gifts. You can use your wisdom and talents in your career, this aspect can give you a boost in integrating spirituality, artistic talents or psychic abilities into your works/projects. You can make incredible strides in your career/ambitions with diligence since you are good with using your gifts in a productive way. You can build a reputation through your works from both financial & social success. Having routines, plans and schedules can bring a sense of stability. 

Helping others through their healing journey is a gift you possess, although its best to use discernment when doing so, since you might have already encountered “wolves in sheep’s clothing”. Charity, religious/spiritual practices, social service, activism, environmentalism, etc. are some of the things that can bring you a sense of peace and contentment. You can have a beautiful life with your loved ones, enjoying the fruits of your hard work only when you can overcome your own limitations, work through disappointments & short comings, strengthen your inner self, build emotional security & stability, without losing faith in yourself and your capabilities. This aspect can be a tough one to navigate, but the rewards will be every bit worth the struggles & lessons.  

Natal Saturn Opposite Neptune 
Occurs once in every 35-37 yrs. Recent: 1971 - 1972, 2006 - 2007. Next 2042.

Pessimistic attitude towards oneself and others. Paranoid. The subconscious mind is always on over-drive, thinking & over-thinking, analyzing & over-analyzing. Not being able to distinguish between intuition and fearful thoughts of one's own imagination. Extreme caution and suspicion around people. Projecting fear onto every situation/person. Spiraling down into negative thinking. Either trusting others easily or not at all. Building tall & strong walls around matters of emotions & finances. Using Superficiality as a shield. Susceptible to deceit & fraud. Might turn towards alcohol and other escapist behaviors to cope with their self-isolation. Assuming the worst outcome and believing in it as if it IS the actual truth/reality. Living in their own world full of delusions and unstable comfort zones.

Truly facing one’s own fears and paranoia may help alleviate the energy blockages. Actively reconstructing one's mindset, monitoring excessive projecting, re-building the bridge to the outer world based on trust and compassion. Since it started in the mind, it should end there too. Finding hope in humanity. Being more active in community work, charity, may be even counselling/therapy (embracing the Neptunian qualities under the supervision of Saturn). A positive approach to life, detoxing the mind, getting in touch with the inner self so as to learn how to trust one's own intuition, finding the courage to leave the hurtful bubble of paranoia. 

Natal Saturn Square Neptune
Occurs every 17-19 yrs. Recent: 1997-98, 2015-16. Next: 2033-34, 2052-53.

It can be an overwhelming aspect to deal with, being one of the most difficult Neptune aspects to find in your Natal Chart. There is always a fear of the shadows from the past, paranoia is your constant companion. You must have endured immense karmic events early on in your life that may have drained you of self-confidence. You might have dealt with people/situations that were extremely toxic. Finding the courage to stand up for yourself and to face the illusions/manipulations in such circumstances may have been risky and live altering. You might have allowed others to take control over your reality without even realizing it at first believing in the good of others. Your innocence and sense of compassion without discernment may have often put you in harm's way. 

You might have felt restricted by your fears, thought patterns or illusions that others purposefully placed you in. You are a gifted individual, with a strong intuition, but external influences may have often muddled that connection to your higher self. There may have been dramatic ups & downs in your life that seemed anything but normal. There may have also been dark-spiritual & karmic attributes to the people & circumstances that you dealt with in the past. Self-limiting beliefs may have hindered your personal growth which you might have started to notice well into your adulthood. The same can be said about your spiritual growth as well. You might have realized just how connected you are to your surroundings and the collective consciousness that sometimes leaves you vulnerable to psychic/emotional attacks from people who have darker personalities. 

Cultivating your own belief-systems, strengthening your self-confidence, drawing healthy boundaries, increasing your emotional intelligence & discernment, utilizing your inner strength and facing your fears are your biggest lessons in life. With all things relating to Neptune, it is likely that you are susceptible to addictions and running away from reality to avoid remembering your past hurts. But Saturn here will present many delays and obstacles by not allowing you to move forward in life before you learn from your past and overcome your own fears. These trials & tribulations strengthen the bond between your higher self your physical self, allowing you to ground yourself and take back control over your own life. 

Breaking psychological patterns, karmic ties to people/places/things, shifting into a more optimistic mindset, etc. are some of things this aspect can helps you achieve allowing you to lead you more fulfilling, prosperous and emotionally secure life. You likely have immense artistic gifts & talents that are considerably inspired by your past hardships. You might have a harder time centering yourself and refocusing your life in a more positive way, yet once you accept them as just karmic cycles that you closed out and release those experiences, you will be able to progress much faster & farther on your life path. You can expect to see the growth in your personal relationships, career and spiritual life as you move towards your mid 20s and onwards. 

Your imaginative & creative abilities can bring you a lot of success. You might even be interested in spiritual-based careers. You might find inner peace in spiritual/religious practices, charity, activism, social service, etc. due to how such activities make you feel grounded. You are a practical person even when you’re indulging in your daydreams, so you can access the wealth of your subconscious and bring it into the world through your works/projects. You can be really proud of how far you have come by closing out such harsh karmic cycles/relationships and embracing your limitless future. Since, if you think about how anyone else in your shoes might not have overcome most of what you have, you can find solace in how your faith & inner strength shines through in even the most disappointment situations. You might hence realize how you were divinely protected and guided all along, bringing you blessings & karmic rewards.

Natal Saturn Sextile Neptune
Recent: 1995-97, 2018-19. Next: 2031.

This aspect offers the perfect blend between Saturnian structure and Neptunian imagination. You are spiritual yet grounded in reality. You are a dreamer, and an achiever, since you are painfully aware that ideals are only achieved through practical means. You can bring your dreams into the real world through pure hard work, and you often know how to set realistic goals with good planning. You know that the key to success is to apply your talents/skills in a practical way. This aspect helps ground the Neptunian tendencies. You are able to solidify your imagination & creative insights easily. You understand the value of hard work & sacrifice, you don’t mind going the long distance without losing faith in the process. So, it’s highly likely that you don’t believe in instant gratification. 

You have a great imaginative mind, but you also have a realistic attitude that helps you to be optimistic and practical, drawing out the best of both worlds. So, you are likely to master the fields of your interest with diligence. You have strong belief systems and a firm mind, so it’s not easy to manipulate or persuade you. You are generous and love to help others, yet you have the discernment to know who truly needs your assistance and who in just pretending. You are empathetic and understand the sufferings of the world, and you try to find practical solutions for them.You are intuitive, and you might even have psychic gifts. You know how to approach inner wounds, since you can understand the patterns and layers of trauma & suffering. Your empathetic insights are invaluable in your own healing. You can be a savior, helping others in their healing journey. This can even be with practical problems where you are able to solve intricate issues. 

Your leadership skills can be captivating and hence you draw in a lot of people who admire you & your work ethic. You might even be sought after to teach/guide others due to your expertise and excellence. Your good deeds & intentions always seem to pay off, since Saturn here will reward you in full. So, involvement in charity, or even just helping your loved ones can come back to you as good karma. Your healing aura can also attract a lot of people who need help. You might have to be careful not to be too practical while dealing with your own suffering and that of others, since you can’t solve every problem that way, so exercising compassion and logic equally in discernment will help you stay grounded and positive at the same time. Your grounded approach to spiritual matters can also help you to stay connected with the collective helping with the evolution of higher consciousness & simultaneously healing the world.
