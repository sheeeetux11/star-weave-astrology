On January 8th, 2023, Lilith entered Leo leaving the watery realms of Cancer. Lilith in the sign of self-expression, inner child, children, and creativity, will expect demand originality. Here, “you either go big or go home”, there is no patience for small minds and narrow views. The rage and internalized frustration will seek outlets in Leo’s themes. Lilith will stay in Leo until October 3rd, so we are already about halfway through her transit now (currently at 110).Lilith represents our inner passion, power that is equal to the forces of nature, raw and untamed. In Leo, it is expressed as undeterred self-confidence, bold sexuality, graceful sensuality, eye-catching, alluring yet not easily attained. 

With Lilith, also comes shame, inflicted shame, forced guilt and sculpted victimization. This astrological point gains its full potential through turning injustice into a reason for rebellion, pain into power, vengeance into life’s purpose. So, there’s great suffering within the darkness, that brews and culminates into power.You might have noticed a transformation taking place, what seems quietly happening within you, is loud when the rest of the world looks at you. Sudden events that seem to be forcing you to face your fears, even people/places/things that make you feel powerless, making it more and more easy for you to process the pain & trauma instead of running from it. You are braver and stronger now, mentally and emotionally.

You might be more expressive about your emotions, especially honest with your opinions. Anybody/anything that hold you back from your ambitions naturally become “the enemy of your progress”.

You might also be more confident about your sexuality, battling through any guilt or shame, snuffing societal narrow-minded opinions. Your transformational journey includes your inner child as well. So, all the internal blocks that you will be removing during this transit will also help you heal your inner wounds. Resentment, rage and hunger for vengeance may not really bring you the satisfaction you need deserve, but taking back control over your life and empowering yourself surely will. Lilith transit in Leo this year will be a glorious one, where you will find your voice again, and finally… ROAR.
