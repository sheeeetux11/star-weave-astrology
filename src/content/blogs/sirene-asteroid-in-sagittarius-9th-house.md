Sirene Asteroid in Sagittarius + 9H + Jupiter Aspects + 9° 21° 🐆✨

LIGHT ATTRIBUTES OF SIRENE
You are an intellectual, you like to delve into things head-first. Whether it is academic or artistic, you have a clear view of what your calling in life is. You are able to gain recognition for your work and become quite accomplished in life. Others look up to you with amazement whenever they see you following your Heart’s Desires & Soul’s Path. This can be a Popularity placement, very much so on a Grander scale. Your words in guidance can be very inspiring, so a lot of people will seek your advice, expertise & in general your support. Your generosity, optimism & happy-go-lucky attitude can be so mesmerizing to others. 

Religion, especially Philosophies around the world can attract your interest. So, you are likely drawn towards learning about theology, mythology & philosophy from different cultures. You also like to explore the world through traveling. Your own culture & religion can bring you a Sense of Purpose and grounding, along with exploring other cultures. Politics and Law can interest you as well, academically and socially. So, you can gain a lot of knowledge and sound-wisdom about a vast number of studies & fields. Wonder & wander can bring you many unexpected experiences and opportunities. 

You are innately lucky, so you can draw more Luck, Abundance, Blessings & Good Karma towards you through your virtue & morals. Your optimistic-outlook on life can help you weather any storm as you keep moving forward with faith & hope. The ‘World is truly your oyster’, so exploring it with your Intuitive Knowledge can lead you towards your True Dreams. You can achieve your Highest Ideals in life and inspire many. Truly believe in your own Inner Guide & Inner Mentor to lead you towards your Life’s Purpose and hence also Great Abundance & Immense Success. This is a wonderful Sirene placement to envision, manifest & work for success in all areas of life.

DARK ATTRIBUTES OF SIRENE
You encounter people who are somehow obsessed with your Charismatic personality, and wish to have what you have. They can see you as extremely lucky & blessed in life, and so become envious of your successful, peaceful & wondrous life. They may attempt to lead you astray from your Life’s Blessings, Opportunities & essentially your Life Purpose through all kinds of distractions. This can be through unfair criticism, bad advise & even unnecessary arguments/challenges. They can literally lead you into a trap or misguide you in order to keep you from ever succeeding. 

Your open mindedness can also get you into trouble if you are not being careful about who can influence you with what kind of information or narrative. Such people may seem quite knowledgeable about things that you are interested in, but they can easily misguide & misinform you. They may wish to see you missing out on important opportunities or even give you the wrong advice. They can also try to limit your Movement & Progress in Life, by preventing you from exploring New Possibilities & Understanding other Perspectives. So, they can mainly play with your mind, and your ability to explore your own True Potential. There will be lessons to not compromise your morals under anybody’s influence. 

You being authentic to yourself and standing firmly on your standards & morals in life can help you detangle yourself from their mess and to also deter such people in general. You might even learn a lesson or two on why not to be generous with your time, resources & effort to just about anybody & everybody, since there will always be people who are willing to do anything & everything to distract you while robbing you of your positivity & hope. You must be vigilant of those who are willing to do anything in order to bring you down, discourage, loose your hope, faith & optimism, etc. You will also have to be careful not to fall for others’ schemes & lies, especially when they only talk about their Grand Plans for their life, yet don’t really follow up with making their own Visions come true.
