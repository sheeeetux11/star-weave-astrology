When Sirene makes a tight Conjunction (or any aspect) of 0º to 4º orb with your North Node, you are bound to feel the profound effects of this mysterious Asteroid. Especially with Conjunction, Sextile or Trine, you can almost hear your Life’s Calling. You are captivated by your Life Path and even mesmerized by what is truly out there for you. Your soul-development can be tumultuous, especially with Square or Opposition, yet thoroughly enlightening. You will likely have to be careful with distractions that seem to pop up every time you have your sights set on your Higher Calling. It might even be possible to loose your path all together just to wake up one day and start your journey once again. 

You might also feel deeply confused as to what your True Purpose in life is, might initially chase after Goals/Life-Paths that aren’t your True Calling, but may have been tough & disappointing lessons instead. Circumstance, devious people or even your own Shadow can pull you away from your Higher Goals in Life; distracted, sometimes tempted, other times stranded; you might have to fight your way back to yourself and there by re-claim your destiny. There is a strong element of karma to your life path, a duty towards your soul’s higher desires and a vow to stay committed to your own future. There will be extreme tests of loyalty with regards to your Life’s Path and what you are willing to do in order not to get tempted out of your own journey towards Spiritual & Material Gratification. This is hence one of the most significant aspects in your chart. 

In Conjunction, Square or Opposition, you will need to be extra careful of those people and/or situations that can tempt you away from your goals & ambitions, here you will also have the opportunity to face your own Shadow Self and hence overcome your vulnerabilities in the process. Since this aspect is highly karmic in nature, on a spiritual level you might even encounter those who wish to switch their destiny with yours, in order to steal your good karma, blessings & rightful abundance. Be compassionate towards yourself whenever you realize that you might have drifted far away from your own destined path. Instead, use that experience to awaken not just to your True Calling but also to your own Inner Potential. Utilize your Innate Talents & Gifts to overcome the fears & challenges in front of you. Intuitively walk the Maze of confusion & distraction, redirect towards your Calling, untangle yourself from the webs of Karmic Temptations that taught you the lesson of being aware of the dangers on your journey and the importance of staying committed to your True Ambitions. 

If you have Conjunction, Sextile or Trine, you will naturally have a stronger Inner  alling towards your Higher Goals. You can pick up on the intuitive signs & guidance towards your Life Path innately. You can even attract the right opportunities to reach those goals. Honor your talents & gifts, be grounded & humble in the way you use them so as not to fall prey to the destruction of your own ego. You can seamlessly go with the flow of your Life towards your Destiny, attaining with it your Sacred Desires & Dreams. 

In general, North Node x Sirene Combination may seem ordinary from the perspective of an Asteroid, yet it can affect the journey towards your Life Path in the most subtle ways (both good & bad) possible, as Sirene embodies the Karmic element of the perils, challenges & temptations that show up when we are on our most significant life journeys (along with lessons) and towards our most cherished goals. Hence, it can also reveal the value of the goals that we are chasing, the journey we are taking and the abundance that is waiting for us as a result. This aspect in your chart can give you the biggest advantage in terms of your Innate Gifts & being awakened to your True Potential, while also exposing your weakness in the form of Distractions & Temptations. Your willpower, determination, wit, grace, adaptability, focus, clear-mindedness & rationality will be tested and rewarded karmically. Abundance, Spiritual Gratification, fulfillment of Sacred Desires, and attainment of Dreams & Aspirations.
