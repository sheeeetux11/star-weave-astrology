Sirene Asteroid in Aquarius + 11H + Uranus & Saturn Aspects + 11° 23° ⚡️✨

LIGHT ATTRIBUTES OF SIRENE
Your Uniqueness effortlessly stands out to others, mesmerizing them. Your originality, quirky personality and even radical opinions can draw people in. This placement can attract people who are not just smitten with you but also inspired by you. Your brave, calm & cool demeanor can encourage them to adapt to such qualities within themselves. Your approach to certain restraining & confining aspects of life, culture or society can open people’s eyes to how they can liberate from such constraints themselves. So, there is a huge ‘DIY aspect’ to the effect you have on others, more on ‘an intellectual, life style & world view’ way. Hence, you can become popular & famous even notoriously so just by being yourself. 

This can make you an intimidating person as well. None the less, your views on the world, even if it is about your own life can inspire others to have a different perspective over conventional ones. Social Injustice issues & Activism that you are involved in can also draw in a lot of attention. So, you can gain followers based on your opinions & thoughts in general. Your ideas & ideals can hence be massively Influential. Intellectual pursuits can gain you both good Social connections & support, especially if you are involved in the Sciences, or Futuristic, even Altruistic fields. Your endeavors in research, invention & discovery can gain traction, funds, & positive feedback. 

You are very likely to draw in friends and acquaintances wherever you go, almost effortlessly. Something about you can make others feel as though they can trust & rely on you, making them feel instantly comfortable. Hence, belonging to a tribe, group or organization that best suits your interests & values become vital & easier with this placement, simultaneously making the people who are around you more proud & enthused to have someone as charismatic as you. You have a lot to offer to the world, your intentions & actions can hence be immensely Impactful. You can bring your Dreams and Aspirations to Reality through your Brilliant Intellect, thereby creating a Wonderful Future for yourself and others.

DARK ATTRIBUTES OF SIRENE
This Sirene Placement can be very subtle in its negative manifestation, as you can encounter multiple people who would want to influence you with the wrong intentions. It is very easy to fall prey to cults & groups with toxic environments built within them, that can seem good in the beginning but turns oppressive & controlling in the long run. So, Social Justice movements with ‘good intentions’ but ‘bad ideologies & rhetorics’ can draw you in very quickly, but can go against your Core Values eventually as the mask slips. Hence, it is important not to get swept into Social Movements & Ideologies without fully understanding their true intentions & purpose. This can even be true for Political Movements & ideologies that are tied to Social Causes. 

Tribes & Groups that appeal to your vulnerabilities, Societal views & your previous negative experiences with injustice, can be completely on the opposite side of what your Values truly are. So, Radical Ideologies & Deconstructive Narratives of such groups can easily steer you in the wrong direction, distracting you from Real Solutions and possibly Peaceful Resolutions. Hence, you will need to be mindful of who has an influence on you and your views. You can expect Friends & Acquaintances in actuality to be Frenemies, Social Environments that become toxic and even Work Culture that doesn’t help you develop your Individuality. 

You might often meet those who are very much Virtue-signaling, chasing Popularity, Grifting or even Wealth through Fraud & Social Climbing. You might have had a hard time adjusting to certain Cultural & Societal views, even had problems with certain Authoritative Figures & Elders. You can be pushed to feel lonely sometimes with Sirene’s negative effects here. Finding strength in your Individuality and defending your opinions can keep you safe from being swept into harmful Group dynamics, and to honor yourself by accepting your Uniqueness. Your Originality, Courage & Self-belief are the Key to reforming both your Life and the Society as a whole, bringing your Ideals into Practical use, working on your Aspirations & Dreams to make them a Reality, benefiting both you the Individual & the collective.
