Sirene in Astrology can be regarded as the kind of people, situations or events that we encounter in our lives when we are at a significant impasse on our path towards our most determined goals and during challenging times when confusion, temptation & manipulation can easily lead us astray. These are often people who seem enchanting either with their physical qualities or of character that appeal to our ideals, hidden desires or fantasies. But, all that may just be an illusion and an elaborate act to lure you into danger in one way or another. 

They may be quite conscious of their intentions & actions or they may just be under the spell of their own subconscious delusions. So, we can meet them as sociopaths, narcissists, etc. (not necessarily as a complete definition, but as parts of their personality) that are Highly Manipulative, charming on the outside, wear Masks to fit-in and entice, have a Darker Inner World, scarier True Face, shallow & vain, Shape-Shifter, Fatal Seduction, Hyper-Sensitive Ego & Super-Ego, regard themselves very highly, Destructive & also Self-Destructive, etc. 

In general, these are the kind of Archetypes that you need to be wary of. Hence, Sirene’s Placement in one’s chart can indicate (on the Extreme Negative) the kind of people with the Archetypes and the Red Flags that one must stay cautious of. On the other hand, (and on the Extreme Positive) this Placement indicates the Natural Gifts one possesses that can effortlessly attract situations, people, opportunities, etc. and how to best utilize Sirene’s Innate Talents & Charm to attain one’s desires & dreams.
