(Neptune was last in Leo during 1915-29, next Neptune in Leo generation will start from 2078)

Neptune in the house of self-expression, creativity and romance is an excellent placement in the chart highlighting one’s spiritual & artistic needs being intertwined with their personality. You may come off as shy on the outside, but you are quite outgoing once people get to know you. There is a deep mystery to your aura that others often seek to find more of, which is why many people are drawn towards you. You are also in-tune with your environment; you can pick up on energetic shifts around you. So, you always make sure that to know how your loved ones are feeling. 

You make soulful connections with people and so you are deeply connected to their needs as your own. You are protective and supportive of your loved ones. Besides the soul-connections that you cherish, you also find solace in your creativity. You might have incredible artistic talents that you love to indulge in. You might be gifted in many skills that you can easily immerse yourself in. You might even be recognized for your artistic pursuits. You have a deeper understanding of your subconscious mind that allows you to draw out the many incredible creative treasures into the real world. 

Although, it might also be very easy for you to get distracted from your tasks and waste time on things that solely bring you joy. So, it is important to be careful about not indulging in things that only seem to bring momentary happiness. Spirituality is a big part of your everyday routine, either through your work or through spiritual/religious practices, which help you with your creative process. You are a kindred spirit with unconditional love & generosity for everyone in your life. Although it might take conscious effort to ground yourself in reality since you can easily get absorbed into daydreaming. 

You might have childhood wounds that you don’t remember or that you intentionally try to forget, but this could be the very thing that you will have to heal. There may even be issues from your past or past lives that seem to repeat themselves in how you handle emotional matters, in what influences your habits & addictions, why you may be afraid of commitments or issues related to abandonment, etc. Love & romance are a major part of your life. You are a charismatic person that a lot of people might have feelings for. You are also drawn towards people that give you a feeling of warmth and vice versa, others feel that about you. Your love life however can turn erratic at the drop of a hat. You have a yearning for soulful connections that are deeply spiritual. But you might not have a clear vision about who is right for you and who isn’t. 

Although you are not quick to jump into relationships, you might make sudden important decisions when you feel like you have found the one. However, you might suffer greatly when you don’t pay attention to red flags waving clear as day. So, it is possible that you might have endured some harsh wake up calls with regards to believing in only the good traits of people without considering their bad traits. You might even lack boundaries due to your naivety that everyone will treat you kindly just because you are a gentle & affectionate person. 

You will have to be ware of all the evil in this world as well as the good especially when your heart is on the line. Your important life lessons may come in the form of toxic, manipulative or abusive relationships with people of ill-intentions, overcoming such bonds may have given you a lot of wisdom (Of course, this could all be vice versa, where you may be the manipulative or abusive partner taking out your insecurities & traumas). You might have to be careful not to indulge in escapist behaviors or get involved with people who have severe additions or other problems since things can quickly turn emotionally or even physically violent. 

Your relationships (of all kinds) can directly affect your self-confidence and inner strength, so it is important to find what you truly want, what you truly deserve and how to honor your true self with only the best of things & healthiest of connections. With this placement, it is extremely important to break the illusions of safety, love & care from abuse (physical/emotional/mental), entrapment & codependency. Wherever Neptune lies in the chart can indicate the areas of life that get better with (both external &) internal guidance (and not influence), like inner & spiritual understanding, professional & medical, or the care of our loved ones. So, you might have endured traumas or psychological wounds relating to intimate relationships, creative expression or self-confidence issues. 

The unconditional support of your loved ones, meditation or even therapy can help you overcome your fears. On the other hand, you might end up with your soulmate & true love in the most miraculous of ways. You might be spiritually led towards “the one” with whom you can experience the true meaning of unconditional love. Depending on other placements/aspects in your chart and the intense lessons you have to endure, this can be the karmic reward of “true divine love”. Your connection to children (or pets even) may be spiritual in itself. You might have unbroken bond(s) with them which might even indicate to having a significant psychic link with them (although it is advised to watch out for any forms of codependency). 

You might however have to actively conform your right & responsibilities as a parent to yourself, since you might have a hard time fulfilling your duties due to other obligations in your life or may be even due to your own inner child wounds. So, depending on how you treat your inner child wounds and consciously navigate through your parental duties with sincerity & unconditional care, you might either create a beautiful soul bond with your children or make them feel neglected (since Neptune can represent neglect or absence). 

Overall, your self-development may be impacted by your creativity, intimate relationships and/or children (kids, pets, plants, literally anyone/anything in your care). This placement can initially pose problems in recognizing your own personality, expressing yourself genuinely, committing yourself to someone on an intimate level or ignoring red flags in toxic relationships, indulging in hobbies, habits & addictions to escape self-confidence issues, etc. But it can help you sort out your subconscious patterns, yearning for soulful connections & unconditional love, true recognition and most of all happiness in life. So, breaking out of your own self-defeating beliefs & patterns, finding solace in your creative gifts, understanding the meaning of true love & care, etc. will help you progress on your soul path and fulfill your higher purpose in life.
