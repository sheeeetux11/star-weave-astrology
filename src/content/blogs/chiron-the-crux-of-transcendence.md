Chiron in our Natal chart (& other charts) can show us what emptiness truly feels like. The seemingly endless void, left behind by unimaginable pain and trauma. Here, you feel utterly helpless & hopeless, worst of all this may be the very thing that you might have earnestly mastered (or were working tirelessly to master). It could be a skill(s), gift(s), talent(s), wealth, health, etc. But when tragedy strikes, it either seems to take it all away or rendered it useless. 

Like how best of the best medicine suddenly can’t cure the ailment it was made for, although it worked like a miracle up until then. Leaving one wonder “There has to be a lesson in here somewhere. Otherwise, the irony of it all is just too unfair.”Pain of any kind is not never truly comparable. Yet, it also doesn’t have to be an endless suffering. Even though the pain and wound may have been totally out of our control, whatever choices we make lie completely with us, in our control. 

Much like the Mythological Chiron’s story, we may be faced with an impossible wound (in this area of our life). But, Chiron’s decision (in his story) to Transcend above it all can likewise give us the key to handle and cure our Inner Wounds.After long endured, Pain becomes pointless, numbing. You get to choose whether you let it eat away at you any longer or do you allow it to transform you. To get to the root of your suffering, to acknowledge the emotional wound and heal it from within. 

Finding your Purpose to live & thrive again; adding Newness to your own Inner World; bringing back Love, Happiness & Peace into your life. Reaching a level of Transcendence. Creating a Selfless Purpose, which also fulfills your own Passions & Desires in a gratifying way, where giving to others also means you are giving to yourself. Opening up your heart to receiving first through selfless giving, then through letting others give good back to you. This way filling up that gaping void inside of you with something even more meaningful, truly fulfilled within.
