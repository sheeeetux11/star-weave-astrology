Sirene Asteroid in Leo + 5th House + Sun Aspects + 5° 17° 29° 🦁✨

LIGHT ATTRIBUTES OF SIRENE
You are blessed with a Unique charm and Magnetism that others find irresistible. You are also endowed with immense Talents & Skills that can bring a lot of Recognition. If you are drawn towards Artistic and/or Athletic pursuits, you are likely to achieve massive Popularity & Success. Your passion for everything that you do can seem so inspiring to others. Your Self-confidence and strong Sense of Self, especially your Courageous Demeanor can attract a lot of admirers. You can also manifest a successful Career & Romantic life. 

Your loyalty & grace can make you extra appealing to your loved ones. Your Romantic life can be fulfilling with partner(s) who can match your passion, vibes & values. You not just command attention from others, but others are also equally interested to get your attention. You can draw in many when you take the center stage and embrace the spotlight expressing your Talents & True personality. This is a Sirene placement that does well (i.e., even more Powerful) when one is aware of it. 

You can be Sensually appealing to many, you also have a captivating body language & communication skills. So, you can attract a lot of opportunities for both your Career & Relationships. Own your Confidence, Originality & Allure. Also, go easy on the (conscious) teasing, you can make anyone go crazy in love 😏. Excellent placement for Popularity & Fame, especially in careers/ventures that have to do with being in the Spotlight, Leadership positions, or expressing your Talents.

DARK ATTRIBUTES OF SIRENE
You encounter people who wish to ‘steal your confidence’. This can be very apparent in your Romantic relationships but also with friendships & family. You might have family/friends who don’t support your Artistic pursuits or Personal goals, they may actively hinder your Self-expression. Similarly, you can encounter people who make you feel as though you can’t truly be yourself around them, which can be a major Red Flag. In Romantic relationships, it is best not to move too many levels too quickly, especially sexually or even make decisions about commitment/marriage hastily. Since, you might not really give yourself the time to assess their character or True Intentions (as Sirene here can bring you sexually charged excitement very early in your relationship(s), which can be a Red Flag in and of itself). 

So, on the extreme Negative side of this placement, you might find yourself stuck in a relationship/marriage with someone who wasn’t loyal/serious/mature enough. Or worse, they might have ulterior motives and only like “the chase/challenge of having you”. There may even be temptations to cheat on your partner (or vise versa) when there are lows in your relationship which can only lead to heartbreak, were even simple discontentment can spiral into an abyss of resentment. Generally, it is best to be vigilant of  those who constantly criticize you, try to “humble you”, etc. since they may just be manipulating you and ‘chipping away at your self-confidence’. 

This also makes it important as to why you should hold yourself to a High regard in relationships (of all kind) and have a Strong Sense of Self. However, Self-obsession and not holding yourself accountable can also make you seem arrogant & unlikable, which others may consider as a major Red Flag in you. If you are in Professions where you’re passionate about being in the spotlight, you will need to display your True character and not become too focused on gaining attention/popularity, lest you are deemed Superficial, since your Talents & Passion will eventually bring you all the Recognition & Success that you desire and deserve.
