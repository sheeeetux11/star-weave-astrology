Background Pic by Anni Roenkae via PexelPluto’s last transit in Capricorn will end eventfully with an intense Opposition to Mars in Cancer at 29º. This Opposition will bring up many issues, fears & even traumas that you will have to face one last time as well, if not actually at least mentally & emotionally. Hence, the next few months (until Jan 3, 2025 when Mars & Pluto oppose again at 1º Leo & Aquarius respectively) will bring incredible challenges along with equally rewarding opportunities. With future-focused Capricorn and ingenious Cancer in the mix, the Opposition on Nov 3, 2024 will hence be a major reawakening of your own Passions & Desires, creating a tumultuous whirlpool of intense emotions & restlessness.For the next two months in particular, pay attention to how you’re approaching the goals that you hold close to you. 

This period will inspire you to give your all to anything you do (are doing), so it can feel like “All or Nothing” at times, further motivating you to keep going forward. However, with such a combination, things won’t seem to be easy or fun, especially with Mars & Pluto being in Cancer & Capricorn. There will be a big emphasis on your higher ambitions & career goals all the while family, security & the responsibility towards loved ones’ equally taking up much of your mind-space. Sacrificing one for the other will not be plausible, so if you are in such a bind, you can consider yourself to actually be in the midst of an internal war of desires & duties. 

The intense energy that is created due to this Opposition can also invoke your own Inner Strength, activating your determination to Succeed. So, this will be a great personal challenge to overcome your own limitations. On negative end, obstacles both old & new will present themselves, creating internal & external resistance to change as well as progress. So, it is advisable to be extra patient with yourself and other, especially with those who may be your opponents, competitors or even enemies. Instead of being straightforward in your response to such negative people, you will naturally be able to take a more reserved yet tactical approach, thereby avoiding more stress & conflict. 

So, be strategic and utilize soft power to overcome any such negativity without directly involving yourself in it if possible of course. This Opposition will hence give you numerous opportunities to understand a new way to handle such situations & people. It is also especially important to stay vigilant and careful with people in general since this time period can have an adverse effect on the whole of collective, and those who can’t get a hold of this energy can become a danger to themselves & others alike. 

Exercising caution can be a good approach to dealing with this rather Intense Opposition.On the positive side, allow things to flow instead of trying to control the process & the outcomes alike. Paranoia in general will be high after all, especially with regards to your Career/Personal Ambitions. Hence, you might be tempted to control & obsession over every little detail, which can be a wonderful instinct bringing out the perfectionist in you, but on the extreme can affect you mentally & emotionally. So, be prepared to find solace in the uncertainty and the unknown, the waiting period & delays, etc. 

Patience and a set of healthy coping mechanisms will hence become vital to your mental & physical health. This will be a great time to revive an old project or to finish a long drawn ongoing one. Dealing with delays, oppositions from others, internal fears, rejections & other issues can feel extremely irritating indeed. So, brace yourself for a rather intense personal development period in the process. This can be an incredibly productive period for you if you know how to adapt to changing situations, reinvent yourself, readjust your approach, and be strategic overall. Focus on either a long term project or a short term one, anything that has been on your mind quite sometime now. This can be in your career life or in your family/personal life, or both. Give this immense reserve of energy a direction, towards your Passions, towards an incredible ambition (no matter how big or small). Make the best use of this wonderfully chaotic Opposition.

Closing out karmic cycles & patterns, in both your career & personal life will be a major part of this Opposition overall. Hence, if this Opposition is affecting you in a personal particular way, you likely are in the midst of closing out karmic cycles related to either or both your career & family related issues. This can hence stake up your internal & external foundations, maybe even prompt you to rebuild them more securely. Your sense of security & stability will see immense shifts. Make little adjustments, one inch at a time, one step further at a moment and you will be able to see massive changes in your life, elevating you out of the struggles that you are working on. This time period will indeed test your resilience and almost give you no choice but to transform your life in a major way. I hope this phase will safely take us through the process, preparing us for the next exciting chapter of our lives.
