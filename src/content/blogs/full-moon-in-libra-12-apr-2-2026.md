🦢 Balance. Clarity. Harmony. ✨

🦢 The Full Moon in Libra brings much needed Inner Focus & Graceful Balance to ground ourselves through some of the agitation & intensity around us during the tense Aries Season.

🦢 Saturn in Aries Opposes this 🌕 creating a bit of a negative impact on both our Self Image & our Social x Personal Relationships. 

🦢 It is hence advised to take the time out to reflect & introspect about these crucial aspects of your life and ease into the changes you are considering both w.r.t. your Self-Image & Expression, along with that of how you interact with others.

🦢 You might realize how you may be veiling your True Self in order to be likable or to fit into certain relationships where you might feel that (especially in Family & Romantic ones) your Authenticity should be respected & appreciated instead of feared, criticized or rejected. 

🦢 As Jupiter in Cancer also Squares this 🌕, issues around codependency & unhealthy patterns in relationships will become apparent, all while giving you the opportunity to make important changes to your relationship dynamics to positively shift them into balance (or to let go for the Highest Good of all).

🦢 You will have wonderful opportunities to both analyze & realign yourself in these relationship dynamics, where you will honor your True Self while also being authentic in your Relationships. 
🦢 Commitments to your Passions & Ambitions may clash with those of your Personal & Social Relationships. 

🦢 However, these frictions (although stress inducing) will provide the neat little window of opportunity to balance your Personal Goals with that of your Relationships. 

🦢 This Pink Full Moon can facilitate a Win-Win proposition to even the most complicated situations in miraculous ways, only of course if & when everyone involved is onboard with the idea (which in and of itself can give us a greater understanding of who we are dealing with and if this is worth the effort or not).

🦢 So, this 🌕 can seem like ‘an intense Test of our Commitments and the necessary Emotional Maturity that is required to maintain thriving & balanced relationships’ (both within oneself & others). Re/building relationships on Trust, Harmony, Cohesion, Common Values, Care & Love. 

🦢 Ending, detaching & breaking down cycles, relationships & dynamics that are Unjust, Unhealthy & Imbalanced beyond compromise or repair can also be a challenging yet Karmically Important decision that you might have to make. 

🦢 A bit of ‘decisiveness and honesty’ can make this 🌕 a great little Game Changer in our lives. Bring into perspective both your needs, wants & desires along with those of others that you care about. Tact & Emotional Intelligence will go well with your Love & Compassion now. 

🦢 A lovely Full Moon where we can positively surprise ourselves. Enjoy the comfort and discomfort of facing important challenges of the Ego & the Heart, Self & Others. May this splendid Lunar event bring Balance, Peace, Harmony, Love, Bliss & Prosperity into your life and that of your loved ones. Many hugs, Much luv. 💕
