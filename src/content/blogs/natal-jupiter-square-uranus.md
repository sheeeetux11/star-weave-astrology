Recent: 1992-93, 2000, 2006-07, 2013-14, 2021. Next: 2027.

You are an innovative genius with a quirky personality. You have a strong sense of purpose that often involves changing the world in a major way, especially by fighting for worthy causes or by eliminating dangerous societal mindsets. You might however take things too far with your ideologies. Your life’s philosophy can inspire others and attract their attention, but your actions towards accomplishing those ideals may lead to disagreements and backlash. You have a strong need for independence and are dead set on never letting anyone/anything have too much power over you, but your need for freedom can sometimes seem like an act of defiance or extreme rebelliousness. This can create a tensed relationship with elders, parents, teachers, and/or authority figures.

You are a true lover of life, always trying to find new and exciting adventures. You have a depth of intellectual capacity that allows you to learn various topics and understand the nuances well. You are an exceptional ally to have since you are loyal and reliable. But you are also quite unpredictable which can cause trust issues with others. Your eccentricity can make you seem more individualistic which can cause tensions in group setting & communities. You will have to temper your extreme idealism in order to feel like you truly belong, since being upbeat & perfectionistic will only make you seem too unapproachable to others. 

Expecting sudden changes and enforcing your ideologies (no matter how crude or excellent) will only make you seem pompous and imperious. Instead of dreading mediocrity, by accepting old norms you can find inspiration to seek much more fulfilling & enriching experiences.You have the innovative and realistic gifts to make a huge difference in the world. But you will also need to discern “when to play by the rules” and “when to bend them to your liking”, since not everything is about breaking existing rules and reconstructing systems from the ground up. 

So, any tendencies towards extremism & risk-taking behaviors will need to be carefully directed towards productive and constructive avenues. You are a true inspiration. You have an influential voice of reason, but you will also have to accept responsibility for your actions. You can express your true-self and claim your originality through experimentation and seeking eventual perfectionism, but you will also need to understand that sometimes being in the present and enjoying the simpler things in life can be equally fulfilling.
