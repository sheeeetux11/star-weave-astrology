Mercury Square Ascendant 

□ You’re a controversial intellectual, in a sweet way.
□ You love to think & analyze and encourage others to do the same since you often feel that people aren't paying attention to details and that can sometimes frustrate you.
□ You have a playful-friendly personality that sometimes can get out of hands and become troublesome to others, but there’s no situation your sense of humor can’t defuse.
□ You’re a lover of knowledge, you love to learn, dive deep into details, and vocalize them appropriately.
□ You often feel the need to express your ideas, that are thought-provoking to others. This is where you might be considered controversial since you don’t hold back on your opinions, and they are always on point no matter how bitter the truth might taste to others.
□ This also gives you many skills and talents that you actively indulge in, since keeping up with yourself is a challenge well received for you. 
□ So, keeping your mind busy & sharp is extremely important for your mental health, since listlessness can severely disrupt your enthusiasm.
□ Your voice, words, opinions, and ideas can create waves of change in the world (for better or for worse, depending on your intentions). 
□ You might be well liked and disliked for the same reason. Controversies, rumors, gossips about you are a part and parcel of this Aspect, that you may have to deal with skillfully. 
□ Indulging in such things yourself might not go well for you either and could affect your credibility in the view of the public.
□ Although, since this aspect might suggest that with Mercury being either close to MC/IC (in most cases) or in 10H/9H or 3H/4H, you might have to balance between your personal/home life and your career/public life. There may be troubles in these areas that affect the other.
□ Since Mercury is your conscious mind and Ascendant represents your personal aura, you might have to be actively conscious of yourself, with everything you say and everything you do. Since everybody else can clearly see it too. 
□ You might have to exercise patience, while facing stressful situations, since you might be prone to become impulsive or even over-share this can also be seen in your body-language (which you’re also skilled at).
□ Your popularity can soar every-time you express your truth in an honest & creative way. (The other way around is true if your intentions are no good).
□ So, you might find success in creative & intellectual careers where you can sing, act, write, teach, speak, etc. where you’ll be well-respected and admired.
