Sirene Placement in your Natal Chart can indicate to your Calling in Life, hence this Asteroid placement can also indicate to Career(s) that can be both fulfilling & successful. This placement is not only be just about attracting Opportunities, People or Recognition, but also allowing your Inner most Desires to guide you towards your most Gratifying Goals, Ambitions & Passions in life. This is especially true if you have close Conjunctions (0º to 4º orb) to your Natal Planets, Ascendant, North Node, Midheaven and/or Part of Fortune. Hence, in addition to the Sign & House, Sirene’s Conjunction(s) and other Major Aspects to significant placements in your Natal Chart can indicate to Incredible Potential for Career Success, mainly through your own Innate Talents & Gifts. You might realize with especially the Close Conjunctions with certain Placements in your chart (but can also include other Major Aspects like Square, Sextile, Opposition & Trine + 0°), you are likely already involved in a Career relating strongly to that Placement, Sign, House &/or Degree.
