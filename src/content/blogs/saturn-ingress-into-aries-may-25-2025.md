Saturn moves into Aries after nearly Three Decades completing a full Transit of 12 Zodiac Signs. Before moving Retrograde again in just a few months (there by dipping back into the Sublime Oceans of Pisces until early 2026), Saturn here will bring rewards for work well-done during his Pisces Transit and present New Challenges in Aries. 

「 Breaking through walls of Dimensions. All New Life Changes, Shifts in Timelines. Completing MAJOR Karmic Cycles. Entering a New Chapter with a New Perspective. Enlightening. Empowering. Immersive. 」

With Saturn in Conjunction with North Node in Pisces & Neptune in Aries, we are entering a time of Immense Growth & Possibilities. Especially breaking through Limiting & Repetitive cycles. Some Inevitable Changes can seem Life Altering, handling such changes with patience & enthusiasm will be a lot more rewarding than to drown in anxiety & confusion. Here starts the Test of your Personal Character & Growth. 

Patience and Persistence are the biggest lessons & the strongest weapons that Saturn in Aries will bestow upon us. Expect increased personal responsibilities and possibly even greater commitments (in areas related to the House where Aries sits in your Natal Chart). Opportunities to move out of stalemates (& stagnancy) towards exceptional Progress will present themselves, of course demanding a more Mature & Grounded Version of Yourself. Commit to Self-Improvement and Soul-Development during the next 3 years, which will be rewarded with the Unbelievable Achievements that you will make, forever changing your Life & that of others.

With North Node & Neptune’s Conjunction to Saturn during the next few months, make the most of this otherwise small (teeny tiny) Window of Time to prepare to make BIG HUGE MASSIVE Moves. Personal Improvements, Career Projects, Financial Goals, Passionate Ambitions or even Commitments in Relationships. Any step you take now will need to be measured & grounded, as impulsive actions now will only be counterproductive. Saturn will also test your Capability, so expect to feel anxious and doubtful of yourself. Furthermore, you may not get the support of elders/authority figures easily, even your loved ones for that matter. Although, you will see how they slowly start to show their support & guidance as they see your diligence & genuine hard work (in any area of Life that you are called to focus on). 

This very brief Transit will give you a Good Look into the kind of Cycles that are about to Begin and are going to solidify within the Next Year. As Saturn in Aries is in Detriment, we are very much expected to face extreme challenges that will (re)shape our Character & Self-Image. Disappointments, Disrespect, Dishonor, Failures, Obstacles, etc. are to be expected, especially if Transit Saturn makes challenging aspects to your Natal Placements. For now along with Pluto’s Retrograde, Ego-Death and Painful Healing of the Self will take precedence in your life, personally & professionally. It is advised to seriously consider every challenge & test that you are presented with, overcome obstacles with humility, not take heartbreaks & failures to heart and not let these “Tough-Love” experiences to dull your Shine or Self-Confidence. 

As you learn how to balance ‘being Humble’ with that of ‘Standing up for what you want/are’, Saturn’s Transit now will bring the rewards of our efforts from his last Transit in Pisces while giving us New Assignments & Opportunities for Self-Refinement. There is much to be said about Saturn & Neptune’s Conjunction. But as for this particular Transit let’s awaken the Prideful & Passionate Warrior within us and allow Saturn to Embolden & Empower us through Strategy, Patience, Consistent Effort & Commitment. It’s time to build a Brand New Legacy and attain Unimaginable Success. 🔥💎
