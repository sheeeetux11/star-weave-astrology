Sirene Asteroid in Gemini + 3H + 3° 15° 27° + Mercury Aspects 🦜✨ 

LIGHT ATTRIBUTES OF SIRENE
Your endearing yet playful personality can be quite alluring to others. You have a brilliant mind, very intellectually driven in your interests, academically focused and/or into artistic endeavors, etc. You might actually be here, there, everywhere, doing the most exciting things and delving into the most interesting topics with an enthusiastic curiosity. This wonder and child-like spirit can attract a lot of people, especially those who share similar interests with you. So, you likely befriend many who have very similar mindsets and opinions as you do. This can become a fair advantage when in business/work settings, gaining you good partners, colleagues & supporters. 

You can hence make friends pretty much wherever you go. Your ingenious wonderment can also make you eager to understand many different things, where this placement can add to your Multi-tasking ability & hence Multi-talented, making you even more mesmerizing to others with how much enthusiasm and effort you can put into life as a whole. Your way of expressing your thoughts can be quite impressive to others, so your communication & social skills are likely excellent. You can use just the right words and the right tone, paired with a good body language to express your opinions, feelings & thoughts accurately, leaving people wanting for more. 

They can become quite curious to know more about you and what you think. This is therefore also a placement that can indicate to Popularity and Fame. Your talents & skills are hence valued and admired by many. Your own siblings, family & community can be very supportive of you, admiring everything that you do and everything that you are. Building a good social network, supportive community and friends is a major positive of this Sirene placement, making you equally resourceful, confident and successful. This placement can hence add to your Career & Public Success as a whole.

DARK ATTRIBUTES OF SIRENE
You encounter people who are just too good with their words, so pretty much anyone can fit the bill; manipulators, con-artists, … anybody. So, it is wise to stay away from people who think they are too smart and wish to get you into doing things simply because they are a little too charming. There is usually a big emphasis on their intellect or even talents than on their empathy or compassion. So, there is a high risk of manipulation, emotional and psychological abuse. This can be especially true with people that you meet through someone you know or through your Social Circles where you have a common ground of sorts. So, they may be con-artists who swindle money, want to pitch a bogus deal, make you invest in a fake fund, join an exploitative business/company/group, etc. 

This can also be through Social Media where you might follow people who seem charming & successful but are living a lie or have a ‘completely-alternate-cruel-true face’. So, the extreme side of Sirene here can have quite a distracting and majorly devastating impact on many levels of your life. Siblings, Family Members, Friends and Acquaintances may also become Frenemies which can be quite a daunting thing. There can be a risk of developing severe Trust Issues with this placement. But, not so dangerous as becoming too intellectually focused and calculative yourself, since this placement can make it easier for you to be influenced by others’ Shadow side, both good & bad. 

So, distractions in life can be astoundingly many, where without a really strong Will-Power and Mindset, you can be led astray by any number of things. So, as wondrous a placement it is on the Positive side, this can be an equally Devastating Placement on the extreme Negative side indeed. Having a strong Mind with Crystal-clear Intentions & focusing on Purposeful Goals can make you Successful and reach your Ambitions. So, Abundance and Achievements that are well-deserved for all your Aspirations, Skills & Talents will need your Conscious & Productive Efforts.
