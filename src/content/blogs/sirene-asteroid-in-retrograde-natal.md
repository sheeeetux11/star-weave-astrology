Sirene Rx in your Natal Chart can either appear in your life through subtle ways (almost undetectable) or make you embody the Dark Attributes of Sirene through subconscious ways. You might even feel drawn towards dangerous people or situations without even realizing it, until it’s too late. You can portray the Dark Sirene yourself in others’ life without being conscious of it. You might find ways to distract yourself from following your own Higher Goals, fearing the unknown or the overwhelming weight of responsibilities that come with your Higher Goals. 

You might hence become subconsciously comfortable attracting people, places & things that tempt you away from your own Calling, adding to your fear & confusion of your own Life’s Higher Purpose. You might often question your own Innate Talents without giving yourself the time & regard to explore your Potential. You might even miss out on key opportunities to express your Natural Gifts out of fear, misguidance or even Over-Confidence. Hence, Sirene Rx in your Natal Chart can enable your temptations and play on your vulnerabilities either through your own actions/choices or through others’. 

This might have played out significantly in your childhood & early adulthood. But later on, you might notice how you are being redirected towards your True Calling, since Sirene represents temptations, it also represents your Sacred Desires. Sirene Rx can hence indicate to karmic lessons that teach you how to honor your Sacred Desires and Higher Purpose in life over the Immediate Distractions & Shallow Temptations.
