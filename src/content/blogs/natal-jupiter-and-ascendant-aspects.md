Jupiter Trine Ascendant

△ You’ve an Inspiring personal aura.
△ Passionate, hardworking, talented, optimistic.
△ You’re also charming, generous, down-to-earth.△ Adventurous, ambitious, studious, friendly.
△ You might find good karma through travel, creative endeavors & being spontaneous.
△ You can easily accumulate fame and wealth (especially if Jupiter is in 5H/9H).
△ You might also make wealthy and affluential associations.
△ You will however need to be careful not to let fame/money bring down your moral standards.
△ You might also easily become lazy (Trine energies are not easy to negate laziness/procrastination).
△ You must also be cautious of Over-indulgence (especially troubling if it stems from instant gratification/mental health issues.)

Jupiter Sextile Ascendant

✱ You’ve a positive mindset & approach to life.
✱ You’re friendly and have a happy-go-lucky personality.
✱ Friendships/relationships play an important role in your life.
✱ You might make many wealthy/influential friends/connections.
✱ Yet you are grounded & kind-hearted, so you’re also popular.
✱ You can accumulate wealth and status over time.
✱ Although you must be careful with over-indulgence of any kind.
✱ You can accumulate good karma from kind-deeds.

Jupiter Opposite Ascendant (Conjunct Descendant)

☍ You might have to face occasional low self-esteem issues.
☍ You’re often times lucky in relationships/partnerships (especially business related).
☍ You might enjoy having a big & supportive social circle (if Jupiter is in 7H, the emphasis may be on friends/lovers. If Jupiter is in 6H, it may be regarding work/institutions/community).
☍ But there may be a need to learn how to be self-sufficient and assertive.
☍ Also, there needs to be a caution against Codependency issues, which can cause a lot of disappointments with others (friendships/business partners etc.)
☍ You might easily attract friends/partners from different backgrounds/cultures/lands, etc.
☍ You might have a tendency towards Self sabotaging behaviors while in stressful/highly competitive situations.
☍ So there might be a need to work on improving self-confidence, in learning how capable and resourceful you are.
☍ You might also have the ability to easily turn enemies into friends through your witty & cooperative personality.
☍ You might develop an interest in law/welfare of community/activism, etc.

Jupiter Inconjunct/Quincunx Ascendant

^ You’re lucky but are usually in-Denial of it.
^ You might have a tendency to Self-sabotage during sad/depressive times of your life.
^ A lot of self/character development will be a constant theme of your life.
^ Living within one’s means might also be a big lesson.
^ You might however have to be careful when over-doing/over-playing your part in any kind of relationship (even in charity/helping others).
^ You will need to humble yourself when feeling overzealous, likewise, you may need to count your blessings and realize just how talented you are when feeling unworthy.

Jupiter Square Ascendant

□ You might often suffer from Self-doubt. You could however hide this behind a confident mask.
□ You might have to be cautious about Overindulgence (4H/IC, food and sensual securities. 10H/MC, monetary and material pleasures).
□ Your Adventurous/travel/explorative endeavors may be profitable (both for career and self-development).
□ You make a truly Great Friend/partner.
□ You might however have difficult relationships with your parents.
□ This might also lead to excessive desires (even desperation) for freedom to live on your own terms.
□ You must be careful not to rely solely on your luck (which could easily lead to laziness/boastful attitude).
□ Or you could have struggled with believing that you are worthy & that you can rely on luck, early on in life.
□ You must also be careful “not to bite more than you can swallow”. 
□ You might find good karma in helping (sometimes just supporting & having faith in) family members/neighborhood/mother figure(s) (4H/IC), or coworkers/father figure(s)/businesses (10H/MC). *Careful not to go overboard though*.

Jupiter Conjunct Ascendant 

☌ One of the best (and my fav) Ascendant aspects ever.
☌ You’re Joyous, Inspiring, Generous.
☌ It may be easy for you to access good fortune, wealthy connections, prestige (especially if Jupiter is in in 1H).
☌ You’re also Adventurous, hardworking, a go-getter with all the luck in the world, a great friend/partner.
☌ You’re an Optimist, always looking at the good in everyone and everything.
☌ A lot of people admire you, want to be around you, learn from you and enjoy life the way you do so effortlessly.
☌ A big caution towards over-indulgence (if Jupiter is in 12H, for alcohol/escapism; if Jupiter is in 1H, with everything from alcohol, food to over confidence/self-centered behaviors, taking things for granted, etc.). Even in moderation, you will be regarded as Hedonistic (for good or for bad, you will be loved for both).
☌ It’s almost like you can make anything happen, all you have to do is go for it, and the Universe aligns it all for you. 
☌ You must however be very careful not to rely completely on luck alone.
☌ Also (especially if Jupiter is in 12H), you might have to be extra careful not to divulge important information to just about anyone (with opposition to Descendant, it can attract a lot of haters/enemies who want to steal your ideas/credits).
☌ You are somehow with a naturally giving personality who often doesn’t have any expectations. You will likely accumulate good karma from helping others (in general, for any charity, social causes, etc.)
