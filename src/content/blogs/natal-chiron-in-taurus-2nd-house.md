Natal Chiron in Taurus (2nd House)

Your wounds of stability go deeper than physical security with this placement. You have an intense need to feel safe, within yourself. You might have had financial issues growing up or lacked comfort in some way. You might even feel that your family or parents did not/could not give you enough emotional warmth as a child. You might have issues with materialism, where you either make them your substitute source of comfort or you might reject them altogether. Your self-worth is deeply wounded with this placement, so you might feel forced to accept your flaws even when you don’t want to. 

You might have insecurities about your physical appearance or how you present yourself to others. So, you always make an effort to look and behave elegantly/charmingly. You have self-worth issues that you seem to hide behind grandiosity or by acting overly confident sometimes, especially in professional settings. You might have experienced setbacks in personal/familial relationships that questioned your entire life or had to face your fears. You might however grow up to form stronger and more secure emotional bonds with your loved ones, especially the family that you create. 

You can heal your inner wounds of losing loved ones or not being able to enjoy such close personal bonds by being with the family that you create. You might even be able to provide all the love and affection that you lacked to your future family. You can overcome your fears of lack, both financially and physically by taking calculated risks and believing in your capabilities to succeed. You can accumulate a lot of wealth through hard work and determination, clearing any wounds of lack of financial stability. 

You will have to pay close attention to your everyday routines and nutrition, since you might feel lethargic whenever you feel pressured to take on many responsibilities or you might just feel lazy and ignore your duties. Your focus and sheer will power to stay dedicated to your goals and maintain a healthy lifestyle in every area of your life will reward you with a lot of abundance both in health and financial success. You can gain perspective through spirituality or religion. You also might be extremely stubborn about ethics morals with this placement, but you will have to learn how to let others live their lives without feeling attacked just because they disagree with your belief-systems. 

You can be a great advisor on financial literacy. Your strength in character and faith will be tested to make you stronger. You might also be admired for your grace and beauty of heart since your inner healing can be seen in the way you present yourself. Your self-worth issues will need extra reassurance as you climb further up the ladder of success, but your commitment to accomplish greater things will keep empowering you each step of the way. Your personal and professional relationships can be healing to both you and others involved. Your wisdom and compassion will help others to embrace their strengths & weaknesses, to never give up on themselves.
