Venus Sextile North Node

✱ You might experience many relationships (be it good or bad) of all kinds throughout your life that seem to elevate you on your life path.
✱ This aspect helps you have a steady and balanced approach to life, career, finances and personal relationships.
✱ You might be blessed with many Venusian talents related arts, self-expression, money, etc. but most of all, you are a true lover of harmony.
✱ You might be extremely loving & caring in your personal relationships, and always wish to see people you love being happy.
✱ You don’t like conflicts in general since you would rather compromise to have a win-win outcome than be reckless and lose it all.
✱ You might also be blessed with personal connections - friendships, familial and business. 
✱ But also in romantic partnerships where you might be able to find a partner(s) that helps you create an ideal relationship with cooperation & unconditional love. So, harmonious relationships become an important part of your spiritual evolution, helping you progress further in your life path.
✱ Since this is a NN aspects, you will still have lessons to encounter even in relatively peaceful relationships. You might be tempted to explore the shadow side of Venusian character, such as self-serving behaviors, greed, envy, control, vanity, or might be taught lessons on self-love, self-confidence, healthy giving & receiving, boundaries, trust, true love, etc. 
✱ You will likely work towards a prosperous life with loving & supportive relationships.

Venus Trine North Node

△ This may be one of the most easy-going NN aspects, that helps you fulfil your life purpose.
△ You’re a generous and compassionate person, who craves for soulful relationships.
△ Your personal relationships may play an important role in your life’s advancement and character development.
△ Your life path might involve interactions with many people who become an important part of your life.
△ You have a sense of destiny, and you only follow your heart that seems to lead you towards happiness.
△ You wish to create a prosperous home life and successful career life since you value love and security equally.
△ You attract people and resources necessary to elevate you on your life path.
△ You might even expect to have a relatively smooth ride through life with periodic highs and lows.
△ Like all Venus aspects, there might be a danger of taking opportunities & people for granted, become ungrateful in general, relying on physical appearance & deceit to gain favors, etc. which may back-fire in the long run since there are no short cuts to reaching the North Node and it’s likely to increase karmic debts in life.
△ You might be talented in Venusian skills like arts, finances, etc. which may bring you success in your career.
△ You will likely work towards a peaceful life, with wealth & luxury, respect & prosperity.

Venus Inconjunct/Quincunx North Node

^ You might have deep self-worth issues or feelings of abandonment/unrequited love that you project onto others or hold yourself back from connecting on a deeper level in personal relationships.
^ You might settle in relationships for the comforts & ease or keep things platonic & purely physical.
^ You might struggle in forming soulful relationships or have troubles staying committed in healthy connections.
^ You might ignore to address your toxic patterns, or others’ negative influence over your personal relationships, which might make things worse as years go on. 
^ You might feel empty due to the obvious imbalance in your inner world of love & satisfaction but choose to over-look it and instead point fingers at your failed relationships.
^ You might also experience many deceitful/vengeful relationships/situations where others might want to trap you in a money scheme, legal battle, marriage, etc. 
^ You might crave for stability and emotional security in love eventually but might still feel inadequate. 
^ You will have to face all the feelings of insecurities, emotional wounds, negative patterns/mindsets before you finally feel comfortable experiencing your own self-love and share a soul connection with others’.
^ You might also understand the concepts of true love, self-worth, trust and harmony in personal relationships looking at live examples of others in your life who have fulfilling & prosperous lives. So, it might be important to reevaluate your idea of love and happiness instead of giving into temporary pleasures that only seem to leave you depleted physically, financially & emotionally. 
^ Learning your lessons regarding love, giving & receiving, compromise & compassion will help you elevate in life and get back on the path of fulfilment. 

Venus Square North Node

□ This aspect can bring you many situations that might disrupt your concept of peace & harmony.
□ You might have certain ideas of love, harmony, success, & prosperity, but unexpected events may provoke you to realize there’s more, and to keep adding to your ideals of love and happiness as you grow though these experiences.
□ You might also have a tendency to overlook patterns that may be holding you back from true happiness and instead just choose to settle. 
□ Old and familiar situations/relationships might give you a sense of comfort, yet you might sometimes be afraid to take the leap of faith to explore new horizons.
□ Although Venus’s dislike for conflict might make you avoidant of making changes yourself since there may be things that need many adjustments and you might not want to rock the boat in your existing situations/relationships, however you will be forced to accept responsibility to make the necessary changes through sudden (sometimes painful) revaluations. 
□ You might have a hard time telling others’ “No”, being assertive, or drawing healthy boundaries with people and these issues may need to be worked on since you might feel a strong sense of dissatisfaction in life every-time you feel invalidated or over-looked. 
□ So being self-reliant enough to pursue your true happiness and compassionate enough to maintain healthy relationships becomes important in your life.
□ You will likely experience painful lessons relating to self-assertion, compromise, trust and honesty that will help you release unhealthy mindsets, toxic patterns in relationships, negative self-talk, etc. 
□ You will have to come to a fair conclusion in order to make the important changes in yourself and your situations since that may be the only way to welcome healthier more fulfilling relationships and situations in your life, simultaneously helping you go further on your life path unhindered.

Venus Opposite North Node

☍ This aspect brings one’s insecurities regarding love, compassion, self-worth, etc. into the forefront.
☍ You might be gifted in arts & finances; you might have immense talents related to Venusian careers/skills that helps you elevate in your life path for the first few phases of your life, but it might get harder to pursue this path after a while since you might become aware of your own self-worth issues that seem to make you invalidate yourself or others.
☍ A healthy balance of self and others becomes paramount in this critical NN aspect since matters of harmony might take up much of your focus going forward on your life path.
☍ You will have to work on self-love and appreciation more than giving to unrequited situations or being self-centered and rude to hide your insecurities.
☍ This might be prevalent in your one-on-one relationships - business, romantic, or familial. (If there’re other aspects/placements suggesting the same) You might experience entrapments, deceit, controlling relationships, or you might be the one doing this to others.
☍ Matters of love/trust and money might be connected to one another, you might find yourself being trapped in loveless or deceitful situations. 
☍ You might become extremely unsatisfied with these imbalances that seem to be holding you back from your true destiny.
☍ Or you might become ungrateful in potentially fulfilling relationships (depending on other aspects/placements).
☍ These unrequited & painful situations might help you release some of your own lack mindsets, toxic relationships/partnership patterns, may be even past life karma, to find what you truly value in yourself and others, eventually reaching a fine balance.
☍ You are blessed with many talents to lead a fulfilling life, when you learn the concepts of healthy boundaries, self-love without being self-serving or over-giving, financial education, etc. you will be able to love and be loved, experience luxury & comfort, gain respect and honors in life.

Venus Conjunct North Node

☌ Your life purpose is strongly related to your personal connections and how you view yourself connecting with others.
☌ You might find success in beauty, art, finances, fashion, music, etc. related careers.
☌ You have a serene personality that helps you attract all kinds of people & opportunities towards you.☌ You might also be popular due to your friendly personality and good people-skills.
☌ You are a resourceful person, with helpful connections, and supportive people.
☌ Romantic relationships play an even greater role in your life, since you might be blessed with supportive (even wealthy) partner(s).
☌ Although it might not be easy to create a healthy balance, you might feel distant or disconnected from your feelings to form fulfilling connections. You might have to go through many dynamics with others to realize the adjustments you may need to make.
☌ So you likely might find the right partner, friends, associates, etc. as you grow older and learn from many of the lessons with regards to interpersonal relationships.
☌ There may be a bigger danger of being ungrateful for all your blessings, self-serving behaviors, vanity, jealousy, etc. with Venus & NN involved, it’s important not to be tempted to do so, since North Node being a highly karmic point in the chart, you might end up racking up bad karma and eventually have to pay your debts.
☌ There might also be self-worth issues that you try to cover up with vanity, or with materialistic success which might make things worse as you grow older and not find true satisfaction. So, self-love and acceptance are also important life lessons that you may have to learn.
☌ You will likely work towards having blessed & harmonious family, social life, wealth, & honor, that helps you create a peaceful & prosperous life.
