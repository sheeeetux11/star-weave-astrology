Moon Conjunct Midheaven MC

☌ Your early upbringing had a profound effect on your world view and your purpose in life.
☌ Especially your experiences with your mother/maternal figure(s) and your family might have shaped your inner emotional stability. So, bad or good, reflects in your adult life and in your interactions with others, particularly in public/professional settings.
☌ You have a soothing presence that others are drawn towards. You make people feel at ease with your gentle personality. So, it is likely that you’re popular with a big social circle and helpful connections. 
☌ Your career path might involve you working with/for feminine figures, expressing your creative & artistic skills, healing/caring professions, or being in the public eye. Since you don’t mind sharing your time & efforts with others and enjoy their pleasant company. You’re also good with reading the room and hence you’re able to gauge the moods of others very well.
☌ You might prefer working on one project at a time, since you like to finish what you started with all the attention and focus you can offer. You also might not like to rush into anything. You take a lot of responsibility in the completion & success of your work.
☌ You might also be well praised for your work ethic, since you manage to put your all into everything you do. Although you might be temperamental depending on your external circumstances.
☌ You’re quite sensitive to your surroundings and the people you’re around. So, negative/toxic environments can affect your moods significantly and hence your productivity. Wherever you work, you have the need to be needed, a sense of belonging & stability. 
☌ You’re also likely to be intuitive (may be even psychic), you seem to follow your heart while making important decisions in your life and your life purpose might feel like a spiritual calling to you.
☌ Your emotional fortitude plays an important role in maintaining the public’s support, since every-time you show inner strength & lead with calmness you might gain the respect of others and other times, when you lose your temper, or show emotional instability, others might be repulsed by you.
☌ You’ll likely create works that are close to your heart & inspire others; establish many endearing relationships in your personal & professional life; spread positivity & compassion; gain support, respect, comfort and prosperity in your professional life and hence feeling content following your purpose/calling.

✱ You’ve a soothing personality that gives you a dreamy yet intriguing personality.

✱ You might be popular with many admirers who feel a sense of calm in your company. So, you likely have a big social circle & professional connections who play an instrumental role in both your personal & career life.
✱ Your inner stability is paramount to your outer stability. You likely realized early on in your life that you’ll need to regulate your intense emotions, since it affects public’s view on you. So, you maintain a calm & mature demeanor that helps you strengthen your connection with others.
✱ Your emotional fortitude also helps you focus on your career endeavors, giving you the intuitive edge to be productive and create incredible works.
✱ Your work is also an integral part of your emotional self, since you feel led to certain careers & projects. So, it's also unlikely that you would feel content settling for a job that doesn’t give you a sense of peace & comfort.
✱ You’ve an optimistic view on the world and your life path in general, since you often feel there’s a higher calling to whatever you’re doing, you sense a much bigger purpose to your life.
✱ You have a vision for your life and your works reflect your inner boundless creativity. You might be admired & respected by your colleagues & mentors for how responsible & sensible you’re with everything you do. Your workplace is also a place of comfort for you, so you’ll do well in positive & uplifting work environments whereas you might suffer in toxic work dynamics. 
✱ You’ll likely spread positivity & inspiration to the world; creating heart-felt & supportive connections; finding contentment in your professional & personal endeavors'; gaining respect, honor and prosperity following your life purpose.
