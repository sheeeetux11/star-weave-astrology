Trouble with Elders & Authority Figures. 

Jupiter in the mix actually lightens things up, especially for the reasons that serious issues now may need an open mind to resolve. With Jupiter, perhaps a much bigger & wiser approach to any situation bothering you right now will become the key. The fear of imperfection can give rise to not trusting your own capabilities.If you have been stressed, anxious & overwhelmed recently (which I doubt any of us aren’t 🫂🥹), you could be challenged to reprogram/relearn ‘how to ground yourself’ and ‘how to find balance in your life (personal, spiritual & professional)’. 

A stressful week or so, yet a fulfilling one with how much we can overcome in terms of our own mindset & handling others a lil more tactfully. There will be a lot of self-accountability throughout this month (Virgo Season + Eclipse in Virgo & Pisces axis). Yet, try not to be too self-critical. This can slip into your outside world where you will be likely to call people out for their irresponsible behavior, so I hope Venus & Juno in Libra will help us with good diplomacy. Take care of your health, & soak in some Sunlight every now & then to relax and recharge. 😘
