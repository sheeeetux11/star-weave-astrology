Mars Inconjunct/Quincunx North Node

^ This aspect could have made itself apparent during your early years through explosive anger, impulsivity and impatience (Shadow side of Mars).
^ You might have had to learn painful (even embarrassing) lessons on how not to get ahead of yourself in situations, especially those that question your values or your passions.
^ Your temperament needs careful conscious control in order to avoid unnecessary conflicts, even when you are being provoked. 
^ This may be one of the many lessons of this aspect that becomes important on your soul journey.
^ You might go through intense situations where you have to hold yourself back from defending yourself to the point of break downs, since with this aspect you can easily attract people who try their best to push your buttons.
^ With time, maturity and guidance you will be able to regulate your anger, even when unjustly provoked, giving you the upper hand in such troublesome situations.
^ This level of self-control will prove to be a gift since you will be able to direct your Martian passionate energy into your career/purpose helping you achieve greater things.
^ Exercising patience and taking calculated risks will help you create great breakthroughs.
^ Your calm & collected, reserved and methodical side will be much praised and looked up to by others, you might even gain their support when in tense situations later on. 
^ The other way around is also true, if you don’t make the effort to learn how to control your anger and impulsivity.
^ You’ll have to work through some of your own intense emotions and urges in order to balance how you express your passionate energies, and will likely be well-respected, honored, accomplish great things and notoriety with age & time.

Mars Sextile North Node

✱ This aspect helps you tap into your inner strength and stamina making you bold and adventurous.
✱ You’re proactive, energetic and driven, you know what you want, and you won’t stop until you get it.
✱ You likely have a goal(s) in mind that feels like destiny, especially during your early years, that made you work towards attaining it/them.
✱ You don’t mind walking on uncharted territories, since you have an inner guidance that helps you take calculated risks and land safely on your feet.
✱ You are a born leader; your successes inspire others and hence you can gain fans/followers/supporters along the way.✱ You are competitive, yet you are ahead of the game since your first competition is yourself and you always strive to be & do better than yesterday.
✱ Your physical stamina might also be really impressive, you might like to be involved in careers that demand you to be on your toes, to even work rigorous hours tirelessly.
✱ Your determination helps you bolt through obstacles and climb the mountains of success.
✱ You might be impulsive when you’re restless or anxious, but you also have the necessary inner balance to moderate yourself. 
✱ You’ll likely create breakthroughs, accomplish great things, inspire many people with your courage, garner acclaim & honor, and diligently make progress on your life purpose.
 
Mars Square North Node

□ This aspect brings the dangers of using Martian shadow side to the extremes.
□ You like to move forward with full momentum, but often might not consider the ups & downs of the journey ahead.
□ You are a great leader, but you might also need to moderate your tendency to be ‘recognized’ as a leader, since most people already know how incredible you’re and being too demanding to be known as so and so might only backfire and anger those who look up to you.
□ You might feel defensive when others oppose you, since you might assume that they are attacking you when in real they might just be expressing their opinions. So, you could end up creating conflicts even in otherwise peaceful conversations.
□ This can also be true in your personal relationships, where your demanding and relentless nature might go out of control and cause heart breaks.
□ You will have to realize that your innate sense of responsibility and determination are always recognized & admired by others. So, seeking validation in an aggressive & defensive way will only drive people away.
□ You might also have to understand when to take risks and when to wait for the right time, when to be methodical and when to make spontaneous decisions.
□ You will have to learn the important & painful lessons of containing your excess Martian energy, since it only causes more harm than good whenever you give-in to its intensity.
□ You’ll also have to keep your anger in check, especially when things don’t go your way. You’re a winner, but you’ll also have to develop humility from loses.
□ You’ll be able to create great accomplishments, since even in your setback & your successes, as long as you’re learning the lessons, you will keep elevating along your life path, reaching your biggest goals and attaining honors & notoriety.

Mars Opposite North Node

☍ You might not like taking spontaneous risks, even when you know what choices you need to make.☍ You might wait too long or procrastinate or freeze in the moment of high tension.
☍ Your indecisions may cost you a lot of opportunities, since your fear of failure, or even fear of success might be holding you back from everything you wished for.
☍ As a result, you may have many regrets, and disappointments can pile up making you feel even more inadequate.
☍ You have innate-assertive and leadership qualities, that you might need a little bit more of self-confidence to fully embrace.
☍ You might eventually learn how to turn indecisions into patience, and procrastination into methodical approach.
☍ So your abilities to take calculated risks, steady focus and balanced approach will help you elevate in your career and general life.
☍ You are courageous and passionate; your self-awareness will help you reach greater heights on your life path eventually.
☍ You’ll likely be able to move past any setbacks or disappointments successfully and reach your goals.
☍ Your struggles & battles are usually inward, not many may know much about how you overcame some of the most tiring situations in your life, yet they will be able to recognize & admire your stamina and inner strength that you exude through all those triumphs.

☌ This NN aspect is one of the few most intense aspects ever. You’re a force to be reckoned with when you set out to pursue your true purpose in life.
☌ There’s a strong pull towards your destiny that makes you ecstatic and anxious all at the same time, opportunities line up every-time you feel ready for a new adventure.
☌ You’re driven and won’t stop until you reach your goals. There may be goals that are more like ambitions to you as well, making you take things seriously.
☌ You have immense inner mental & physical stamina, that allows you to work tirelessly towards your passions.
☌ Your career path might seem like a solo expedition to you but that might be one of the karmic lessons you have to learn, being independent and assertive, with a solid foundation of self and others.
☌ With Mars here, you might also have to face many challenges that may seem difficult at first, but you will effortlessly solve them with courage and determination.
☌ You might be seen as a strong competitor in your field of work, since you always seamlessly climb up the ladder. 
☌ You might be prone to anger, since you expect perfection and precision in your work, so working with you might be a little challenging to those who don’t have the same stamina as you do.
☌ Although to those that have the will and skills to keep up with you, you are an inspiration and a positive representation of a bold leader. So, you might like to work on your own, or where you can lead others forward.
☌ You’ll likely attain immense success, honor & acclaim, inspire others through your courage and accomplishments, make great strides in your career, and elevate on your life path through diligence and determination.
