### Ascendant Opposite North Node

☍ You’re a hopeless romantic, yet you often feel disconnected from your true emotions, since you might mistake obsessions for connections.\
☍ You might feel the pull towards people who will likely teach you many karmic lessons with regards to relationships, of all kinds.\
☍ You might sometimes overlook your own needs, wants & desires in order to fulfill that of others’ and might get hurt in the process just to come back to self and feel unreciprocated & unsatisfied. So, it becomes important to involve yourself in **win-win relationships** and not in one-sided situations which only end in unrequitedness. \
☍ Your biggest lessons in life will be that of self-love which allows you to love others, without over-receiving or over-giving in relationships, especially on one-on-one basis, so this includes romantic, platonic, business, familial, etc.
\
☍ You might have a strong ability to manifest favorable situations in your life, especially if it involves other people, as in collaborations, partnerships, public interests, etc.\
☍ You might even be blessed with your Perfect Mate (especially if North Node is in 7H) &/or perfect work environment, social circles, etc. since *NN is Conjunct your Descendant.* Although you might have to endure many karmic lessons & relationship dynamics that may leave you feeling pessimistic about your happily-ever-after. 
\
☍ These painful lessons however only help you realize what toxic patterns you may be over-looking, what your ideal love truly looks like and what it certainly isn’t, so that you can make the necessary changes in order to attract and accept healthy-fulfilling relationships into your life.\
☍ You’re likely popular, every-time you embrace your true self, instead of settling or wearing a mask of a certain persona. Since your integrity and a strong sense of self (SN Conjunct Asc) are keys to how others see you. So, you gain fame and popularity organically & naturally. It is also important to learn the benefits of diplomacy, compromise & negotiation since you might be prone to anger & easily provoked by injustice (especially if *SN is in 1H*).\
☍ You’ll have to understand the healthy balance of self & others in your life. Since your life-purpose involves others for guidance/assistance/companionship but not as a necessity or bondage, so detaching from an obsessive need to be with others yet instead creating trust-worthy and truthful connections with them will help you reach your highest potential all while helping them reach theirs.\
☍ You’ll likely attain respect, honor, fame, prosperity, harmony, and balance in your life through strengthening significant connections and detaching from unhealthy ones, by realizing your self-worth and upholding the integrity of both yours and that of your loved ones’.

### Ascendant Square North Node

□ You might feel like if you wanted to go one way, life always takes you towards the other.\
□ There is often a sense of frustration and unrest with how you expect your life to be.\
□ Although every missed opportunity, unexpected U-turn, curve ball and detour in your life led you to much better places/situations than you ever planned for.\
□ You might have to deal with anger issues while facing such spiritual detours in life, since every-time your ego takes the blunt hit. \
□ So even when you end up in much favorable situations you might not be able to appreciate the blessings since you might get caught up in your ego.
\
□ Hence, it is important that you **believe in yourself and your intentions,** since your life path can be much more enjoyable if only you trust in all the things that didn’t work out for you and go with the flow.\
□ This also helps you shift your view on life, what you thought was your destiny might be something you’ve already overcome (you might be looking at your SN instead of NN), since it is likely that you have an even more incredible life-purpose full of untapped potential waiting for you (NN).
\
□ This will also help you to detach yourself from pursuing things that may be hindering your growth and to realign your focus on things that bring you a sense of satisfaction. So, it’s important to follow through on your path and realize that the paths that show dead-ends & extreme resistance causing you frustrations might not be right for you, but the paths that challenge you & bring healthy obstacles may actually help you reach your true potential and hence fulfillment. \
□ So, through all the ordeals & struggles, you might be able to learn how to discern what is your true purpose in life, and what isn’t part of your journey anymore.\
□ You’ll likely attain a sense of purpose and direction, go through intense character development, reach your truest potential and hence fulfill your highest goals & wishes in life.
