Jupiter Sextile North Node

✱ You are self-confident and an optimistic person who wants to look at the bright side of life. 
✱ You’re also a realist since you notice what’s wrong in the world. So, know how to blend both attributes to create positive changes in both your life and that of others.
✱ You feel it’s your destiny to spark a revolution or bring much needed changes in otherwise stagnant & unrelenting situations that only seem to cause more harm than good to others.
✱ So you likely are an activist, a leader, a teacher/preacher in your own right.
✱ You are generous and love to help others. You get a lot of joy in making people smile and spending quality time with your loved ones.
✱ Your positive mindset helps you attract the necessary opportunities, resources and people that elevate you on your life path.
✱ You’re also studious and obedient to elders whom you recognize as your guides/mentors, who help you ground yourself and provide you with timely advice & encouragement.
✱ You are humble and know how to show humility in the face of setbacks, and celebration in triumphs. People around you admire your down-to-earth nature.
✱ You face challenges head-on knowing that you’ll overcome them even if you don’t know the details, you have the fullest of confidence that things will turn out for the better in the end.
✱ You’ll likely keep elevating on your life path through optimism & faith, encouraging others to do the same, reaching greater heights, helping - teaching - changing things people and situations for the better by illuminating the way.J

upiter Trine North Node

△ You have a positive mindset that helps you get through the tough times and enjoy the happy ones.
△ You’re optimistic and a believer of all things good, so you’re naturally able to attract prosperity into your life.
△ You are focused and determined to reach your goals that seem like an inner calling to you.
△ The more you explore your inner callings, the more you seem to attract opportunities that lead you to your goals. Your explorative personality also attracts resources and influential people who are often willing to help you reach your potential in life.
△ So it is likely that you choose your career path based on your inner callings as well, hence gaining support, wealth & respect along your life path.
△ You are blessed with a good physical & mental stamina that helps you stay active, learn new and interesting things, and go on adventures of your own.
△ With all Jupiter aspects, and especially with Trine, it is important however that you never take any of your good fortune for granted or over-indulge & become lethargic after the initial wins. 
△ You’re always ready to involve in activism or charitable causes that are close to your heart, so your self-less deeds can help you amass good karma.
△ You have the right point of view on life, and this perspective helps you advance on your life path easily.
△ Your optimistic attitude and joyous disposition also attract many admirers who would like to learn from you, work with you, and share a good time.

Jupiter Square North Node

□ This aspect brings a lot of inner tension in accepting your inner callings and following what others expect you to do.
□ You might spend the early part of your life being diligent, learning and working towards a certain type of life, or settling for mediocrity, what the society usually considers as the norm, but slowly you might realize that you are exhausted and unfulfilled.
□ You might have to overcome numerous obstacles trying to follow what was expected of you, and through those situations you might end up discovering an alternate way to do things, a much simpler more innovative one.
□ You will have to fully embrace your need for freedom and creativity, since the mundane bores you.
□ You have good karma on your side to help you achieve your goals as long as you believe in yourself and make practical plans to reach them.
□ With your optimistic attitude you are able to attract influential people, resources and opportunities that help you advance on your life path. So, it’s likely that you don’t always give ear to pessimistic people or their cynicism.
□ You might initially feel like you wasted your time or that you should have chosen so and so paths before, but you’ll also be relieved to know that you might have actually paved a way for so many others, inspiring them to believe in themselves and explore new paths to fulfil their destiny themselves.
□ You will likely go against the grain every now and then but even through ups and downs you always end up reaching your goals as long as you’re open to learning from your mistakes and successes equally.
□ With this Jupiter and NN aspect, it’s important that you don’t take your blessings for granted and always remember how you had to earn everything you have accomplished so far. It is also critical that you uphold integrity and truth, since it’s very easy to amass good karma through good-intentions and equally easy to amass bad karma through devious deeds.
□ You’ll likely work towards a better future, inspiring others through your successes, gaining respect and wealth along your life path.

Jupiter Opposite North Node

☍ This aspect can cause a bit of confusion with what is your true calling and what is just a distraction.
☍ Jupiter Conjunct South Node gives you a lot of initial success, but you might get stuck in the loop of repeating the same experiences over and over again.
☍ This means you get too comfortable indulging in things that are only distractions, even the work that seems fairly easy and successful might just be holding you back from your true potential.
☍ You might get too relaxed in your comfort zone that it can feel like you have already made it in life.
☍ You’re an optimist and you can attract opportunities to help you advance further than ever before even if they seem extremely challenging. 
☍ However, if you played small, and settled to stay in your comfort zone, or indulge in the things you’ve outgrown, you might end up feeling dissatisfied.
☍ You might have to endure through some harsh wake up calls in the form of karmic cycles and events reminding you that this is just the starting phase, and you still have a long way to go.
☍ So, in your early years, whatever makes you feel comfortable and brings you easy success might not really be your true calling hence will eventually leave you feeling empty and numb.
☍ You will realize how more capable you’re when you start reaching higher, which is usually challenging and out of your norm, but you might also realize that this path brings you a lot of enthusiasm even through the ups and downs.
☍ You might even start to fully appreciate gifts and abilities that you never knew you had, and how you have come much further than you ever thought before, attaining more success, respect, wealth and prosperity along the way.

Jupiter Conjunct North Node 

☌ This aspect can bring the best in you, yet you might have to ground yourself often to not lose sight of reality.
☌ You’re a born optimist, but you might have had to consciously exercise your positive mindset since it wasn't always something you were used to.
☌ Self-belief and self-confidence may also have been things you had to consciously embody during your early years in order to fully make them a part of you.
☌ You know what your life purpose and goals are, and you know how to get there. You don’t mind taking a leap of faith or making spontaneous decisions concerning even the most crucial situations in your life. 
☌ Since you lead by faith and not always by practicality, you might have to exercise patience and methodical planning as well.
☌ Your career(s) are closely related to your life purpose, that makes you determined to accomplish them even more and hence take things seriously.
☌ You will likely find immense amounts of satisfaction and fulfilment while elevating along your life path since your positive attitude towards both failures and successes help you stay focused.
☌ There is however a bigger danger, whenever you over-indulge, rebel for the sake of it, and take your blessings for granted, since this can easily impact your further success and others’ view on you. It is also crucial to stay righteous and uphold your integrity since you can easily amass karma through your deeds and intentions, good and bad alike.
☌ Your ever joyous, expansive, optimistic, and brave personality attracts many admirers towards you, who respect you for your humility and courage.
☌ You’ll likely make an immense impact on others, bring fresh perspectives, attain great success, wealth, happiness, prosperity and honor along your life path.

^ You might start attracting miraculous opportunities, that can help you expand beyond the compounds.
^ Or you might find that you have the ability to turn things around to your favor and bring the experiences you long for towards you.
^ You will likely crack the puzzle to reaching your inner callings through your own optimism and self-belief.
^ You might gain immense amount of knowledge and wisdom through all your experiences that can also inspire many others who might be dealing with similar situations. Hence your successes and breakthroughs might become the guiding light of hope to others.
^ You’ll likely work towards improving your current circumstances, finding new and innovative ways to overcome obstacles successfully, making huge leaps on your life path, attaining honor, prosperity and happiness following your life’s purpose.
