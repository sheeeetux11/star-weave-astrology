Lilith in Astrology often points to how Human Shadow & Ego work in one’s life. There is always some kind of Ego-related wound that gets triggered/created either in others or within you when Lilith is activated in a chart. Often Dark themes like bullying, abuse, harassment, obsession, possession, murder, corruption, etc. are associated with Black Moon Lilith. Something about you can trigger someone’s Shadow (or vice versa) to such an extreme level that they may loose control of their conscience and perpetrate acts of violence (again, ranging from subtle to severe). 

Lilith hence simply exposes just how cultivated (or not) one truly is within oneself, since to harbor negative feelings & thoughts about someone is often considered Human nature, but to unleash one’s darkness (at all) brings totally justified concerns towards that person. Again, this is not an epilogue to justify any Human behavior, dark or otherwise. But, only an observation of Social Behavioral Psychology if I can put it subtly. Regardless of Lilith’s Mythological, Religious or Historical origins and validity, Lilith is primarily an Archetypal energy, which is very real & relevant in our Society and Human Sociology. 

In astrology, Black Moon Lilith is a placement that signifies potential for danger in one’s chart. Much like the Mythological Lilith, you can trigger someone’s Shadow based on their own Inner Wounds and the Darkness that lies within them (or vice versa someone/people w.r.t. you). In most cases, Lilith related triggers can go completely un-acknowledged by the one being triggered almost as if they are consumed by their own darkness, giving-in to their Negative urges and hence are able to justify their thoughts, intentions & actions. Belittling, controlling, harming & disempowering you might give them a strange satisfaction, high and/or validation. You can often notice a prominent Lilith placement (+ its activation) in the charts of criminals, Narcissists and other Dark Triad personalities.
