Mercury Conjunct North Node

☌ Intellectual talents & their development will be one of the main priorities in your life.
☌ With this aspect, Immense communication skills could be tapped into with ease, with little to no effort.
☌ Learning new skills, languages and you're a great orator.
☌ Exceptional mental fortitude and wit will help you progress in life effortlessly.
☌ You have a playful, easy-going and friendly personality.
☌ You know exactly what to say to appease others and this will bring you much popularity.
☌ You may enjoy a big social life with many friends and acquaintances.☌ You can be progressive in your thinking and bring many new ideas into reality.
☌ Any Mercurial career(s) will be a great choice(s) that can bring you many rewards & acclaims.
☌ There could be a danger of misusing your natural gifts of communication to indulge in fraudulent businesses, manipulating the public opinion, misleading others, spreading misinformation, gossip, using people to your advantage, etc. or you could attract such behaviors/people in your life often that you might have to carefully & skillfully deal with.
☌ You might also have to be careful not to swing with the popular opinions and not stand your ground just to look acceptable, since people-pleasing or image-saving behaviors could easily backfire if not at the moment surely in the long run.

Mercury Square North Node

□ You can be incredibly intellectual or not much, depending on other aspects in the chart which could lead to facing many challenging situations while understanding others and being understood by others.
□ You could have had a childhood full of stressful friendships & situations.
□ You may have faced challenges where you had to develop your intellectual gifts all on your own or with many criticisms/feeling unsupported even by those who were supposed to help you early on in your life.
□ So you might have had to learn that to over-come challenges, you will have to do things on your own without expecting much from others, and each time you come out with better ideas and feel even more empowered than before.
□ This aspect presents a lot of obstacles in your life, and as a result helps you take incredible strides, progressing forward always. But, it is also advised not to get too in your thoughts, as overthinking can lead to missing out on opportunities.
□ You might have many intellectual talents and they will help you immensely the more you work hard in perfecting them.
□ You might sound too intellectual or less of one, and so your ideas might sound far-fetched or outdated accordingly.
□ There may be many who oppose your choices/ideas due to lack of understanding, and this could be very frustrating to deal with.
□ Although any criticism and discouragement will only fuel your confidence even more.
□ One of the many lessons you have to learn will be to trust others the way you trust yourself and not everyone is against you, that you can believe that there are like-minded people out there who can understand you without much drama no matter how rare those people seem to be.
□ Delays & stagnancy can cause a lot of frustration in you, since you might feel like you are lagging behind and your progress has been halted somehow, however with maturity you will be able to trust in the detours of life.

Mercury Trine North Node

△ You are a visionary. You feel like you have a higher purpose in life, with a lot of wisdom to offer.
△ It’s as if life presents you with many opportunities to progress.
△ You are often popular among your peers and are well-respected.
△ Since often times Mercurial aspects tend to not prioritize empathy and may seem cold & intellectual, one of the best traits of this aspect may be that you not only can tap into your intellectual side but can also communicate through your heart.
△ Your loveable & witty personality can win a lot of people’s admiration & respect. 
△ Trine aspect can bring in laziness to even the best of planetary pairings, and with North Node involved, extra effort may be required to stay focused & dedicated to your goals.
△ Your career path can bring you many achievements and acclaims.

Mercury Sextile North Node
✱ One of the very few easy-going North Node aspects. 
✱ Mercurial talents are easy to tap into, and the more you learn, the more you feel fulfilled in life.
✱ Your love for learning new skills, about other cultures, languages, teaching, music etc. will be well supported by your family and mentors.
✱ Your fun-loving and youthful personality attracts a lot of admirers.
✱ You can dive deep into any topic of your interest and master it with much dedication.
✱ You can easily communicate your ideas & visions to others often gaining approval and popularity.
✱ Caution must be paid, since there could be a tendency to blend-in so much so that you lean into sheep-mentality which may come off as being superficial, therefore dimming your popularity in the long run.

Mercury Inconjunct/Quincunx North Node

^ There may be insecurities about your style of communication or may feel the need to not stand out.
^ You may have had to deal with memory or communicative problems early on in life.
^ You may have been sensitive about such issues or could have been bullied.
^ This could have either inspired you to learn and adapt more to your shortcomings to turn it into your own unique trait.
^ Or could have made you recluse from others even more.
^ Either way, you may have to deal with your self-confidence issues to the best of your abilities and trust that you will be supported by others if you opened up.
^ If not careful, much like difficult Mercury aspects (and other equally problematic aspects elsewhere in the chart), there may be a risk of developing personality-disorders or superiority/inferiority complexes which could be just projections of your own insecurities, this could mean you may cause a lot of trouble without little to no remorse (much like a trickster-God). Mostly with this side of the aspect, you may be famous for all the wrong reasons, at the cost of your conscience.
^ On the flip side, you can find a lot of recognition and fame when you decide to accept your uniqueness and heal your inner child. This way you might end up being regarded as an inspiration to many others.

Mercury Opposite North Node

☍ There may be many untapped talents with this aspect.
☍ There could also be a few fears, insecurities or difficulties in expressing those latent talents. 
☍ Your life path may involve communicating with others and the world at large, but you will have to also learn how to express your emotions and not just your thoughts.
☍ You may often have to face situations out of your control related to Mercurial themes.
☍ You may have to be careful how you express your thoughts in public, since they may be used against you, or cause controversies without you meaning to.
☍ Your communication style, playful attitude, wit, curiosity, etc. may be your greatest assets and also on occasion, your biggest downfall, both situations may be played out in destined ways.
☍ You could have been unclear about your purpose. There may be a lethargic approach to your life early on, but you may have had to mature in a rather painful way due to a series of hardships which could have shaped you to be responsible and reliable.
☍ This could have helped you refocus your life and its purpose, helping you progress with dedication and discipline.
☍ This could also help you attain much acclimation and respect in your professional life, reaching further than ever, putting your well-earned talents into good use.
