Natal Saturn in Gemini (3rd House) Generation 🐚✨

Saturn in Gemini/3rd house can make you an intellectual and resilient person. You have a way with words, and you have a way with silence. You have a more mature view on life due to the tough lessons you had to learn during your childhood. You might have felt coldness or distance from your family either due to circumstances or other serious issues. You might even have siblings that you look up to (or look up to you), on the other hand, you might not be able to connect emotionally with your siblings (family members in general) with ease. Your parents may have been restrictive especially when it came to you speaking your mind. They may have also been very serious about your education. 

You might also have had to study harder than others since you felt uncomfortable during your early education. You might have had to overcome many insecurities regarding schooling and studies. You might have been extremely studious, or you struggled to keep your grades up, either way, you had to develop self-discipline in order to succeed during your schooling. You might have been blessed with great teachers, but you might have also encountered extremely strict ones. You might have had trouble with your early friendships, who may have given you a hard time in some way. You might have met true friends later on who became an important part of your life, making long lasting bonds. You like to keep your friends circle tight even if they are few. 

You might have had to learn how to stay focused since multi-tasking in the beginning might have overwhelmed you, but with time you might have learnt how to efficiently manage several tasks with ease. Although you like the thrill of multi-tasking, you might still prefer to do things one at a time especially since you value the quality of work you put out. You might dislike the idea of wasting time, be it with projects, tasks, careers, or even connections. You have a sharp intellect. You are a keen observer; you prefer to listen a lot more than to speak. You might have had trouble with your voice, speaking or issues related to throat/limbs in some way that may have made you self-conscious. 

With time and maturity, you might have learnt how powerful your mind is, especially since you are precise in everything you say and do. You might have had to learn how to speak up for yourself and make your opinions knows, which may have been a form of restriction since your childhood. You might have also felt neglected when you tried to voice your concerns or ideas. There may have been certain fears and insecurities from your childhood that have become deep inner wounds that might have to heal and recover from. Negative self-talk can be your biggest enemy, especially since 3rd House/Gemini rules your mind. Hence, keeping your mindset positive and undoing any self-limiting beliefs will help you immensely. You might often feel attacked when criticized, you might also have been subjected to unfair criticism and you definitely hate unsolicited advice by others. You can be a harsh critique yourself, especially when you are overcome by emotions of inadequacy. 

You might have to be careful not to hurt others’ feelings or undermine their efforts when you are not feeling your best, just the way you wouldn’t want others to do the same to you. You have a depth of knowledge that others might seek, question, belittle or appreciate, but through all that, you will have to always believe in yourself and your capabilities. You might be good at research-based studies/work, you have an eye for details, and you always seek the truth in everything. You respect truthfulness and you might even despise those who are known to lie. You might not like small talks, gossips and indulging in rumors. 

You are an excellent communicator, but you might have had trouble accepting it for most part of your early life. You might even be good with handicrafts, if not you might be interested in works that need intricate detailing. You might even take up serious and rather difficult subjects like mathematics, science and technology that can stimulate your eager mind. You might not like public speaking, writing or expressing your skills that get a lot of attention. But you might feel a need to focus on improving your stage fear and embracing the admiration that comes with your work. You might even challenge yourself to learn one of these skills that you might have been afraid to try before. 

You might feel as though you have a calling to teach others. You might consider either to become a teacher professionally or find other means to guide people like blogging, workshops, seminars, tutorials, etc. You have a very particular taste when it comes to your surroundings, you might not like untidy places and prefer to keep your house clean, likewise you might prefer to live in a modest neighborhood. You might even consider moving to better locations everything you feel you need a level up in your life. You have a good grasp on finances, you can easily learn different ways of managing money. 

You might also be good with learning new skills be it writing, driving, swimming, etc. You are artistic and so can develop skills like singing, dancing, painting, crafting, sculpting, etc. You might find traveling to be either a hassle or a liberating feeling. You are shrewd and talented. You are strategic, patient and persistent. You can do well in careers related to Gemini/3rd House themes. Saturn here can make you an excellent teacher, artist, marketer, financier, etc. Avoiding scams, indulging in unnecessary rumors, rude behaviors, misleading others, misinformation, etc. aka, Gemini/3rd House’s trickster tendency will save you from Saturn’s harsh judgement karmically (likewise, you will be protected karmically from such fraudulent crimes/people).
