Moon Conjunct Ascendant 

☌ To begin with, Moon literally represents you, as a person, your temperament, your emotional self, etc. so it makes up your entire identity (just like how Sun represents your ego, your purpose, your drive, etc.). So, this aspect emphasizes that, even if your Moon is in 12H, being Conjunct the Ascendant, others will always see you for who you are, even if you don’t believe it or have a hard time accepting it.
☌ Here, honoring your feminine side becomes extra important regardless of your gender or sexual preference.☌ There’s an obvious mystery about you that is so appealing to everyone around you.
☌ You wear your emotions on your sleeves, yet you don’t mind it being seen, unless they’re insecurities since you’re very sensitive to how vulnerable you wish to be seen.
☌ You may be the sweetest person that anyone can meet, and I’m not exaggerating it, since your intentions are always out in the open, be it good or bad, others can always make them out, and so you’re effortlessly loveable & trustworthy.
☌ You are emotionally sound (even if your upbringing didn’t happen in an emotionally/psychologically healthy environment) you likely grew up to be a sensible & compassionate person.
☌ You wish to enjoy the company of others, being in your presence can be a soothing experience which reflects your moods, so if you’re not feeling yourself somedays, people can feel it right away.
☌ There’s a level of unconditionality to you that is hard to miss, and this giving-caring side of you can attract many people into your life.
☌ You are naturally popular, even if it’s not so obvious, you are loved, you make friends easily, you know how to form healthy relationships, you crave emotional stability in your interactions with others, you enjoy your daily routines.
☌ You like to include people in your life & work, others around you often feel like you treat them like family, so you bond on a deeper level with them. You might also enjoy taking periodic breaks, just focusing on yourself, recharging & relaxing.
☌ Although with compassion comes over-giving, so you could have learnt that over-sharing, or over-giving love/attention/care never ends well, and since it deeply affects your own sense of identity, also on a cautionary note, (being opposite Descendant) this is extra important in romantic relationships, where (if there’re Lilith/Pluto influences in your chart) you might attract obsessive/manipulative partners who could leave you feeling depleted, so you need to build healthy boundaries in your relationships.
☌ The same can be said about Mother, mother figures, feminine figures in your life no matter in what form, you might share profound bonds with such people in your life, and again, with other placements & aspects in the chart, this could be good or bad, meaning you could expect to experience happiness & harmony with them or jealousy & envy form them (no matter how subtle).
☌ You’re extra sensitive to smells & senses, you might also dislike violence (in your/others’ life & in the world as a whole), emotionally charged situations, disharmony, immorality, etc. 
☌ You are intuitive & might have psychic sensitivities. You have a spiritual bend to your personality.
☌ There must be caution in not allowing others to change your opinions, especially if it’s about yourself. As much as you like to listen to your family & friends, you must still be careful not to take disrespect, envy or manipulation lightly, but rather build your own confidence and stay balanced in your own sense of self.

☍ You have a warm heart and know unconditional love yet demanding it from others will only leave you dissatisfied in life, since most issues that seem to come from this aspect has its roots in your childhood or your upbringing.

☍ You can be an amazing business partner, or a diplomat even, since you know how to blend-in other’s feelings & good intentions into creating something worthwhile. 
☍ You might also find it hard to keep your romantic feelings for someone a secret since Descendant/6H/7H are public aspects of life & Moon here doesn’t make it any easier. You might also like to have PDA moments with your partner(s) & dream of a fairy-tale love-story (with other favorable aspects, and frankly a blessed partner, you might actually make this dream come true).
