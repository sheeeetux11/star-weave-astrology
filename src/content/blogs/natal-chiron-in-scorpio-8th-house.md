Natal Chiron in Scorpio (8th House)

You are loyal and trustworthy, yet you often encounter people & situations who are not quite honest, leading to betrayals and disappointments. You know how devastating losses and endings feel since you have experienced way too many since childhood. You have seen the worst sides to any situation so surviving through such harsh circumstances made you weary and cautious of everything and everyone. You might have encountered near-death experiences or had to endure the loss of loved ones early on in your life. Your childhood could also have been chaotic in some way that makes you feel destabilized even in your adulthood. You might experience losses even in monetary or partnership related endeavors just to learn how to improve your life and financial circumstances drastically later on in life since you are exceptionally talented at such matters. 

You might have deep wounds relating to sexuality and intimacy. You might even have turbulent relationship dynamics. You can be paranoid of situations that make you feel like you are being controlled or under somebody else’s mercy. You can also be quite controlling in certain areas/situations in your life. You might have strong psychic and intuitive abilities that can help you in all areas of your life even in your career endeavors. You possess deep observational skills that allow you to assess situations and people easily. 

You can develop psychological and emotional intelligence through your powerful insights and curiosity. You might be subjected to distrust or scrutiny by others due to misunderstandings or sometimes even due to hidden agenda/manipulation that made you over-protective of your image and not to become easily accessible to others. You are a passionate individual; you pursue your ambitions and desires with devotion and determination. You are also equally compassionate towards your loved ones, especially in intimate relationships. 

You are a natural lie-detector, your emotional intelligence can help you determine others’ true intentions easily, so you are hard to manipulate or control. You will however have to be careful of your own shadow side with this placement since you can become controlling, possessive or obsessive easily as well. Your healing will help you gain a much-needed inner stability from all the chaos that you experienced externally. Your ability to trust and love others will help you heal parts of you that were subjected to betrayals and feelings of being misjudged. You can turn pain into power with this placement, your transformations can be extremely painful yet life changing events. 

Your strength in character will be tested and solidified as a result. You must however learn to nurture and cherish yourself, since often you might be compelled to neglect your spiritual and physical well-being due to external battles. You will have to find empathy for yourself and self-love just enough to keep you in a positive frame of mind. This placement can also help you break generational cycles/patterns and close out painful karmic cycles successfully. 

You are compassionate and insightful, so you will also realize that vengefulness and other expressions of rage will only leave you depleted in the end, especially since you will come to see karma playing out in your favor in such situations on its own. You will inspire others to embrace their shadow-self and shed light on the deepest darkest parts of their inner-self. You can guide others through their toughest phases in life. Your protective and passionate side will be loved and cherished by your loved ones. You will also see how your healing can clear out pain and sorrow, bringing blessings and karmic rewards into your life.
