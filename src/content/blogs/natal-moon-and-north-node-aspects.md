Moon Square North Node

□ There may be a reluctance to believing in yourself, especially since a very young age.
□ You might constantly doubt your instinctive nudges even though you’ve a sharp intuition.
□ You might also let your fears and insecurities guide your decisions than your dreams & aspirations, which in turn blocks your intuition.
□ You are creative and artistic, you’re a people-person even if you look reserved at times, but you don’t always know where to direct your passions. You might feel like nobody understands you or might have had difficulty with feminine figures in your early life that left you feeling like you need to walk on eggshells every-time you have to interact with others. A lack of sense of comfort, intimacy & trust in relationships (of all kinds) might make things even harder.
□ You might hold on to your past out of guilt or a sense of responsibility/security, but a new fulfilling path may be nudging you to take a leap of faith.
□ You often know how to express your sensitive self, you’re deeply emotional, yet you might be guarded to ever let others know your vulnerability.
□ Although your ability to verbalize your feelings, your intense & deep emotional depths might actually be part of your calling. Your vulnerability, genuine emotions - pain, sadness, sorrow, happiness, etc. are part of your lessons, and so you are fully aware of how to connect to others on such a deep level.
□ There may be intense emotional situations that seem to realign you on your true path, since you might have been resisting change. You will have to learn the importance of accepting your own vulnerability before locking yourself & others out of your heart. Your passions and life purpose will help you realize how to balance vulnerability & find emotional security.
□ Your scars and journey to recovery have the power to inspire many others who have been through tough times. You’re a motivational factor in so many people’s lives, which in turn helps you heal while healing others.
□ You’ll likely understand the value of emotional vulnerability & security; use your instinctual skills & talents to open up on a heart-to-heart level simultaneously helping others understand both yours and their own feelings; forming reliable & relatable relationships; gaining confidence, developing your creative & intuitive skills, accepting healthier relationships with others & self; finding true comfort, stability & peace, fulfilling your life purpose.

Moon Sextile North Node 

✱ Every moment you are connected to your emotional self, you feel fully integrated with your life purpose.
✱ There’s a sense of reliability that comes with this aspect to NN, since you know that you can always find people to support your vision.
✱ You’re also a source of inspiration to others, especially your loved ones who enjoy a close emotional bond with you.
✱ Feminine figures (mostly motherly) play an important role in helping you gain a sense of stability in your life that in turn gives you enough confidence to follow your purpose. 
✱ You might also have Feminine figures who enter your life to transform your inner world, hence helping you become more mature in the way you ride the tides of your emotions.
✱ You’ll meet many who will want to support you and be a guiding light, in both good & bad times. You will return this feeling of intimate security to your loved ones.
✱ You can use your intuition and your rational mind equally well, which helps you to navigate through any situations, good or bad, in a soulful and tactful way. You’re rooted in your traditions and strong moral values, this also helps you stay grounded through all the ups & downs, adding to your charm, this emotional fortitude appeals to others.
✱ You might have many artistic skills; you’re an effective communicator, you know how to express your feelings without much effort; you have good people skills that help you make trustworthy connections in both your personal & professional life.
✱ Your generous, mellow and well-mannered personality attracts all kinds of people and opportunities towards you. You might be quite popular for the same reasons. You value such connections no matter how vague or deep they may be.
✱ You’ll likely enhance your intuitive & logical fortitude; nurture deep emotional & supportive connections; spread positivity, healing & compassion; following your life path with faith, endurance & purpose; cultivating happiness, comfort, and prosperity.

Moon Trine North Node

△ You exude a serene aura when in your element, that attracts all kinds of people and opportunities towards you.
△ You are in tune with your intuition, even if you’re not consciously aware of it. You have a natural emotional fortitude, that helps you to tap into your inner guidance.
△ You might have many artistic and creative skills, that you deeply feel are connected to your soul purpose.
△ You don’t mind making decisions solely based on your gut instincts, and fearlessly take a leap of faith. Sometimes, these sudden changes you make can startle even your close personal relationships, since you seem to start things even without making plans. 
△ You trust yourself enough to take calculated & un-calculated risks, and always land on your feet.
△ You follow your emotions, wherever you feel the most fulfilled & secure, so you don’t mind wandering in roads less travelled, eventually creating a path for others to follow.
△ You have a vision in mind, and you know how to get there. You rarely plan out every little detail, since all you need is the initial step and the route to the goal opens up on its own.
△ You are an optimistic person; this deep inner faith helps you to stay grounded even through the storms of your life. You believe that you’re somehow always guided by higher forces, that brings you enough inner strength to continue forward, safe in the knowledge that you’re following your true purpose.
△ You have a nurturing side to you, that helps guide others. You have a natural calling to integrate your spiritual self with that of your career/purpose, hence creating soulfully inspiring works/services that help in the collective healing, just the way you’re able to heal your inner most traumas and wounds.
△ You’ll likely work towards a better life for yourself and the world at large; spread positivity, healing and happiness; inspire others to walk in faith and trust their intuition; cultivate peace, comfort, prosperity, and compassion following your inner calling and fulfilling your life purpose.

Moon Opposite North Node (Conjunct South Node)

☍ You might find yourself repeating painful experiences over and over again in your life, even when you know how to exist them.
☍ You might often feel crippled by fears whether they are real or imaginary. Whenever you feel the urge to make positive changes in your life, you might stop yourself through the projection of your own insecurities.
☍ You might have had childhood experiences that were either true or a fantasy or both, which seem to have a strong hold on your life choices. You have a strong sense of fear of accepting help from others, even though there may be many who love you dearly enough to support you through anything that you may be going through. 
☍ You might also be extremely sensitive & emotional yet feel like you can’t fully express them to others, out of certain restrictions that you think are being imposed on you, even if there’re none.
☍ You might be blocking emotional intimacy or feel trapped whenever you’re in a healthy connection. Resulting in ups and downs, turmoil and distrust in your close personal relationships.
☍ You might also have a hard time embracing your intuitive gifts. Your past experiences, traumas and fears often cloud your judgement, and hence locking you out from your own inner guidance.
☍ You might follow a comfortable path, feeling safe and secure, yet feel completely dissatisfied with it. Changing course might be a painful experience since you were emotionally invested in your early path, even if you didn’t have the full trust in where you were going. But eventually, all circumstances, lead you towards your rightful path, helping you become more aware of yourself, emotionally and mentally.
☍ As you evolve and understand the depths of your own inner world, you’ll start to find the missing pieces of your happiness. Reconnecting with yourself and your true purpose might be scary at first, since there’s a lack of security that the old path (Moon conjunct South Node) might have given you. 
☍ Facing your fears also helps you to reconnect with your intuition, and hence getting back the access to your inner guidance. This also helps you to tap into your creative gifts, develop people-skills, form trustworthy bonds without the fears & insecurities, and display your true emotions to the world.
☍ You’ll likely go through intense emotional transformations; learn how to trust in your instincts and hence trust that there’s a higher force guiding you towards your true path; release negativity and fears from past experiences; show love & compassion without holding yourself back; nurturing your inner self and hence inspiring others to do the same for themselves; establishing security, stability, peace and comfort in your life following your life path with faith. 

Moon Conjunct North Node 

☌ You have a profound sense of self, even if it was a hard-earned gift.☌ You have a strong intuition, even when you’re not consciously aware of it, you trust your inner feelings & gut instincts.
☌ You can make split-second decisions, or plan for a bigger mission, you have the ability to ride the waves of change with ease.
☌ You naturally follow where your intuition leads you, you value your emotions and how the direction you’re taking feels rather than how it looks on the outside.
☌ You might have been through intense emotional situations, or had to witness them in others, which might have ignited a passion for healing, nurturing and providing security as a part of your inner calling.
☌ You are an endearing person; you know how to make soulful connections and nurture them. You might also have strong connections with feminine figures, whom you love and cherish, or wish to be an inspiring role model to others.
☌ You know how to relate to others and give off a perception of relatability to others. Your friendly and welcoming nature invites all kinds of people, opportunities and situations towards you.
☌ You yearn to create lasting relationships in both your personal & professional life, and so you enjoy a good social circle full of supportive and resourceful people. You have a healthy emotional balance, so you don’t over-take or over-give. You expect to form harmonious relationships, since you have a successful one with yourself.
☌ You integrate your compassion and happiness into your career and life path easily. Hence, providing the necessary guidance & support to others, in turn helping yourself heal through your own traumas & past experiences.
☌ You’ll likely express your genuine emotions and create balanced relationships; encourage others to follow their heart; spread positivity and happiness; create a healthy life for yourself and others; gain support, fame, fulfilment & prosperity following your higher calling.

Moon Inconjunct/Quincunx North Node

^ With this aspect, you will be put in situations where you’ll have to face your fears, no matter how painful they may be.
^ You might feel unsure of your path, even if you know what your true purpose in life is.
^ You might ignore your intuitive calling, and instead let fears lead your way. Eventually making decisions that put you in unfulfilling situations.
^ You might also have a fear of abandonment or rejection. Feelings of inadequacies and imperfections might creep up on you, every-time you wish to make necessary changes in your life.
^ But when you give into those insecurities, you might find yourself being codependent, emotionally manipulated or dissatisfied.
^ You might have to find the right balance between your needs and the needs of others, since you might over-give or over-take in emotional connections. There’s a longing for emotional intimacy and security that usually is at the root of all of your coping mechanisms and insecurities.
^ You will eventually reach a conclusion, to make the necessary changes, and to face your inner wounds to heal them.
^ You will go through painful yet satisfying emotional transformations that lead you back on your true path giving you the happiness and stability that you have always craved for.
^ Your biggest assets are your intuition, inner compassion and unconditional love. You’ll learn how to consciously use these gifts to move forward in life and inspire others to have faith.
^ You’ll likely understand the value of believing in yourself enough to trust in the process; following your inner guidance and facing your fears head-on; having faith, safe in the knowledge that there is a higher force guiding you every step of the way; using your life experiences to inspire others’ healing; spreading positivity and compassion; finding peace, true intimacy, security, comfort and fulfilment, following your higher calling.
