Natal Saturn in Pisces (12th House) Generation 🐋✨

Saturn in this ethereal house/sign is a soulful placement. You are extremely aware of yourself, sometimes self-conscious about every little thing about yourself and your surroundings. Saturn here heightens your sensitivity to the outer world, painfully so. Spirituality, compassion and sentiments are inseparable parts of your character. So, you might take your spiritual practices or charitable efforts very seriously. You are well-grounded, but sometimes to the extreme where you might develop low self-esteem (This can be quite a devastating placement if you have other placements and aspects hinting the same). Your subconscious is a vital part of you, even when you don’t realize it. If you are not careful, you can convince yourself that you are your own worst nightmare. 

Your thoughts and beliefs are powerful, they can create your reality, and so any self-suggestions can manifest in your habits, surroundings, decisions, relationships, etc. Without a purpose in life, you might lose yourself easily, Saturn here will make sure that you find your calling and be dedicated to it. Self-seclusion is a great way to recharge yourself, especially since you might feel drained easily by social interactions even when it's with your loved ones. Since this placement can feel like your worst critique is always in the back of your mind, you’re constantly aware of your thoughts and patterns. So, you will have a hard time if your self-talk was your own bully. 

Saturn here will help you to be practical and grounded all while utilizing your gifts to their fullest, hence, using Saturn’s guidance in a positive way will be a great way to accomplish your goals. Your success and failures affect you deeply, lessons and rewards will enlighten you equally. Sometimes it may seem like you are being forced to live in the reality rather than swimming in your own dream world. Saturn will help you bring the essence and knowledge from your subconscious world, you never know, you might be tapping into the ethers with invaluable knowledge, gifts and wisdom. Hence, creative practicality can be an excellent talent you might have to master. You might have a tendency to trust others easily, or be blind to red flags, constantly let others take advantage of your generosity or tolerate toxic behaviors. You might have also endured traumatic experiences in your past life/childhood that stayed deep in your subconscious quietly influencing your decisions and creating your patterns. 

Unravelling these threads of fate and delusion, distinguishing between the two, will become a lifelong journey of healing. You might have a strong memory, or you might even have the ability to selectively forget details of traumatic situations, either way, you might not realize just how powerful your subconscious may be until a certain point in your life. Sudden events may trigger your psychic ability(s), or you might already be vaguely aware of some since your childhood. You might notice how the circumstances in your waking life may affect your dreams, you might experience frequent nightmares whenever you are dealing with stressful situations, likewise, you might have happy dreams whenever you are feeling content in your waking life. You might even see prophetic dreams with signs, symbols, animals, people, etc. sometimes guiding you and other times warning you of events to come. 

You might have a profound religious experience during your travels (especially to foreign lands) or have a spiritual awakening out of the blue. Spiritual or even mindful practices/routines may help you relieve from insomnia. Your health, both mental and physical will need dedication and attention throughout your life. You might even be able to find help/guidance from professionals if you ever feel overwhelmed by setting up a health routine of any kind. You might be extremely sensitive to allergies, food poisoning, miss-diagnosis, strong scents, etc. You might generally be a selective person when it comes to choosing your friends and trusting your loved ones, but hidden dangers may always keep you on your toes. 

You might have also experienced betrayals, heartbreaks and drama from people who showed you their true colors when you had no doubts in them before. Harsh lessons early on in your life may have made you vary of everyone in your life. Setting strong boundaries will be extremely important.This placement can help you access your greatest creative potential. You might have artistic and altruistic talents. You might be a gifted artist, singer, writer, painter, etc. or you might even possess special Clairs. Discipline and devotion can help you bring out the treasures from underneath the oceans with this placement. You will have to wear a thick guard when it comes to criticism since there may be a lot of projection than truth in them. Projections of opinions, negativity, envy, evil eye, etc. may be a big part of your life. Although, any negativity that is projected onto you will go back 10 folds stronger since Saturn here will make sure karma is served, so your good-will and kind heart is sure to be protected karmically. 

Self-pity, sitting in despair for far too long, escapism, etc. are a big “no, no” with Saturn firmly placed here. You will be reminded time and again that compassion and sensitivity are essential human characters but not learning the lessons and making excuses to not move past painful experiences shall not be tolerated by Saturn’s sternness. Losses of all kinds may be extremely heartbreaking for you, be it losing toxic habits, relationships, materialistic things, people in unfortunate circumstances, etc. eventually making you quite mature in dealing with such situations. You might be compelled to establish a solid spiritual foundation for yourself, it may be a hobby, routine, career, charity, activism, environmentalism, etc. 

Learning about foreign lands, or even visiting such places can help you find some solace. Artistic talents may serve as your purpose and career. Donating unused possessions, volunteering, gardening, cleaning, swimming, regular meditations, etc. can help you access this placement’s true potential. Facing your fears is a big part of your healing, and ultimately, Saturn here will help you rest, truly rest at ease and find your inner peace. Your journey to healing and even salvation may seem like a long road, but this placement will help you master it with devotion, acceptance and self-love. Healing and fulfilling your purpose become your spiritual duties.
