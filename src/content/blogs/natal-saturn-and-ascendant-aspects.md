Saturn Inconjunct/Quincunx Ascendant

^ You have immense drive and dedication; you work hard to achieve your goals.
^ You have the determination to go the distance, but you might lack the consistency.
^ You might also feel like you are being restricted by others, especially elders/authorities, so you rebel against them or grow bitter towards them.
^ You might not like following the rules or feel like you are constantly being monitored. You feel that all your actions are under scrutiny.
^ This weary attitude also hinders your personal growth and character development. You might initially reject help/advice from others.
^ However, you might have suffered consequences for your defensive behavior. You might regret pushing loved ones away, or sabotage opportunities, etc. which might have resulted in extreme self-criticism.
^ You will come to realize that your hard work & determination lacks focus and discipline. 
^ Your passions lack direction and organization. So, your failures & setbacks are important lessons with regards to what pieces of puzzle you were missing before.
^ You will eventually learn to pace yourself better, act on your instinct and not on your impulses. Also, to respect others’ opinions without disregarding them out of fear of control. You might even understand that teamwork can take you further than ever before, and how others’ cooperation is truly a gift.
^ You’ll likely understand the value of looking at life realistically even when it is filled with cruelty; to practice self-acceptance and so you can appreciate your loved ones’ better; face the criticism without letting others’ opinions to affect your ego/self-confidence; learn from small mistakes in order not to pay a bigger price later on; gain perspective, resolution, patience & wisdom following your life path to attain your ambitions and find contentment.

☍ However, your reliable and committed personality is what everyone admires about you. You always finish what you start, even through procrastination.
☍ You take every opportunity seriously and so you are grateful for all the rewards & experiences.
☍ Your focus might be mostly more on others than on yourself. This can be due to work, or personal relationships.

So, you might have to endure harsh lessons with regards to close-personal relationships. ☍ You see love and marriage as a serious commitment, so you are loyal and caring towards your partner. But you will also have to deal with delays/disappointments in relationships which come as tests of commitment & character.
☍ You’ll likely learn how to appreciate your hard work; show consistency in your efforts by investing in your self-confidence; believe in yourself to make the right decisions and take responsibility for both success and failure; be flexible in your expectations from both yourself and your loved ones; gain patience, perseverance, compassion and fulfilment by following your life path.
