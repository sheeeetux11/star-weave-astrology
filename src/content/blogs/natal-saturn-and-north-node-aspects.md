Saturn Inconjunct/Quincunx North Node

^ There may be a strong reluctance in pursuing your destiny, due to lack of support from others.
^ Being neglected, ignored or treated coldly by elders/parents (especially the dominant parent)/mentors could have turned into deep childhood wounds.
^ You might also have an inner resentment towards such elders and always seem to clash with authority figures or feel restricted in your path no matter what you try to do.
^ With age, you will be able to gain more confidence in yourself and also able to trust authorities/elders in general who will be receptive to your needs.
^ As a result, you could have experienced pessimism, depression, lack of purpose, etc. especially during childhood.
^ There may be a need to develop good decision-making skills, discipline, methodical approach, and understanding how rewarding hard work & persistence are.
^ You will have to continue working on your self-esteem, step goals and make realistic plans without the fear of rejection/opposition, since with time you will find supportive people who will guide you towards your destiny.
^ Staying on-course, believing in yourself enough to dream big regardless of all the discouragement or negative projections will become your important life lessons.

‌Saturn Opposite North Node

☍ A challenging yet rewarding aspect. Early life could have been riddled with feelings of lack of purpose and a sense of destiny all at the same time.
☍ You might have understood the (painful) importance of duty, or you may be forced to assume responsibilities (even at a very young age).
☍ There may be a danger of being bound to restrictive habits, mindsets out of fear of leaving the comfort zone, and so welcoming new & innovative ideas might become a challenge. As a result, you could come across as pessimistic, hyper-critical or cynical.
☍ This attitude could easily slip into your view on life purpose. If not careful, you might miss opportunities to develop personally and spiritually due to your negative mindset and reluctance to accepting fresh ideas.
☍ You might have grown up learning how important persistence, hard work and self-discipline are, giving you a strong character.
☍ You will have to adapt new and positive mindsets, to overcome the stagnant cycles you seem to keep going through over and over again without much satisfaction, since combining optimism with your great work ethic will reward you with stable foundations and help you redirect to a life path that's more aligned with your true destiny. 
☍ Here Saturn will teach you how important structure and organization are, yet how to go with the flow following your karma without resisting it because of old/outdated ideologies.

‌Saturn Square North Node

□ This aspect creates a sense of lack of control over one's purpose.
□ Your life seems to be riddled with obstacles and challenges, often testing you to the brink. 
□ There may be a strong personal sense of duty and high ambitions that you actively seek to pursue. But the constant feeling of being over-burdened can leave you exhausted.
□ As a result, you might feel compelled to become pessimistic, losing a sense of self even, since you seem to associate your hard work & self-identity with your ambitions & goals.
□ The rejections might even leave you feeling defeated and to give up on your dreams altogether, making you feel even more miserable.
□ Difficult relations with father/parent figures, authorities can add fuel to the disappointments you experience in your life.
□ There may be a difficult relation with the general public at first as well, during your early teens for example, yet with age, your mature and solid character can easily dispel all the misunderstandings others have on you.
□ With age & maturity, you will find that persistence and self-discipline pay off with big rewards. This can mean a good position in society, well respected, may be even highly honored for your dedicated work, wealth and status.

Saturn Trine North Node 

△ This aspect is rather a special one, Trine energy is difficult to cultivate, yet since the strong driving forces involved here are Saturn & North Node, it creates a powerful sense of destiny.
△ Hence, there is hardly any chance for lethargy or lack of ambition (even in trying times) that Trine energy brings, in fact it is the exact opposite, with increased focus on one’s life purpose.
△ You could have been an inspiring and charismatic character from a young age.
△ You could have been through many challenging situations, often resulting in productive outcomes, helping you master skills, learn patience through the process.
△ It’s as if all the difficulties seem to arise when you are aligned with your destiny (more like positive lessons through obstacles) and when you are off course, you could experience stagnancy leading to restlessness, making this an indication as to if or not you are on the right path.
△ Your struggles inspire you to accomplish more, and push even harder, giving you a practical yet positive mindset. This quality often attracts the respect and admiration from others.
△ You may also be blessed with mentors/elders/authority figures who seem to guide you through your journey, helping you mastering skills even.
△ You are focused on your path, even with many detours and mishaps, you seem to be determined to achieve your truest potential.
△ This aspect gives you the ability to accept your flaws much more easily, with an optimistic outlook.
△ You will likely attain a good social standing, wealth and respect with age, blessed with the karmic rewards of your past endeavors.

‌Saturn Conjunct North Node

☌ This aspect helps to easily access the good graces of Saturn. You are patient, persistent, grounded, & organized, making you reliable and well-respected.
☌ There is a potential to attain good social standing, especially through your career endeavors. 
☌ You may be blessed with mentors who guide you through life's troubles, (especially after your 1st Saturn Return), giving you a sense of support.
☌ With a career or not, your life may seem very eventful with many situations often showing up as obstacles and rewards for work well done, helping you attain mastery in everything you do. 
☌ You may be compelled to find new ways of doing things, merging the old with the new, traditions with innovations. 
☌ This can give you the ability to attract the admiration of both elder and younger generations equally, since you know how to blend-in with both.
☌ You look serious yet heartwarming since you can connect to your inner self easily, which could have been a challenge during your young age. 
☌ This aspect doesn't mean that you will be rid of all the restrictions and burdens that Saturn often seems to bring, but quite the opposite. You will however learn how to effectively face such challenges, reaping many karmic rewards for the same (this is really true for any Saturn x North Node aspect). 
☌ You have a steady focus on your goals, and often seem to be working dutifully to fulfil your purpose.
☌ Karmic path and destiny are both intertwined in this aspect, which makes the journey towards North Node that much more rewarding, giving this aspect a sort of cleansing quality. 

Saturn Sextile North Node 

✱ This aspect gives one an optimistic outlook on life (surprisingly, Yes!). 
✱ Much like most other Saturn aspects, your early years could have been filled with challenging situations, often coming in as blessings-in-disguise. Yet you seem to gain more and more courage with each obstacle you overcome,
✱ You may be blessed with mentors who could have been particularly harsh on you, but you still harbor a sense of respect towards them since you had the opportunity to learn many important skills/lessons in the process.
✱ The challenges and difficulties you face time & again seem to always be redirecting you on better and much better paths towards your life purpose.
✱ If anything, all your struggles and consequent victories could have inspired you to create a stable and strong foundation for yourself growing into your adulthood.
✱ This resilient attitude gives you a certain charm that garners admiration from others (especially during & after the 1st Saturn Return), often inspiring others.
✱ The patience you learnt throughout your early years will eventually lead you to a path of good fortune, with karmic rewards.
