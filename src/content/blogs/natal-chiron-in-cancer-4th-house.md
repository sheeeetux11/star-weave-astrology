Natal Chiron in Cancer (4th House)

You might have experienced struggles early on in your life, either emotional instability or a lack of warmth from family, you felt neglected and rejected. In worst case scenario, might have even had to fight for your birth right in some way. Your home could have been the cause of your fears and trauma. You can be very compassionate towards others but neglect your own feelings in the process. You feel deeply and you express your emotions with honesty, yet you might feel misunderstood sometimes. You might even feel distant from your parents in some way or you had to fight for their affection/attention at some point in your childhood. 

You might however wish to create a loving and generous family of your own in the future. If you have unresolved issues with your parents or suffered through trauma, you might not want to settle and have a family initially or you might refuse to commit to your parenting duties. You can feel unfulfilled in your emotional life easily but also you might get consumed by resentment and anger. You can also project your insecurities and fears of abandonment onto your loved ones making them feel over-burdened by your emotional baggage. You can even become over-protective of your kids/family at the slightest hint of danger or even a misunderstanding. 

You have empathy in abundance with this placement, so you understand where someone is coming from. You are always there to nurture others and help them find stability in their life. You might even heal your inner wounds by forming a family of your own and giving them all the warmth and genuine affection that you lacked growing up. You can also encourage people to open-up emotionally and help them recover from their traumatic past. This placement can help you break generational curses, either from family or your birthplace even. You might even have certain memories from your childhood (or even your past lives). 

You might feel like you are being kept on the side lines or ignored sometimes, especially when your loved ones don’t make you their priority or when they are dealing with their life issues. You might be afraid of failure especially about letting your loved ones’ expectations down. You might even neglect your public reputation to uphold traditions, your family’s duties or help your loved ones. You are willing to make great sacrifices for others. 

Your inner fortitude will be tested time and again, your emotional strength will serve as an inspiration to others. You might have troubles with women/mother or feminine figures, be it in professional or personal life. But you can also be an inspiration to women/feminine figures through your accomplishments and commitment to both your professional and personal relationships. Your loyalty and compassion will help you heal your inner child issues and also motivate others to embrace their emotional self. You will be able to create a secure and strong emotional foundation for your future generations as you heal.
