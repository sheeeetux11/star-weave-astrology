This Combination in your Natal Chart can indicate to being extremely vulnerable, even exposed to the envy & hatred of others until you recognize your wounds and stand up for yourself gracefully. Sirene’s influence in your chart can be quite subtle yet apparent all at the same time. There can be a serious issue of self-destruction, even self-harm. You might have childhood & past wounds that can affect you to the point where you can start to develop many self-fulfilling-prophecies. The trauma & past experiences themselves can become a form of distraction in your life, leading to temptations in the form of unhealthy coping mechanisms. (Depending on other placements & aspects) You might even be (or have been) prone to substance abuse, self-destructive tendencies, toxic relationship dynamics, etc. 

Your Self-esteem can be severely compromised at some point in your life. So, the Inner Wounds and the process of finding relief from that pain may both have taken you on a very different path in your life than you have ever imagined. You can be easily influenced by others’ words, ideas & projections. Your Self-confidence can be affected by various things, especially the tragedies of the past. You are hence likely to attract people who seem caring & loving, ready to help you and give you the comfort you so long for, but they often turn out to be emotionally & psychologically exploitative (if not abusive). These people can even have their own pain and past wounds to work on, yet they may be more focused on trying to heal others, which might be misguided in and of itself. 

You can encounter people with whom you might initially relate to in terms of your past experiences and the deep empathy you share, but it can slowly morph into extreme co-dependency or even a level of entitlement as to becoming a weird savior-victim dynamic. Hence, it is extremely important to heal your inner wounds in a healthy & (possibly) professional environment rather than with another person or group of people, since there will be a risk of getting involved in something more detrimental to you, whether that was their initial intention or not. You are also likely to encounter people with extreme Mental Health issues and/or Personality Disorders, who need help, care and compassion. But, unless you are a qualified Professional yourself, it is advised not to become seriously entangled with such people or groups of people, since they can be very persuasive themselves and can take advantage of your empathy (again, whether that was their initial intention or not). 

Encounters with certain people in general will repeatedly emphasize & remind you to have stronger boundaries, develop healthy coping mechanisms and be discerning with your empathy.If you have deep Inner Wounds to heal, consider seeking the support & care of your loved ones, may be even a therapist or counselor. Since, there can be instances where your mind can play horrible tricks on you, your thoughts can lead you to bad conclusions (which I sincerely hope is never the case) and your Mental health can severely affect your physical, professional & social life. With Chiron & Sirene, this aspect combination can be a cathartic and transformative one. You have the Innate Gifts to heal and overcome many of the challenges that most would consider almost impossible to. Your insecurities, fears & vulnerabilities can also become your greatest strengths of healing. Hence, your healing journey can be a powerful one that can inspire you and everyone around you.
