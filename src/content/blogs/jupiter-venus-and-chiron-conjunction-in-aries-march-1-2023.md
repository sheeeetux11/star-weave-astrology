Jupiter and Venus make a glorious conjunction in Aries on March 1st, 2023. Later in the week, Venus makes an exact conjunction with Chiron as she moves forward through Aries. Jupiter in Aries has been an exciting transit with increased productivity, passion and a sense of acceleration in life. Venus in Aries has sparked an interest to balance all that intensity while enjoying the spontaneity. These two aspecting each other in a beautiful conjunction is a sight to behold (we can see this glorious conjunction play out in the night sky for the most part of early March 😍). 

Happening in the fiery cardinal sign, there is a momentum that is being created to speed up the slow-moving energies from all the heavy retrogrades we recently ended. A lot of determination to jump into projects that you always wished to start, more focus on routines, put plans into motion, etc. Love and relationships in general get a boost of optimism and harmony, although there may be tension with how pushy you are while expressing your feelings (and vice versa, others towards you). This whole week (and until the mid-March), we can feel the enthusiasm rising, our surroundings will start to feel refreshing with positive new energies flowing in. 
Jupiter has been Conjunct Chiron for a while now (and will reach an exact conjunction soon), helping on our healing journey, with an optimistic outlook on life. Venus joining them will add more positivity. Although with Chiron here, it won’t particularly feel like “roses & lavenders” on the outside, since issues related to self-worth, money, love & relationships, karmic rewards, luck & fortune, etc. will reach a peak in a positive yet melancholy kind of way. There may have been a sudden rush of positive energies sweeping into your life that you don’t know what to do with, since all your past wounds & insecurities may be making it harder to truly enjoy the shift. 

Jupiter & Venus conjunction is one of the most auspicious aspects that brings luck & fortune in all forms. With Chiron in the mix, issues relating to luck & fortune themselves may bring back bad memories where you might have experienced misfortune or didn’t have much luck in certain areas of life in general. If you have Jupiter, Venus or Chiron retrograde placements or challenging aspects to them in your chart, then you might have felt this even more deeply. You might receive enlightening intuitive downloads regarding previous unfortunate situations especially regarding money & personal relationships that will help you to make a final release of financial karma & break karmic bonds with people/places/things that are no longer in alignment with you.     

Jupiter and Venus are the divine couple in astrology, so this can be a great time to heal & harmonize our divine masculine & divine feminine energies within. Similarly, there can be healing in romantic relationships by clearing out karmic cycles and inviting cooperation, prosperity & peace.

You might be feeling more grounded and in harmony with yourself, integrating wisdom and insights at this time. You might also be realizing how protected and divinely guided you are when you look back on certain situations that you have overcome. You could be letting go of a past version of yourself in the form of releasing patterns & experiences that no longer serve you, and this conjunction is here to help you leave the past behind as happily as you are welcoming the new You. You are no longer holding yourself back out of doubts or fears of past cycles repeating again. There may be a sense of slow progression in your life, especially if you felt stuck & stagnant during the past 6-8 months. 

Fears of the unknown, waiting for the other shoe to drop, a sense of instability, financial karma, indecisiveness, etc. may be some of the aspects that you’re willing to make positive changes in. You might be more serious about your shadow work and releasing limiting beliefs a lot more than usual around this time, healing on a soul level. Acknowledging the past while letting go of the karmic cycles for good. Through Spiritual/religious & self-care routines, making use of this time to take adequate rest and release any inner blockages will fully prepare you to ride this wave of positive changes a lot easier. You can expect to see advancements in professional & personal life. This conjunction is bringing in a much-needed shift in perspective by igniting a passion for life with a renewed faith & inner strength.
