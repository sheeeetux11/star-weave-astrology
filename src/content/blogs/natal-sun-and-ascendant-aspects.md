□ So, you likely don’t take failures and setbacks lightly, if you’re not careful, you might even feel defeated when you are.
□ You might also be in danger of basing your self-image on how successful you are in your public/professional life. Hence, your self-confidence fluctuates with successes & failures.
□ You might have a hard time accepting help from others, even in the form of advice, since you think its criticism or weakness on your part.
□ This can also be true when it comes to your relationship with authority figures/elders/mentors. But you will have to break through that negative mindset since you need to accept help and guidance in order to move farther than you have been before.
□ You are a capable leader, with an incredible work-ethic. Others admire your works/services since you know how to integrate your passions and creativity. But you will have to learn how to manage your dominant personality when working with others and understand the importance of compromise and diplomacy.
□ You will go through many situations that challenge your public image, especially those from your enemies/oppositions. You will be called to work on your insecurities and fears in order to overcome such challenges. Hence, you will be able to develop your character and strengthen your sense of self, by reinventing your self-image & public image.
□ You’ll likely make significant progress on your professional & personal life through your journey of self-development; make impactful & creative works/services and inspire others through your successes; learn to embrace your fears of inadequacy and move forward with reviewed self-confidence; gain wealth, recognition, respect and power following your inner calling and find contentment in your professional/public accomplishments.
