Natal Chiron in Gemini (3rd House)

Your inner wounds of communication can have their roots in your childhood either from siblings/family or schooling/friends (even teachers). You might even feel like you need to speak a lot to make sense of what you are thinking or speak too little when you feel not welcomed in a place/conversation. You might have experienced challenges in your early schooling. You might even have been/or become the victim of rumors and gossip. You might feel ignored, or others read too much into what you say, either way, you often feel misunderstood. You might not know when to use silence and words as an answer. You might even over-explain your actions sometimes out of fear of misunderstandings. 

You might have been criticized unfairly as a kid. You will however have to be careful not to criticize others too harshly since you might just be projecting your wounds and insecurities onto others. In worst cases, you can be too cutting with your words even to your loved ones. You are gifted with many creative talents, and you can learn technical/mechanical skills. You are curious and quick-minded, but you will also need to learn not to let your curiosity make impulsive decisions. You have great communication & people skills, yet you hold back when you need to express yourself. You can use your talents as an outlet to express yourself when in doubt. 

You might not be thrilled about being popular at first, but you will eventually like the appreciation you receive from others. You can be a good teacher/mentor to others and a great friend. You can be too logical sometimes and be disconnected from your emotional self, so you’ll need to face your emotions even when you feel challenged to do so. Since, your words and communication skills greatly rely both on your intellectual skills and emotions. This makes you a heartfelt communicator. You can be an incredible public and motivational speaker. You have a lot of wisdom and experience that you can convey through your works and services. 

You will also need to be careful not to let negative mindsets ruin your enthusiasm. You’ll need to consciously reprogram your thought-patterns and think positively about yourself, others and life in general since pessimistic thinking can easily turn to self-sabotaging behaviors and potentially even hurtful confrontations with others. You have an inner child that needs attention and fun, since you might have grown up too early due to certain circumstances in your childhood. 

You can use your literary, artistic or marketing skills to best use in your career endeavors since you know how to make a good first impression. You are an avid learner, especially of people and their behavior, this can also help you advance in your career through good people-skills. If you have wounds related to family/siblings, you can heal that through forming healthy friendships. Your words, actions and works can be quite influential, so knowing how impactful you are will help you to consciously channel your healing journey into your works/services and inspire others in their recovery.
