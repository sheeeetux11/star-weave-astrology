Part of Fortune is one of the Ancient Astrological methods that has endured through the ages, invoking perhaps even more fascination in the Modern Astrological Era. Part of Fortune, or even known as Fortuna, Pars Fortuna & Lot of Fortune: is an Astrological Point in the Natal (& of course other types of) Chart that represents our Innate ability to attain Worldly Success, Abundance & Prosperity. Indicating one’s Natural Talents, Good Luck & Gifts, this Calculated Point has the symbol that looks like an ‘x’ inside a circle 「 ⨂ 」Part of Fortune (PoF) is one of the many Arabic Parts & Hermetic Lots, dating back to its use since 1BC (perhaps much older). 

Although most famously associated with Arabic Parts (align with 500+ such similar Parts); Lot of Fortune, Lot of Spirit, Lot of Eros, Lot of Victory, … are also some of the Lots of Hermetic (7 in Total, we can dive deeper into them one by one soon enough ;). In general, these are points in our Chart, calculated with Ascendant, Sun & Moon (along with other planets like Venus, Jupiter, etc.). PoF in particular is calculated with one’s Ascendant, Sun & Moon degrees. The exact Time of Birth becomes crucial, since degrees of these placements along with day or night time of birth becomes important for accuracy. Astroseek has a quick and easy way to calculate Lot of Fortune using the correct Formulas (along with the calculator for other Lots). (Although not an ad, I absolutely luv this website for its versatility & accuracy ❤︎)Here is a bit of Math & Geometry if you would like to learn how to calculate the traditional way.

Traditionally there are two Formulas to calculate Part of Fortune, one for the Day Chart and other for the Night Chart. If your Sun Placement lies on the top of the Natal Chart (i.e., from 7th House to 12th House), then you have a Day Chart.Part of Fortune = Ascendant + Moon - Sun If your Sun Placement is in the bottom of your Natal Chart (i.e., from 1st House to 6th House), then you have a Night Chart.Part of Fortune = Ascendant + Sun - MoonNow the degree of each Placement will need to be converted in order to equate them to 360° of a circle (Chart). Simplest way to do that is to divide every house and equate them to the degrees of a circle, 360° / 12 = 30° each.  

Now, let’s learn to calculate PoF from an example,Pisces ♓️ Ascendant 16° 04’ 
Converting : Pisces ♓️ starts at 330°, so Ascendant is at 330° + 16° 04’ = 346° 04’
Sagittarius ♐️ Sun 14° 57’ Converting : Sagittarius ♐️ starts at 240°, so Sun is at 240° + 14° 57’ = 254° 57’Leo ♌️ Moon 4° 02’Converting : Leo ♌️ starts at 120°, so Moon is at 120° + 4° 02’ = 124° 02’
Let’s assume that this is a Day Chart,Then,Part of Fortune = Ascendant + Moon - SunPoF = 346° 04’ + 254° 57’ - 124° 02’ = 215° 09’ = 5° 09’ + 210° where, 210° is at the start of Scorpio, so Part of Fortune here is in Scorpio 5° 09’ (which lies in the 8th House of the respective Chart)

Interpreting Part of Fortune is much like any other placement in the chart:
Sign + House will show you the Key to your Abundance and Success in life. 
Conjunction to PoF is often more significant, likewise Opposition also becomes an important Major Aspect.
Sextile, Trine & Square to PoF take the next precedence.
Degrees reveal extra details about the Archetypical energy that most suits your PoF.

The Sign of PoF shows how best to access your Fortune & Innate Potential. Embodying the energy & archetype of your PoF’s Sign will help you to naturally harness your Inherent Gifts. The House of PoF shows which Themes & Areas of Life present the most Opportune Chances and bring Good Luck to you. Following PoF is much like taking a HighWay to your Ambitious Dreams and making them a Reality. However here, you will have to tap into a hidden Wealth of Knowledge, Wisdom & Virtue in order to make the best use of your own Inner Potential & Talents. 

Since this Point in your Chart is derived from your Sun, Moon & Ascendant, your PoF also becomes a very simple yet Powerful way to harness the Energy of your ‘Big Three’ in a ‘concentrated’ way, i.e., through the combination of One Sign + House. This indeed can cut through the tension created by any harsh aspect between your Sun, Moon & Ascendant, as even Sun Square/Opposite Moon, or Moon Square/Opposite Ascendant and so on can often create Inner Frustrations as to how to balance these very important aspects in your chart individually. By focusing on your PoF, you can easily diffuse the complexity of such harsh aspects between the three most significant placements in your Chart. Hence, PoF becomes a Karmic Pass to both your Abundance and your Inner Stability. 

You can almost simultaneously balance your Emotional Needs (Moon) & Inner Ambitions (Sun), effortlessly turning them into a part of your Life’s Focus (Rising). Part of Fortune is also where you can find Contentment & Joy, where Happiness naturally manifests. So, the Sign + House of PoF can represent the themes of Life where you can find Pleasure, Comfort & Serenity (similar to Lot of Spirit). This is also where you can naturally share wonderful things with those you love & cherish, so Gifts, Celebrations, Donations & Charity relating to your PoF placement can bring both Spiritual & Emotional Gratification.
