A generational transit. Recent: 1996-98.

Your individuality could have gotten you into a lot of trouble in your early years, creating a lot of conflict within yourself. You could have suppressed your true self to fit in, or rebel against everyone who expected you to behave a certain way, either way, you might have been in constant agony. This could come out as emotional outbursts every time you are pushed to the edge. Your independence was compromised growing up making it even harder to develop your self-esteem and hindered your character development. So, you might have grown up being extremely conscious of this. 

You had all this energy swirling inside you wanting a creative outlet, which made it harder to suppress. Your earliest pain might have been of rejection from social groups or communities that left a deep wound. You might have felt alienated making you feel misunderstood. The constant internal friction may be asking you to accept your anger & pain, to welcome change into your inner world, empowering you. This aspect can be painful yet, has the potential to bring you relief from the pain of rejection. You’ll be able to live as your true self all while accepting that you’re out of the norm, since your life has been anything but normal, for good or for worse. So, you have the gift to see beyond the veil of ordinary and envision a better future. 

You can overcome your wounds by becoming a part of groups and communities, by believing that there’re like-minded people who will appreciate you for exactly who you are instead of expecting you to be something you’re not. Your journey of healing will be inspiring to others even those who might have hurt you initially. Finding healthy and productive ways to expend your excess creative energies can bring you a sense of relief through productivity. Your ingenuity and accomplishments will bring you recognition.
