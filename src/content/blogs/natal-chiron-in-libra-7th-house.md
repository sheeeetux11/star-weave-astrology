Natal Chiron in Libra (7th House)

This Chiron placement can prove to be heart-breaking, sometimes literally. You might have grown up witnessing love and relationships to its ugliest forms or had no positive reference of love and compassion. You might have felt guilty for not caring or taken advantage of for caring. There could have been instances where you felt that you can’t have and experience love. You might have even felt betrayed by life for how harsh the reality can be, since you only seemed to attract chaos instead of peace. You hate confrontations and especially conflicts with your loved ones can make you feel guilty or ashamed. You have a fierce sense of loyalty yet you rarely see that in others. You might have experienced jealousy, envy and hatred from others, you might have also felt so towards others at some point. 

You have a hard time balancing your priorities in life, this is especially true with the time you give to yourself and others. You might have issues with setting healthy boundaries in relationships, so you could have experienced or encountered toxic partners, this can also be true with regards to friends and acquaintances as well. You take disappointments very seriously, even if you already saw them coming. You feel burdened to keep a healthy dynamic in relationships, since you might be pulling all the weight and the other person(s) might not be putting in their fair share of the effort. 

You might have even experienced traumatic break-ups, betrayals, or even losing a loved one unfortunately that could have changed your entire view on life. You also have a hard time recovering from such sudden events. You might have experienced betrayals in business or financial partnerships or felt used in some way for your expertise. Others often see you as a compassionate and charming individual. They may see you as kind and generous with your time, money and effort. 

You have a naturally calming presence and others can feel relaxed around you. You love to make new friends and acquaintance, so you have a big social circle. You are also good at networking, so you have resourceful contacts. You might not like to spend much time in public places or in the spotlight. You might even feel shy or anxious about social events. But you are also a great host, you love to spend time with your loved ones and make memories. You might feel drawn towards activism and social causes. You might advocate for certain issues that you feel empathically connected to. You might even have had bad experiences with the law or the justice system. 

You might feel compelled to study law or other activism related subjects. You have exceptional people and communication skills, so you know how to attract a crowd and put your thoughts across effectively. You are humble and forgiving, but you will need to learn how to set healthy boundaries and learn when to let go of certain situations or relationships when they turn toxic. You might even have to dissect your own toxic traits and behaviors that cause heart breaks in your relationships. You will have to understand the difference between compromise and self-sacrifice. 

Your ability to defuse tense situations with wit and diplomacy will be highly regarded. You can gain excellent business/financial skills, also run successful enterprises and partnerships with your expertise. Your healing might be quite public, where others can witness your heartbreaks and also see you recover, making it an inspiring journey. You have a special charm to inspire others through your experiences and art, so your works/services can touch people’s hearts and encourage them on their healing journey as well.
