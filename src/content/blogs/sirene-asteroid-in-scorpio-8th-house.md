Sirene Asteroid in Scorpio + 8H + Pluto & Mars Aspects + 8º 20º 🐦‍🔥✨

LIGHT ATTRIBUTES OF SIRENE
This is one of the most powerful placements for Asteroid Sirene, but also the most dangerous, so caution is paramount. You are extremely sensual, alluring and magnetic. Your personality can captivate anybody that you wish to charm. There are likely many Secret Admirers in (all areas of) your life. This is especially true in your Romantic life. You are likely desired by many, but being Unapproachable & maintaining a Sense of Mystery can work in your favor. You also likely have an excellent Business Acumen that can make you quite successful. You are shrewd & ambitious in Money Matters and can often attract the right kind of Financial Partners. 

You can be quite intuitive and even inclined towards spirituality. Your passions & ambitions can be both intimidating & enchanting to many. You are extremely Emotionally Intelligent and good with words, quite enticing in many ways. You have a Powerful Presence that instantly captures those around you. You likely have many (many many) Secret Admirers, some of whom may even fantasize about you. Many may fall in love with you but some can become quite fearful of you right after, even if they don’t have a reason to. You can attract trustworthy people (who can also become quite obsessed & over-protective of you) overtime with the Highest of Intentions and well-suited to your Ideals. 

You can also attract a Romantic Partner(s) who is equally Powerful and Resourceful, so you can likely Marry into Money, Inherit money through your own family (or both) and/or create Monetary Success together with your Spouse. You manifest & work towards Excellency, Great Success and Abundance in life. You have the Power of Pure Intentions to achieve your Ambitions and wield Great Authority if you so desire to (again, emphasizing on the “good” part of intentions) where even the universe will equip and cater to you with whatever is necessary. You have Karmic Blessings & Protection that will help you overcome numerous challenges and transform Pain into Power.

DARK ATTRIBUTES OF SIRENE
You often encounter Malicious and dare I say, quite Formidable people. They may be obsessed with dismantling your life. You are likely to be surrounded by frenemies as well. There can be those who scheme, plot & derail you into an entrapment, not just in terms of Finances but also Sexually and Social Status-wise. It is hence important not to commit yourself to any kind of partnership/relationship without first discerning others’ True Motives. Temptations for infidelity and cheating are likely when dealing with inherently manipulative & devious people. But most importantly, (in any kind of relationship) you are advised not to waste your loyalty on those who don’t deserve it, not to hold onto such relationships and to let go of them as safely & quickly as possible. Since, in relationships (of all sorts) there can be those who wish to create power-dynamics where they want to dominate you, suppress you or even disempower you either to gain control over you or just to feel superior. 

On the extreme side, you might have to deal with jealousy, envy & unreasonable hatred. On the extreme - extreme side: Severe Emotional, Psychological & even Physical Manipulation. So, you might learn some gruesome truths about the dark human emotions, intentions & wickedness. This can also be true when it comes to Spiritual Attacks (even involving Dark Arts) which can not just be directed at you but also towards your family & loved ones. Vicious rumors & falsehoods can plague you especially during your younger years (and towards your early adulthood). You might even have been victimized in someway. There is an almost ‘otherworldly essence’ to you that these kind of people will want to capture and misuse if not to destroy. But you are also bestowed with Incredible Resilience and a strong Willpower that will help you overcome any challenge no matter how arduous. Standing in your Power, owning/reclaiming it and whenever necessary “showing people who they are up against” will be essential with this Sirene placement.
