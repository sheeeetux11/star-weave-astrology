0º is one of the most Powerful Degrees for any placement in a chart, and certainly with Sirene we are bound to see the Archetype of Sirene develop in incredible ways. You might feel the urge to explore your own Shadow Side, whether through innocent curiosity or the influence of someone/something outside of you. This Degree can greatly enhance the influence of Sirene in the respective Sign & House. So, you are likely to be a little apprehensive at first to recognize this placement (on a Personal level) but also excited to explore its potential. You can hence utilize Sirene’s placement through understanding your own Innate Talents & Natural Charm. 

Since this degree is also considered as a significant one, you are likely to see Sirene’s effects in your Chart more prominently. As 0º is the very first degree in Astrology, it can often indicate how we perceive things in life for the first time. So, when Sirene is at 0º, pay attention to the House & Sign, as you will very likely be surprised as to how profoundly Sirene here affects your chart. Hence, you will have to be conscious of your first reactions & impressions to everything related to that House & Sign placement in your chart. 

You are likely to encounter situations, people & places that may not be exactly who/what they claim to be. Your first impressions can hence be misleading, either through your own Innocence or through External manipulation. So, it is highly likely that having extremely High or Unrealistic expectations here can be risky and dangerous. Yet on the other hand, you are likely more driven to explore your Innate Talents and to honor your Inner desires related to this House/Sign. Sirene here at 0º can hence be the significant placement which can bring many life changing experiences into your life, potentially changing it completely.
