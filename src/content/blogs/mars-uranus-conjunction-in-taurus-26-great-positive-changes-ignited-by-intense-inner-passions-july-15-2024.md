Mars and Uranus join forces in Taurus at 26° on July 15, 2024 making it their last Conjunction in Taurus together. Mars will start a two-year cycle with Uranus, marking the beginnings & completions of major projects, mainly tangible & practical in nature. The Conjunction also happens with Algol, the infamous Fixed Star of Disaster & Misfortune. This Conjunction will hence create a sense of panic and sets off negative events which later lead to a much brighter & prosperous future.In your personal life, you can experience stress & anxiety to the extreme levels just a few days leading up to July 15th & right after. 

Mars & Uranus’s energies are too volatile for each other, fueling the passions & ambitions within you, but simultaneously creating overwhelm & tension. This can lead to physical & mental exhaustion, severe anxiety & panic attacks to say the least. You might wake up to a sudden change that shakes up your stability in life, either in finances or health. Your life can feel a little out of your control, affecting your sense of security. Calming your nerves will feel harder and harder the more you think about it. More over, stressful overthinking can lead you to sleepless nights & disrupt your daily routine.

On the other hand, there will be moments where you will feel completely in control of your mind & body, your life & ambitions alike. Sudden realizations & epiphanies can bring you wonderful insights with regards to your plans for the future. Your productivity can increase significantly. You might feel this intense inspiration to take on certain idealistic dreams & goals to make them a reality, one step at a time (the True Taurian way). You can surprise yourself with what you can do with this innately exhilarating energy. So, this all means that you don’t have the tolerance for any kind of outdated restrictions that may be holding you back. There is an almost primal need to release the past and pave a path to your future. 

Impulsive decisions can lead to disappointments, but intuitive decisions can lead to immense abundance. So please, do exercise caution and discern your moves carefully for at least the next few months. Since this happens to be the last Uranus x Mars Conjunction in Taurus before Uranus moves into Gemini in 2025, we will feel tempted to get to the finish line at top speed. Any Projects, Major Ambitions, etc. that we are currently completing or yet to begin New Ones will be on our “To-Do-ASAP-List” for the whole year, or at least to lay solid foundations now in order to revisit them in the near future. Pacing ourselves will make things much easier and help us direct all this wonderful momentum in the right direction.

Mars x Uranus Conjunction with Algol can indicate that a great source of karmic restriction in our personal life will be dispersed through sudden circumstances. This can be a toxic relationship, a troublesome contract, negative mindset, or even our own bad habits/patterns. Any restriction or overwhelming control on our life & ambitions will be released. Personal freedom, independence & sovereignty will be restored on a soul-level. There can be massive shake ups in life, especially in finances. It will all feel extremely chaotic and stressful. 

Spiritual abilities & intuition may be heightened during this time, along with a few health/routine changes. Globally, this can be a stressful time. But, if we stay a little patient & grounded, we can weather this storm of uncertainty, the looming darkness & overwhelm a bit more easily.Pluto Retrograde can add to the immense anxiety; things/people/cycles from the past can come back around or bring back unpleasant memories. Stay strong, and take time-outs whenever necessary. The past will only revisit us now to be released out of our lives, and to test if we have learnt our lessons or not. 

It will be important not to get discouraged and also not to get too caught up with the uncertainties at hand right now, as they will all resolve favorably pretty soon. Memories or even situations that relate to past injustices can drudge up feelings of repressed anger, angst & sorrow. But, this Conjunction along with a Trine to Pluto in Rx (also Sextile Neptune in Rx) can urge us to face those feelings of anger & betrayal, and finally let it all go. To let Karma handle it all so that we can fully embrace our new life. 

This can hence be a powerful time of reinvention where your past hurt doesn’t define who you are now and ever again. You can take this opportunity to connect to your true inner self, untouched by trauma and anguish of the past. Taking back your Power from everything that has been holding you back and walking fearlessly on your destined path. Karmic ties & cycles broken, curses & trauma released.Algol although creates disastrous situations, can also lead us to deep transformation. Any sense of overwhelm, stress or fear that we are faced with now will only take us through a process of awakening. Soon, the very stress & fear that overpowered us will be warded off by our own transformation. 

This is not an easy combination of energies to deal with, nonetheless, it is an extremely powerful time of change. Personal growth opportunities will push us into a whole new way of thinking & being. The shift in perspectives will help us find remarkable solutions to problems & situations that may have seemed puzzling & impossible before. Moving beyond the past and embracing the future will open up doors of abundance. ⚡️🗝️
