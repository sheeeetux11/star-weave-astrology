Sirene Asteroid in Libra + 7th House + Venus Aspects + 7° 19° 🦢✨ 

LIGHT ATTRIBUTES OF SIRENE 
You have such a sweet personality that others may initially think that ‘you are too good to be true’. You might have developed excellent People & Communication skills since your childhood. You are someone who craves fulfilling friendships, and love that is eternal. You are also likely quite talented in arts, poetry, music, etc. You can socialize and form wonderful connections overtime. You can be inspiring to many with your simple yet a Soft Charm; your voice, body-language & demeanor can be so captivating. 

This is a Sirene Placement that can be very subtle in its effects yet quite effective. You are a skilled negotiator, talented at bringing people together (or onto your side, depending on the situation). You are also good at avoiding conflicts, you know how to find your way out of any confrontation or bind. This can be quite helpful in legal and other risky situations. So, all your charm, intelligence and resourcefulness can attract a lot of people who would wish to be on your good side/team. This also means that you can attract exactly the right kind of people into your life, be it for business/career, friendship or love. 

Your innate Gentleness and sharp Intellect can be a valuable combination in both your Personal & Professional life. So, you are likely to choose careers that are stress free, comfortable & exciting all while providing you the space to express yourself and make the best use of your skills & talents. You also naturally make a great First Impression and can keep it up with your good Social Skills, so you are generally well-liked, even admired bringing you effortlessly Good Social Status and Popularity.

DARK ATTRIBUTES OF SIRENE
You often encounter people who are very good at making you question yourself. They are highly manipulative; intellectually & mentally. They can act like your ally but backstab you without seeming suspicious the whole time. Frenemies are a huge problem with this placement. Caution in Marriage/Legally binding contracts. In romance, you can encounter people who may prey on your vulnerabilities, who at first seem like ‘everything that you are looking for in a partner/spouse’, but can turn into something completely different once the mask falls. So, these are the kind of people who are skilled at mirroring you, and presenting themselves as something they are not. It is likely that there can be instances of the ‘wandering eye’ and the temptation to cheat, by either your partner or you, even on Intellectual & Emotional levels if not physically. 

Another thing about Sirene’s negative effect here can be your own lack of assertiveness. Constantly avoiding conflicts/confrontations can lead to not dealing with important issues that can become bigger & more difficult to solve as time goes by, building up resentment along with it. This can also mean that you don’t take assertive steps towards securing your own future or sustaining balance & peace in your life, which can be a Red Flag to others, or a loop hole for manipulative people to take advantage of you in someway. 

You will also need to be careful not to become too dependent in relationships, or entertain codependency from others, since that can become burdensome (on either or both sides) leading to resentment and/or manipulation. Standing strong in your Personal Identity, Character & Goals in life will be extremely important to not become shallow, attention-seeking or lowering your standards in life. It is hence important not to rely solely on other’s opinions or to try your hardest to be liked by others at your detriment. Fitting into other’s expectations can lead you astray from your true fulfillment in life.
