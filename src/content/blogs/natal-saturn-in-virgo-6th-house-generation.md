Natal Saturn in Virgo (6th House) Generation 🐹✨

Saturn in the house/sign of details, day-to-day affairs and health, feels at home with it being in the Earth Element. You are diligent and hard working; you can work tirelessly towards your goals and ambitions. You have a brilliant mind that can solve complex problems easily. You rarely look over even the slightest things, so you are excellent at detail-oriented work. You have a natural tendency to push harder than your limits to accomplish a task. You are extremely responsible and rarely complain about any amount of work or effort that is needed from you. You are also appreciative of others for their service.

You might not always feel welcomed in your workplace or career field. You might feel as though you are being outcasted, bullied or ridiculed either by the people or the system in some way regardless of what career you are a part of. You might have to endure working in toxic work environments for the early part of your career life which might have a profound influence on your world view. You might not always enjoy what you are doing. Even when you are in your dream job/career, you might feel it as your prime duty than something that you do for personal satisfaction. As a result, you might have to consciously put in the effort to experience the joy of your work, since you are truly doing your best, eventually with time and maturity you will be able to feel content in your chosen profession without feeling any sense of unease or guilt.

You might also not feel appreciated for your hard work in the early years, but your persistence will eventually attract the right people and opportunities that will help you reach higher and making you feel recognized. You might undervalue your efforts quite often due to your self-critical mindset and the experiences you have had before. As a result, you might settle for jobs that have toxic work cultures or get underpaid, etc. Saturn here will protect you karmically from such poor experiences, but you will also have to recognize your own true potential and never settle for less again (if ever), this might be one of the most crucial lessons you might have to learn. You might be able to attain mastery in the field of your work. You might even be sought after by many for your guidance and expertise. You are also a giver at heart, since you are always willing to be of help to others, be it to your loved ones, coworkers/acquaintances, community or even the country. So, you might find donating, social service, activism, environmentalism, etc. to be spiritually grounding.

You might have had a harsh upbringing, either due to family issues or due to the environment you were raised in. Your early experiences may have made you realize that life is all about hard work and only those who work deserve to be content. You might have also struggled to find your inner self as you reached adulthood since in one way or another, you might have spent your childhood being a mature adult already. Introspection is your greatest tool, yet you might find it hard to turn off the self-criticism setting in your mind. You are always aware of every single thing you do, especially your mistakes, no matter how small or insignificant. In a way, this honesty is what makes you a perfectionist and a quick learner. Although, you will have to realize that self-criticism is supposed to uplift you and not dwindle your hopes. Since, even though you are a mentally strong person, under excessive negative self-talks you can break too, and nobody should ever be under such pressure, externally or internally. So, keeping your mind positive, practical and realistic will be a challenge but comparatively it's only a small issue for your intellectual mind to solve. 

Likewise, you can be overly critical about others, especially those who don’t take their work or life seriously. You dislike wasted effort, and you certainly don’t tolerate irresponsible behaviors. You hold everyone accountable for their actions. You are organized and methodical; you are also a good conversationalist. So, managerial positions, Human Resources, collaborative works, marketing, etc. along with medicine, engineering, science, service-based jobs like nursing, environmentalism, armed services, etc. can be some of your career interests. 

You are a perfectionist none the less, you demand the best of best in every aspect of your life and never lower your standards for anyone. You will however have to be careful not to be too demanding in your workplace, from your coworkers, subordinates to your boss even, since not everyone may be able to keep up with your efficiency or quality of work. So being pushy or overly critical of others can create strife your professional life. Likewise, expect jealousy, envy or even drama in the workplace as a result of others’ projecting their insecurities onto you. You might have had the opportunity to work from a young age or struggled to find a career that suits your interests, either way, even the rejections & delays may have been blessings in disguise with this placement, since Saturn here can eventually lead you to your destined career/life path.

You might have struggle with time management, multi-tasking, everyday routines, etc. during your childhood. You might have had to put in extra effort to learn essential skills that made you feel like you have the control over your life. This is extremely vital to you, since if you feel that even one of the things in your life is out of order or messy in some way, you might feel distraught until you can fix it. So, in your adulthood, you might be well-organized in everything you do. This is all thanks to your solid self-discipline. You might have endured physical ailments, accidents, illnesses or even misdiagnosis, etc. during your childhood. Such experiences may have scared you initially and eventually made you health conscious. 

You might have chronic illnesses, or you might be extremely susceptible to seasonally allergies, etc. that need to be regulated carefully. It might need a lifelong effort to maintain a well-balanced lifestyle, with regular exercises, conscious & nutritious diet, self-care, meditation, etc. You are also advised to take periodic rest days to indulge in doing what relaxes you. You might like to keep your environment tidy and clean since you despise mess. So, you might even enjoy cleaning activities in your free time or be extremely particular about how your home, office, etc. are maintained. This placement can also caution you about OCD, hypochondria, or similar issues hence you might have to be careful about such tendencies. 

You might have to be extra careful about your mental health, especially since you unknowingly take on a lot of responsibilities. Your positive mind frame is key, otherwise, negative thought-process alone can create problems when there are none. Any sort of excess stress can affect your mental health, and with this placement, your mental health can affect your physical health as well. So, staying in a productive yet positive state of mind will always benefit your mental & physical well-being. 6th House/Virgo can also represent hidden enemies in the chart; hence, you might have to deal with challenging situations like court cases, rumors, unnecessary conflicts, etc. 

It is also quite crucial to your over-all wellbeing to carefully choose your inner and outer circle of trusted people, since you might encounter those who may have hidden agendas, willing to take advantage of your kindness, or those who are wishing you harm even if its as subliminal as evil eye/intention. So, protecting your work, health and efforts will help you stay away from unnecessary drama and chaos. Saturn here will guide you through your routines by expecting you to maintain schedules & budgets, taking control of your health & wellness by conscious efforts, your work & reputation through diligence, spiritual balance through selfless services/donations & community work, and above all, by encouraging peace & tranquility in your day-to-day life.
