Natal Saturn in Scorpio (8th House) Generation ☄️✨

Saturn in the house/sign of death, rebirth and transformation can be an interesting yet powerful placement. You are extremely loyal and reliable. Your love is deep and enduring, yet you rarely feel loved the same way you do from others. You might have had a hard time accepting yourself for all your flaws, which in-turn could have affected your view on intimacy & love. If anything, you might either forbid yourself from intimacy or have no limits for your lust, all depending on the aspects Saturn is making in your chart and what other placements there are. You view relationships as painful experiences especially if you have had to endure traumatic situations in the past. You might not trust others too easily, since most people in your life betrayed and backstabbed you in one way or another. You have a firm disdain for people who are disloyal, greedy or generally envious since you might have endured harsh experiences due to such people. 

Your life experiences may have given you a keen sense of self and intuition. You might even have latent psychic & other spiritual gifts. Death may either be a fascination or a terrible fear as well. You might have escaped from near death accidents or endured serious ones; you might have even witnessed your loved ones suffering through harsh health issues. So, you know how cruel, yet inevitable death & suffering are. You are realistic and practical in your approach to life as a result. You rarely let yourself enjoy the good times since in the back of your mind, you are always expecting for something to go wrong. This cautiousness encourages you to always have a back-up plan and think several steps ahead of everyone else, since you feel a sense of instability that scares you deeply. However, it is also important not to get enveloped by your fears so much so that you are always expecting an impending doom to occur in your life, since this placement can certainly trigger your deepest insecurities so there is a high possibility for paranoia. 

Staying realistically cautious and taking everything that happens with a grain of salt will save you a lot of mental & emotional stress. This placement can help you make your deepest desires become a reality through methodical & practical approach, giving you great manifesting abilities. So, you can use your rich inner world and a positive subconscious to your advantage making use of your true potential. You might have had a rough upbringing, maybe even raised in dangerous surroundings. Even if you have not experienced poverty, you might be afraid of ever being in a situation where you or your loved ones have to suffer. You might have been falsely accused of crimes, involved in dangerous situations, fraud, or even set up in some way, etc. You might have felt lonely and abandoned during your childhood, even if it wasn’t anything physical, you always felt it on an emotional level none the less, making you feel distant and unloved in some way. 

You might have endured some sort of abuse, neglect, or accident that left you traumatized, wounding your inner child gravely. You might have vowed to yourself that you will never again be in such situations, yet you are constantly afraid of history repeating itself. You are excellent at reading people’s intentions, so you are a great lie-detector. You despise dishonest people especially those who are fake & lack integrity. You might have encountered emotionally/psychologically manipulative people in your life who may have played on your love & loyalty for them. Such betrayals may have wounded you deeply making you extremely cautious of others. However, deceptive relationships like that helped you gain emotional intelligence as a form of defense mechanism which is your greatest asset. You can almost read others’ minds if you wanted to. You can pick up on details that others miss, and you know how to easily connect those patterns. You are extremely skilled at detail-oriented works and your curiosity is highly focused. 

You will however have to be careful not to become the monster you are fighting, since it is very easy to get consumed by others’ sins & darkness especially if you are prone to seeking justice, revenge or even losing control over your anger. You are extremely defensive whenever others falsely accuse you or are trying to ruin your reputation in any way. But you must also not get caught up in their traps by your aggression and reacting without reading their intentions. Your emotional and psychological intelligence will be your greatest weapon, so it is wise to exercise patience and plan your way out, since Saturn here can help you find logical solutions to even the most dangerous problems. 

This placement will often force you to undergo painful transformations both within and with-out, where you might experience external loses or have to let go of toxic people who don’t serve your highest good, while internally you will have to experience ego deaths; releasing your need to control, curb your rage, face your deepest fears, come out feeling renewed and more connected to your soul.Money is an important factor in your life. You might have been poor or born rich, either way, you have a complicated relationship with money. You might have to break generational curses and karmic cycles relating to money and relationships with this placement, which will be challenging yet immensely rewarding. 

You might work hard tirelessly and neglect the rest of your life since you can be prone to being a workaholic or you might let your fears & insecurities to hold you back from making any kind of career moves. You have an inner passion to accomplish great goals, but you will have to work on your self-esteem in order not to let your fears stop you or your ego to sabotage your efforts. You will have to overcome some deep-rooted feelings of unworthiness before you can fulfill your career goals. You are extremely passionate, and have an unstoppable determination, so it’s important that you don’t let your fears of inadequacy to deter you from your ambitions. Scorpio/8H also rules taxes, shared resources and banks, so you might have a successful career related these themes. 

You will have to however be careful not to fall into any forms of temptation and indulge in fraudulent practices, in taxes, or not paying due bills/loans, embezzlement, etc. since Saturn won’t be pleased with any injustice and karma will be repaid in a big way, so if its vice versa, where others have done such things to you or have devious intentions towards you, you can rest assured that you are karmically protected. You might notice that you have a good handle on money, or you might have been encouraged by certain events in your life to become good with financial management. You can find opportunities to accumulate wealth over time with patience & persistence especially through partnerships, marriage, divorce, or inheritance, etc. although, you might have to face delays, troubles or obstacles along the way (especially if there are challenging aspects or other placements indicating the same). 

Wealth, power and position may be your ambitions, always determined to find opportunities to attain them. You might be extremely attractive to others. You are a dutiful lover. You love with all your heart. You are also a passionate partner in intimate settings. You are loyal and you expect the same kind of devotion from your partner and nothing less. It is hard for you to open up to others, even those you love, but you will feel at ease when you can form enduring bonds with them even when you feel vulnerable to let others into your world. 

You are mysterious and might even guard your heart with all your might, but this is also what makes you attractive to others. Discernment is important with this placement, since you are aware of those who might want to get close to you just to hurt you, but you will also have to be careful not to accidentally hurt those who truly love you by misunderstanding their intentions. You can expect to find trustworthy connections (romantic, business, platonic) through the heap of toxic ones that you had to let go of. Saturn here will protect you from other’s ego and that of your own, helping you transform, and attain your deepest desires.
