Sun Conjunct Midheaven MC

☌ You might consider your life purpose as your personal identity, so you pursue your dreams relentlessly.
☌ This can be a motivating factor to you, although you also take failures & setbacks seriously. Every win and lose affects your self-confidence profoundly.
☌ You have a bigger than life personality and your goals embody your passions well. So, you always give your best and then some more.
☌ This also makes you a fierce competitor. Others in your field respect you for your courageous approach to career/business. You somehow always have the upper hand over your enemies/opponents with this aspect.
☌ Your ambitious personality attracts many admirers as well. You might even be seen as a role model by many who are inspired by your accomplishments & strength in character. So, you can be quite popular.
☌ You face obstacles and challenges head-on. You rarely back down in the middle of the task, so you always finish what you start.☌ You are responsible, and you take your job seriously. This also makes you a brave leader, since you know when to take risks and when to approach methodically.
☌ You are extremely proud of your achievements, and you can even be boastful sometimes. You will always find recognition for all your efforts. However, you must stay humble throughout your journey and maintain a healthy ego, since your mood can rise & faint with successes & failures, so you might seem arrogant or docile to others accordingly, neither of which are good for your public image.
☌ You might even climb to the top positions easily or be an entrepreneur with excellent leadership skills. Although you might not like to take advise from authority figures sometimes, causing tensions in those dynamics. You are also quite studious with this aspect; you will refine your skills & talents to stay up to date with the trends/changing times.
☌ You’ll likely pursue your ambitions with a brave heart; create incredible works/services and inspire others through your accomplishments; earn many acclaims & rewards; be a fierce leader/pioneer in your chosen field; gain wealth, prosperity, honor & respect from your career/business/public endeavors and express your authentic self through your many achievements.
