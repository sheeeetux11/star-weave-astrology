Natal Chiron in Pisces (12th House)

You are sensitive and compassionate, sometimes to your own detriment. You might have experienced many betrayals and disappointments. Sometimes, you might not even know what is troubling you or why you are feeling certain intense emotions. You have many healing and psychic abilities with this placement, you might even have extraordinary artistic and creative talents. You might feel a strong desire to help those in need, yet you often feel ignored or neglected when you need assistance. You might experience frequent anxiety attacks and you don’t like to stay in tense situations/environments. 

You can pick up on energetic shifts easily hence you can also absorb others’ negativity and distress. So, you might not be able to differentiate your emotions from that of others sometimes. You might even be paranoid of others due to all the disappointing situations and events you have been through in your life. Your mental and physical health are extremely sensitive with this placement. You might have to consciously take part in routines, habits and activities, also surround yourself with trustworthy and positive people in order to stay healthy both physically and mentally. 

Self-love can be a key factor in your healing journey since you might have a hard time being kind to yourself and your needs. You have a unique gift of healing others just by your positivity and presence. You might even notice that people who need healing or comfort are easily drawn towards you. You can be a great help in health and medicine related fields, social services and charitable causes. You strongly advocate for peace, so you do your best to resolve conflicts and bring more positivity into all situations. 

Your creative talents can be put to productive use in your career/services, since you can convey your emotions and opinions intentionally through your work. So, your works and accomplishments can have an inspiring and healing impact on others. You might have a hard time maintaining relationships especially since your generous heart can overlook red-flags and give one too many chances to the wrong people at first. You will learn how to set boundaries and only cherish healthy relationships as you love yourself and understand that self-sacrificing in relationships is not always worth it. You will also learn how to let go of hurt and disappointments as a part of your healing. 

You are quite spiritual even if not religious. Your faith will be tested numerous times with this placement each time making you stronger than ever. You might even find healing in spirituality and such practices that bring you calm and serenity. You will learn how to discern what is good for your personal growth and what is just a hindrance through your life’s experiences no matter how painful. You know how to turn pain into wisdom and healing. 

Your positive mindset can help you lead a much more fulfilling life, so you will have to consciously change any negative thought patterns or self-talks. Your innocence and generosity will help you uplift others by standing in your good intentions. You can bring awareness to mental and physical health issues, social causes, environmental issues, etc. through your work and services. You might even feel divinely guided towards such pursuits. Your dedication and devotion to both your spiritual and healing journey can be reflected in your daily life as serenity, peace and contentment.
