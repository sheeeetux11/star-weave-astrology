Black Moon Lilith and Sirene’s combination is a Phenomenal aspect to see in a chart (especially in a close Conjunction). Your biggest insecurities can inspire you to become that much more powerful in order to overcome those vulnerabilities. You are likely seen as someone who knows a lot, who have been through a lot, but also as someone who has overcome a lot. So as much as this quality attracts others’ admiration, it can also intimidate them immensely. There is a calmness about you that most people can’t seem to ignore, the kind of calm that hides a layer of intensity underneath which can become explosive at any given point in time. You hence have a commanding presence, whether you wish to be the centre of attention or not, and for that matter whether the attention is good or bad. You have a hold on others that can be subtle yet obvious, so in personal, romantic & intimate relationships, this aspect can be more apparent. There is something so unconventional about your personality, physical appearance, life style & career choices, etc. that is quite alluring to many, so you are likely to be quite popular, even with many secret admirers. 

It is also very very Very likely that you encounter people who may have all of your desired characteristics, yet have an alternate personality that can be much more appealing to you. So, this can also be vice versa, where others may be extremely attracted to an alternate personality of yours or a side of you that you like to keep hidden or sacred. Also, with Black Moon Lilith & Sirene’s combined effect, you are likely to encounter people who are really dark in their persona, thoughts & lifestyle. This can be something most people would consider unconventional, or dark as in DARK. The risks and dangers of this combination are hence numerous. You might have to be careful not to let your curiosities to be used against you. In many ways, the experiences bought by this aspect along with your Inner Child wounds & past traumas can also lead you towards immense Personal Transformations in life, awakening you to your Inner Power, Strength and Potential. 

You likely either have a past of injustice & wounds of victimization, or you have witnessed immense injustices around you. Your experiences and journey hence can be such an influential element in the fight for justice & rights of those things that you have first-hand-knowledge about. Your opinions may not be so popular, nor even the causes that you are involved in, yet your positive influence on them can make a huge difference. With everything, of course this can be vice versa. Yet still, Black Moon Lilith & Sirene are the two Feminine Archetypes that have similar connotations, stigma & hence influence tied to them particularly in Astrology. So, this combination of aspect in your Natal Chart hence needs to be utilized with the utmost care and responsibility. Being conscious of their effects on your personality, relationships & life in general can help you avoid the combined Dark Attributes and immensely amplify the Light Attributes. There is so much Raw Power, Resilience & Determination that can be harnessed through this aspect; in order to manifest your Greatest Desires, achieve Incredible Success and attract Immense Abundance.
