Natal Chiron in Virgo (6th House)

You are an exceptional help to others; you look after your loved ones always making sure that they do well in their endeavors, but you tend to ignore your own priorities sometimes. You can be extremely self-critical; you might even have the tendency to compare yourself to others and their accomplishments. You might even overlook your skills and merits that way. You take great pride in your work, but you might not fully appreciate your efforts for accomplishing those goals. You might even have health issues, especially during your childhood where you fell ill often. You might have stomach & skin related issues or feel extreme anxiety. 

You can even feel sick easily when you are under extreme stress. You take your work seriously, but you might also have tendencies to take opportunities for granted from lack of empathy towards yourself. You might feel inadequate to attain your dreams sometimes just to end up sabotaging your efforts and opportunities. You might even feel unworthy of praise or appreciation for a job well done. You might have a hard time keeping your thoughts positive. You might even suffer from mental health issues over time. You might have depressive mood swings or anger issues as a result. You might even have trouble working in teams or in closely knit organizations since you feel inadequate or over-burdened by responsibilities. 

You are however an adaptive person who can easily adjust to changing circumstances and make split-second decisions. You are good with managerial and organizational works since you pay close attention to details. You can contribute more to team-work than you give yourself credit for, speaking of which, you need to make a habit of giving yourself enough credit for how incredibly amazing you are and how much progress you have made. You need to take care of your mental health by changing your self-talks and thought-patterns. Any number of ups and downs, failures and successes are fair and acceptable, you must learn how not to blame yourself for every outcome or circumstance especially those that are out of your control. 

You will need to be realistic but not cynical about your life’s direction. You can pursue your desires and ambitions with confidence knowing full-well that you are more than capable of accomplishing your goals since you are extremely methodical and practical. You can also improve your health by consciously choosing workouts that fit your needs or other enjoyable physical activities, nutrition, meditation & mindfulness, etc. if you feel overly invested in your health routines, you will have to learn how to rest and relax as well, since your single-minded focus can leave you exhausted even when you are focusing on healthy things/practices. 

Your patience will be tested in many areas of your life, especially when you are waiting for the outcome of your efforts, so you will need to learn when to let go of control and go with the flow instead of over-analyzing what has already been finished especially when you give it your all. You might also have to consider responsibilities more as healthy challenges than as burdens. You can change your perspective on life by actively participating in it without feeling pressured to be perfect or adequate, but instead as your true self. 

Your help, guidance, work and services can bring healing and positivity to others. You might even feel satisfaction in volunteer works or charitable endeavors. Taking part in team based or community related activities can help you to use your skills productively all-while reducing social anxiety. Your knowledge and expertise can take you higher than you ever thought, so you can embrace your hard-earned skills unapologetically.
