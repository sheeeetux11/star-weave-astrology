It can feel like, the early part of your life was one big “dark night of the soul” kind of experience where you might have endured through heart-breaking, disappointing and hopeless situations, events that seem to rub salt on your inner wounds. You might have grown up thinking you need to dedicate your efforts & time towards your family, household, culture or even country and somewhere along the line you might have put your needs to the side. Since you feel a lack of stability and security deep within, you might expect others to treat you badly or neglect your well-being.  Read More →
