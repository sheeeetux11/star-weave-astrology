You are Intense, Dramatic & Self-Empowered. You make a strong & striking First Impression, even when you feel unseen or ignored, you are not. Something about you speaks volumes, like a Quite Power that can take over everything if its left unnoticed. You are extremely passionate, a true force to be reckoned with. This aspect hence gives a level of Mystical Clarity about your Life Purpose, as Lilith’s Intuition aligns with your Sun’s Identity. This of course can make you so much more Powerful, Intense & Self-Confident, thereby also making you intimidating to others. 

Your Sensual & Sexual Intensity can also become a major part of your Personality, depending on how responsibly you use it, it can also become your Core Strength. As our Sun can indicate to a fulfilling Career Path, with Lilith’s Conjunction, you are likely to be deeply connected to your Passions & Ambitions. There may be a bit of unconventionality to the way you approach your Career Life as well, be it the way you work or the profession you choose. Your Public & Professional Image will hence be filled with mystery, taboo or suspicion but certainly surprise. 

Your Intense Focus & Resilience will bring Incredible Success and Achievements in your chosen path. Abandoning this crucial part of you can however lead to immense dissatisfaction in Career and Life as a whole, so this Aspect will require you to be True to your Wants, Needs & Desires along with being Confident & Self-Assured to pursue those Ambitions unapologetically. You likely will have numerous opportunities & experiences that help you to develop a Strong Will Power & a Healthy Ego, a kind of Personality that can be both Powerful & Inspiring. Your ego will need your attention however since if you indulge in too much focus on yourself, you might become deluded by Power or Self-conceited. Here, your Shadow self may not be your enemy who wishes to sabotage or ruin you, but a powerful ally yet with deep wounds & fears who will need healing & empowerment. So, not letting your Wounded-self take over your Life will be a spiritual challenge. As this aspect will require you to understand your Shadow Self, thereby your deepest darkest fears, insecurities, weaknesses, etc. 

You will also have to recognize your deep buried Desires, Intense feelings, Talents, etc. Similarly with others, where you will be able to see their Shadow Self, whether they are self-aware about it or not. So through the influence of this aspect, you get to interact with others & their Shadow, also others consciously or sub-consciously interact with you & your Shadow. Through these experiences, whether pleasant or unpleasant, along with the actions of others & the consequences of your own, you will learn how to refine your Ego, Character and True Self. This hence is an aspect that is best explained through this quote, “Every gold has to pass through fire before it can shine”. 

Lilith & Sun Conjunction gives an edge to your character, a presence that can invoke extreme emotions in others. This can be jealousy, envy, hate or anger. A strange inability to understand you for who you are, especially when you present your True Self. You likely have a history of being targeted, especially when you try to express yourself. Everybody want you to abandon your true self. They will work overtime to make sure that you willingly disconnect yourself from your true self. This can be through bullying, hazing, or to the extremes of harassment & victimization. Trauma that penetrates your Core Identity, where you feel like the only way to handle the initial shock is to abandon yourself. 

This is also where you learn how to transform yourself, to the point where you get to discover who you truly are and to get in touch with your Inner Power. The healing process may seem crude and even unconventional on the outside, as it is unbearably intense, the trauma & the wound is just that deep. But, the person you emerge out as on the other side of that process is downright ‘invincible’. Lilith is the point of projection, so you go through a phase in your life where you are the black sheep, you are the scapegoat. You are almost too perfect to these people who just don’t want to see everything that you are, that which they are not within themselves. On the other hand, when you stand strong on your convictions and insist on expressing your True Self, in the best of situations you are likely to inspire many who will respect & admire your authenticity.

Your Inner Feminine Dark energy is so apparent to others, it is just too intimidating, imagine the Dark side of the Moon (although that isn’t what Lilith represents, but it might as well). There is so much Raw Power, Intensity & Mystery… it’s Pure Magic. In many ways, this is your gift, so owning your Raw Feminine energy will ultimately transform you into an Alluring, Mystical, and Incredible being, almost Other-worldly. Often times, this is what those people, places or things, whoever or whatever they are, tried to suppress and outcast. So, the healing transformation process brings out all this Natural Power that is within you in order for you to amalgamate into your Core Identity, making you a Force of Nature itself. You will hence develop all the necessary skills & resources to stand up for yourself, and not let anyone treat you any less than you deserve.

Of course, this can play out in the other way around, where those with Lilith Sun Conjunction often use their darkest deepest insecurities and project them onto others, creating all the drama & trauma in their wake. With Lilith it’s always about Self-recognition & Empowerment, so this aspect can play out either way. But ultimately, this is one of the most Powerful Aspects for Lilith, and a Purifying aspect in a chart with respect to one’s own Inner Femininity and Self-identity. It makes you extra Self-Assured, Independent & Passionate. This aspect in your chart will always end up demanding your attention towards it. It gives one the most mesmerizing yet almost untouchable presence when in a chart.

You will work towards owning your True Self and embracing your Divine Feminine Energy. Finding yourself through the chaos around you, and creating chaos to fully express yourself. Your Art, Self-Expression & Ambition will indeed draw the eire of those in authority & society in general (especially when also with Saturn’s Opposition or Square). Established conventions & societal norms don’t have any hold over you. That of course doesn’t mean you can abandon all morals and indulge in destructive vices & behaviors either. But, stepping out of the strict conformities and exploring the Potential within the Dark Feminine & Unconventional energy can bring incredible things into this your life & into this world.Thereby, bringing wonderful new Art & Self-expression, Leadership qualities that inspire people to embrace their creativity, sexuality & passion without being tied down to senseless limitations. 

The way you embrace yourself and all that Raw Power & Passion will inevitably challenge limiting-beliefs of an overwhelmingly structured & unimaginative system, society or even civilization. Your Authentic Self & Presence can shake things up, especially when old & archaic foundations turn into walls that close in and restrict Personal Potential. This aspect hence will give you a rebellious streak, a feisty rebel with a cause. With such Power, you will also need to be responsible not to use it for self-gratification & destruction of good stability. 

Getting drunk on this power can only lead to Self-Destruction, as Lilith’s Intensity can delude Sun’s Core Identity when boundaries within get blurred or broken down. Use this aspect instead to Empower yourself & others, to challenge the status quo, fight for justice, and bring more Positivity & Fairness. This aspect will bestow many wonderful Talents & Gifts, giving you all the right tools & abilities to handle extremes highs & lows, challenges & victories that will help you transform into your Uniquely Adept, Strong Willed, Resilient, Intense & Mesmerizing Self.
