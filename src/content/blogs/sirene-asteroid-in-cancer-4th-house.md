Sirene Asteroid in Cancer + 4H + Moon Aspects + 4° 16° 28° 🦀✨

LIGHT ATTRIBUTES OF SIRENE
You are a genuine Sweet - Heart, with the best of intentions towards everyone. This quality can attract a lot of people towards you, especially since being near you can make them feel at ease. You likely have many admirers who also respect you dearly. You are probably the one person they would rather not loose in their life. The same way, they would want to be a special person in yours as well. So, many of your loved ones, friends & family can feel so blessed to have you in their life and be a part of yours. Your family and upbringing can inspire you greatly. You might also be quite fond of your culture and country. You can attract admirers from your place of birth, culture &/or country as well since they can feel a Sense of Kinship with you. 

You are Emotionally Mature and can be very understanding of others without biases. Your Compassion & Innate Emotional Understanding can make you an ideal person to seek help, support & advise from. So, you are likely to attract people who are in need of help. You can also attract a lot of children & animals. You might be regarded as a Support System, who is reliable and resourceful, so you can belong to a community that truly values you for who you are. 

You also make a great spouse who is trustworthy and admirable. The same can be true about being a devoted parent. So, your children may adore and respect you. Hence a loving Family, Fulfilling Marriage and Adoring Children can be your Major Inspiration and/or Goal in Life. There is an Innate Charm about you that can feel so comforting to many, especially those who are subconsciously longing for care, love & comfort. So, you can make any place that you walk into a hundred times more Vibrant and any relationship that much more Loving. 

DARK ATTRIBUTES OF SIRENE
You encounter people who can distract you with comfort & security. So, you likely come across those who wish to be resourceful and reliable towards/for you, but by taking away your Self-Sustainability and hence also your Self-Reliance. Hence, Codependent-People and the dynamics that they create with you can become extremely damaging to your Individual Sovereignty. You might be enticed with a loving and a fairly Equal Relationship, but soon realize that they are Emotionally Manipulative and Destructive to your own Sense of Safety (at least emotionally & psychologically). 

There is a Sense of involuntarily making you Sacrifice your True Self in order to stay connected to them and to pour in all of your effort & emotions into that relationship to keep it working no matter how destructive it may be. This can also be true with parents, children or spouse who wish to live off of your hard work or make you work for them (emotionally &/or financially). So, it is vital to have a (True) Solid Support System who can have your back in times when you desperately need them, and to feel safe when in such situations. This of course can also advise you to be careful who you wish to commit to and build a family with, since children or even a marriage can become a huge disappointing trap otherwise. 

If you grew up in a not so caring & loving family, you might have experienced the Negative Side of this Sirene placement where Emotional Manipulation was almost the basis of your childhood, making you feel so much more unsafe, insecure & unsupported in many aspects of your Life. Your vulnerabilities may have been used against you, so you might have learned many lessons about not being an Open-Book to everyone in terms of your emotions, and also to not give your compassion to everybody without first getting to know their True Intentions. You likely have learnt ‘not to let others mistake your Kindness for Weakness’ as well, demonstrating how strong and resilient you truly are.
