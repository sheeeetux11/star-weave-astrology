The Keys to your Innate Fortune & Luck with Natal Part of Fortune in Aries / 1H / 1° 13° 25° 🗝️🍀

Strong Sense of Self 

You will have many opportunities to develop a strong Self-Confidence and Immense belief in your own Capabilities. Above all with PoF here, a Positive Self-Image is Key to your Good Fortune. Your radiant Personality naturally attracts many admirers, friends, acquaintances, partners, etc. A strong Sense of Self is simply your Authenticity. Staying True to who you are will naturally bring you exactly the right kind of relationships, opportunities, situations, etc. With this PoF, you will have to learn how to be Loud & Proud of who you are, and unapologetically yourself. 

Independent + Self Reliant 

You naturally thrive in environments, relationships & careers where you can be Free from extra restrictions & bureaucratic constraints. You have excellent Leadership skills and a Charismatic personality that can inspire others around you. Although you work well in groups & teams, you are likely to find fulfilling Success when you take a Solo-Path to Career & Life as a whole. You can hence do well in Entrepreneurial, Business and/or Freelance endeavors that give you the flexibility to express your Innate Potential as a True Achiever. Relying on your Knowledge, Skills & Capabilities will bring out the best in you, unlocking many opportunities that feel more like an adventure.

Pioneering Spirit

Being a Leader not just in your Career but also in Life, you are likely to ‘march to the beat of your own drums’. This means you may be a little unconventional, or take on challenges that others might rather not. You will hence encounter the most satisfying Success when finding your own Path, following your own Dreams. So, you are likely to choose what you wish to achieve on your own terms, rather than to let someone else’s expectations, plans or advice influence you. This will lead you on a journey of both Self-Discovery and finding New Frontiers. Hence, finding your Niche, your own Passions & Inspirations will make you a Trailblazer in those fields (especially if you create a whole new Career Path in the the process, or set effortless Impressive Records in existing ones). 

Assertiveness

Being Spontaneous will bring you numerous Opportunities in all areas of your Life. A little bit of (calculated) recklessness, impulsivity and (dare I say) audacity will set you up for Success in many ways. This hence also emphasizes on being in-tune with your Intuition, as naturally moving through your Instincts should bring various opportunities. Your bold ‘decisiveness’ will be a Major factor in your Successes. This will give a Daring Quality to your Personality, further adding to your Courage and Self-Confidence.

Passions + Ambitions

Your Innate Talents & Skills along with Energetic Curiosity will need the right Direction to Focus and Achieve. Your Passionate and Immensely Ambitious Personality can take you to Greater Heights. Explore your Passions & Desires, Dreams & Aspirations, those Instinctual Goals that naturally light a spark of Inspiration within you. You need to focus all that Energy in a Positive, Productive & Fulfilling Direction. Loyalty towards your own Goals & Aspirations will naturally and effortlessly bring all the necessary Opportunities & then some more.

Courage + Determination 

One of your biggest strengths is in your Raw Determination & Courage. You are ‘a force to be reckoned with’, as when you Passionately set your sights on your Goals, you become Unstoppable. You have Immense Will-Power and often don’t back down from obstacles, competition, or even opposition. Your Bold and Ambitious nature will help you win even the most impossible battles in your life.

Take on Worthy Challenges

Your Life Path is what you choose for it to be with this PoF, and everything you do (and not do) has a huge impact on your Life. Take on challenges enthusiastically. You can take one step at a time, jump into things without much forethought or even stumble and make mistakes, but as long as you are following your Instincts & Passions, you are listening to your Heart and so all of this is worth it. Here, you will need to challenge yourself in order to keep bettering yourself, and every time you better yourself, you naturally reach New Heights of Success. More than competing with others, you can always compete with yourself, as your most worthy opponent is you yourself. You are also likely to meet people with whom you can have a healthy competition with as well. 

Pot of Blessings + Fortune 💰✨

You are blessed with a Positive Self-Image and an Optimistic Outlook on Life. You likely have an attractive body type (regardless of your gender), even a charming personality. Healthy obsession with fitness (Mars influence) can make you even more alluring. Your Boundless Energy naturally lights up the path to your Success. Your Passions bring Purpose & Clarity into your Life. You can naturally and skillfully navigate through Life’s Ups & Downs, where listening to your Intuition & Instincts lead you to a more Fulfilling Life. 

Your Inner Fortitude often gains the trust & respect of those around you. Your Authenticity opens the right Doors to the right Opportunities at the Right Place & Time. You need to put yourself out there to make the most of this Incredible placement, where your Personal & Public Image will lead you towards the Success that you wish to accomplish. Be energetic, if even a bit restless & eager to achieve your goals. Taking care of yourself, health-wise and aesthetic-wise can also bring many more Fortunate opportunities. A bit of healthy Self-Respect, Pride & Confidence can always do you wonders. 

You will find the most Success & Fortune when you are working towards New Endeavors, Individual Growth, Passionate Goals, Grand Ambitions, etc. anywhere you can be Yourself, and Inspire others. Fighting for yourself, your loved ones and others in need will also bring you Personal & Public Gratification. You will also learn how to choose your battles wisely, and how not to waste your effort towards meaningless action & goals. 

Seize opportunities, explore New Frontiers and build an Immaculate Self-Image through your Life’s Journey (with both your Achievements & Failures). Make as many mistakes as you can, learn the right lessons and stand up to try again, conquer your own Fears & inadequacies in the process. Achieve as many Goals as you want, reach however far & high as you can, ‘the World is your Oyster’. Most of all, love Yourself and commit to always uphold your Honor through achieving worthy Goals.
