Sirene Asteroid in Virgo +  6H  +  Mercury Aspects  +  6° 18°

LIGHT ATTRIBUTES OF SIRENE
You have the blessings of great Focus and Visualization. You have a sharp Intellect and can make the best use of your skills. You are also dedicated to your craft, causes and loved ones. You know how to make incredible works happen, since you can even calculate & handle all the details (no matter how big or small) all by yourself. You are also a good team player, you just need people who have the same work-ethic and passion as you do. So, it is not uncommon to meet people who only seem to cause troubles, but you can also meet those who perfectly fit into your career expectations. You can attract excellent friends, mentors & partners who support your dreams. 

You can also bring a community together to work towards a common cause. You voice your opinions very articulately, you can even inspire others to work for/with you. So, you make an excellent Co-worker, Boss, Business partner, etc. Furthermore, your down-to-earth, selfless, & humble demeanor can draw in many admirers of your character, elevating your Social status & respect. You might be blessed with an Innate Knowledge on medicine and so healing might come to you naturally. You can also find success in helping out the community with your knowledge & expertise. You can gain a lot of admirers who are often drawn to your ingenuity. You can be good at dealing with government related issues, documentation’s, legalities and bureaucracies since you can often be involved in those matters. So, you also seem to attract people who need your expertise, either as advise, suggestion or service. 

You can gain Notable Recognition and be respected by others, also have those who are thankful for your help/services. This Sirene placement can hence bring you opportunities to use your skills & knowledge towards positive causes, hence reaping Good Karma. This is a wonderful placement to be aware of, since though subtle and mundane, Sirene here can bring immense blessings, abundance & karmic rewards.

DARK ATTRIBUTES OF SIRENE
You encounter people who are almost desperate to steal your work and peace. You likely come across people who don’t seem to understand the value of effort, dedication, and self-discipline. These are the kind of people who rely on other people’s honest hard work to get ahead in life. So, this can be thievery, plagiarism or even through enslavement. Hence, you will have to be cautious of such people especially in your career field. You can see them as bosses, managers, coworkers, or even as clients, patients, etc. In one way or another, they are able to first appeal to your kindness or earn your trust and then slowly start making more ‘requests’, which become ‘demands’ overtime. Sometimes subtle, other times blatant. 

This can also be someone who holds a favor they did for you over your head and expects a few in return. Hence, be cautious of any contract that you sign or laws & regulations that can be unfairly used against you. There is a real sense of threat to your sovereignty and honor when are dealing with these people as well, since they don’t seem to have strong morals themselves, at least not really even if they are pretentious. They don’t seem to know how to work as a team with mutual understanding & equal respect. This can be a difficult placement if you are in careers/situations that need your service, where you will have to be unconditionally available or empathetic as part of your job/relationship; such as a medic, civic duty officer, nurse, social worker, etc. / child to a parent, a parent to a child, taking care of (may be even sickly) relatives, spouse, siblings, etc. 

So, you will have to be vigilant of those who can play the victim all while exploiting your services, empathy or efforts. They may even use underhanded manipulative tactics, belittle your capabilities, harsh criticism, etc. Since there is a karmic element to this placement, you will also need to understand the value of what you can offer to the world, and not underestimate your own accomplishments. ‘Not letting others to be entitled to your time, hard work & rewards while they disrespect, exploit and/or belittle you’, this will likely be a painful yet Empowering lesson in your life.
