Inter-generational aspect. Recent: 1990s, Expected next: 2080s. 

You are a rebel, lover, revolutionary and a warrior of justice. You have a unique personality that is captivating, even though all you are doing is being authentic. You rarely entertain superficial people/behaviors since your presence alone commands depth and originality. You have a strong inner character that never lets you betray your conscience. You value your intelligence, both emotional & mental. You have empathy for others, but you have no mercy for betrayers and cowards. You might have dealt with dishonest and manipulative people early on in your life especially those who were in your friend's circle or were elders with some sort of authority/control. Such encounters may have made you more certain about who is worthy of being in your life and who isn’t. 

You don’t let restrictive mindsets and outdated belief systems to hold you back, if anything, you might get offended by those who try to oppress you with such intentions. Your ideas and general behavior are often considered controversial by elders, making you a natural rebel who is often at odds with authoritative figures, yet your daring personality simultaneously inspires the younger generations. You are a fighter and a leader. You move forward, breaking shackles restriction, seeking freedom and passion, transforming limitations into possibilities. You don’t settle for co-dependent relationships (romantic, friendship, business, etc.) and hence you will always leave toxic situations once you realize that they are only holding you back. You have ideas & ideals that are far ahead of your time. You are passionate and undeterred in your resolve. 

Your opinions command attention. You are an influential figure. People are drawn to your depth, intelligence, electric personality and unique perspectives. You have the ability to find resourceful people who believe in your causes and help you in creating an idealistic future together. You are able to bring out the genius in others and help them find their highest potential with ease (this can be vice versa). You are hence quite inspiring. Your reputation may precede you. You might have initial troubles while working with groups, but you have the ability to lead and bring people together to work harmoniously. You are determined to do away with any toxic mindsets, systems, ideologies that have been detrimental to the society. But no elder before you (& sometimes your own generation) may have not bothered to change or eradicate them out of negligence or some archaic views on how a society needs certain necessary evils, which you consider to be inexcusable. 

Your creative, intuitive and spiritual capabilities are strong, giving you an almost prophetic ability to predict the future (both the good and the bad) since you’re good at picking up on patterns (and even intentions). This also means that you have a strong connection to the collective conscious. You are an excellent problem solver; you might have a keen interest in technological, scientific and/or mathematical fields. You might even have ambitions of inventing solutions to solve major issues or have plans for projects that contribute to the betterment of society, etc. You have a strong moral compass which allows you to make use of your gifts in a positive & productive way. You have incredible manifesting abilities; you have big aspirations and are determined to make your dreams a reality. You will always keep making incredible strides on your personal transformation, often elevating you instantly to greater levels, hence your passions will keep getting more intense, helping you reach your highest potential on your soul path.
