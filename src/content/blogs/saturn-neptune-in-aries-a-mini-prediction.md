Here’s one fragment of the prediction for the next 2-3 years. This period will be the most fulfilling for those that are truly spiritually grounded, recognize their Divine Path & Purpose in life, are willing to be patient and to go through mini-many-humbling moments without giving into their false/Shadow-Self and fragile ego tantrums. (Although this is not a personal, political or social reference, but a very real & collective one). Now, it will be a mask revealing time for those who are lost in their own delusions, deep insecurities, fear-induced character, etc. as they will become more & more delusional and lost in their own falsehood, unable to cope with reality-checks that Saturn brings during his transit here. They may become even more adamant that everything is going better than ever, continuing to desperately do all the wrong things expecting good returns. 

Saturn & Neptune entered Aries together (beginning of 2025). This greatly (and fortunately) set a Higher Vibrational precedent for Neptune in particular. Saturn although in his ‘fall’ position (in Aries) will still have a greater control over Neptune’s illusionary influence on the Collective’s sense of self (Neptune + Aries).Those walking on the path of Truth & Humility will of course have their own challenges, but will be immensely Rewarded by Saturn during (and especially after) his transit in Aries. While those who have lost the True Essence of Life, Hard-work & Humility will pay greatly (Karmically + Energetically).

Closing one’s eyes to Reality & Truth will not make it something else. Here, wishful thinking & frivolous way of life can easily turn into arrogance, which Saturn will love to correct by harsh/rude awakenings, anything to restore the Karmic & Dharmic Balance (both individually & as a collective).The effects of this intense transit combination will carry on as Saturn dips into Taurus (in 2028) and meets Chiron. Now that’s where things will become truly intense & interesting (with all the other Astrological influences), because honestly I can only pray it will be a hopeful time, since I shudder to think otherwise given the combination of Saturn & Chiron in Taurus. I might have been a little dramatic but, it has nothing to do with anything personal, political, or whatever else and everything to do with huge Energetic shifts on a collective level. So, the important thing is, we have a lot to ponder on about ourselves and who we are. Shadow, Light and all. So, I will be hopeful that this transit will help us reconnect to our True Self, our Light, with Love & Courage, for the collective Highest Good of us all.
