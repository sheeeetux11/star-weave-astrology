Sun Square & Inconjunct/Quincunx North Node

□^ You have a strong desire to succeed in life, although you are not always sure that you'll reach your goals. This aspect will teach you how "fortune favors the bold".
□^ You might have big dreams, goals and aspirations but feel incapable of achieving them.
□^ Your lack of confidence might even cause you to self-sabotage many opportunities to reach higher during your early years.
□^ You have a natural calling to put yourself in the spotlight and express your innate skills. But you might feel you won't be accepted or be ridiculed. You might even have unique talents that sets you apart from the crowd, but you might hold yourself back due to unfounded fears.
□^ You might even change directions from what you already know is your true purpose just to go through many hardships. You might eventually realize that you can only do things that bring you true happiness & satisfaction.
□^ So, you unconsciously manifest a path towards your purpose, this time facing your fears and insecurities. You will be surprised by how well you'll be recognized for being true to yourself.
□^ You have a strong sense of self but are also very self-conscious, this aspect will force you to accept all your flaws with your perfections and help you express yourself unapologetically.
□^ Your charm and magnetism draw in a crowd that truly admire you for who you are since you are authentic and original. This further empowers you to believe in yourself & your skills knowing full well that you are loved & appreciated.
□^ Your brave nature might intimidate many competitors, but you have the ability to stand tall even when facing challenges and conflicts which will only work in your favor eventually. 
□^ You will likely find self-acceptance in order to fulfil your goals; create works of inspiration; trust yourself to handle challenges bravely; find support and recognition by being true to yourself; gain respect, position, wealth, honor and satisfaction following your life path believing in yourself.

‌Sun Opposite North Node

☍ In turn you might have developed a healthy ego (sometimes even with low self-confidence) which makes you seem down to earth.

☍ There's a sense of steady progress with this aspect which gives you all the right tools/skills/opportunities to advance in your life mission.
☍ You might eventually encounter events that seem to unfold themselves as opportunities to a completely different reality, making you reluctant to the infinite possibilities.
☍ You might have to muster up a lot of courage to embark on this new path which could be a complete 360 to your earlier life even. But once you fully embrace the changes you will realize how everything in your life was always directing you to this exact path/moment. 
☍ You might even be thrust into spotlight all of a sudden or decide to take your skills to a bigger stage in search of a bigger audience which works out in your favor due to your refined skills & down-to-earth attitude to success.
☍ You will however have to maintain humility throughout your journey and face your future with confidence since even if you doubt your capabilities to handle a brand-new path you will eventually realize that your dedication will help you handle any challenges that may arise.
☍ You will likely embrace your destiny with confidence even when you at first doubt yourself; inspire many through your humility & charm; spread happiness, creativity, and positivity; gain wealth, position, honor, respect and prosperity following your life purpose trusting in your capabilities and higher guidance.
