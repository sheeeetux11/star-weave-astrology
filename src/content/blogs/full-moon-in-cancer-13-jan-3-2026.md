A truly magnificent start to the year with Full Moon in Cancer (Conjunct Jupiter). You may be feeling pressured to make some very important decisions in Significant areas of your life, especially in Family + Cancer. Although anxiety-inducing, this can also ignite your Passions and inspire you to take the right steps forward.

You likely feel grateful for the precious relationships, resources & stability in your life. A bit of fun & carefree lifelessness can alleviate some tension & stress during this time. Good rest & food, some much needed time-spent with loved ones can be most relaxing & blissful.

You are likely to be more excited about closing out old cycles & patterns, in order to open new chapters in life. Now more than ever, you may be feeling inspired to make big moves that align with your Grand Vision for the future. This can involve both your Professional & Private life, therefore you may be needing to pay close attention to your Long-term Plans & Ambitions. 

The Full Moon 🌕 brings a touch of Pragmatic Insight to an otherwise ultra Rational & Serious period in time. This Full Moon will hence prompt you to consider not just your Practical Mind but also your Intuitive Heart in the plans & decisions that you are crafting.Venus & Moon’s Opposition brings your attention towards what commitments you wish to make in your Professional/Career life, and what bonds you are choosing to cherish in your Personal Life. 

Evaluating what responsibilities and duties are both emotionally + karmically important, fulfilling + worth your dedication towards. You are encouraged to deliberate on the kind of relationships that you want to prosper and the kind of Ambitions to pursue. Some old goals may take on new Form while completing & closing out others.Essentially, you may be intuitively feeling the need to balance your Personal + Home life and Public + Career life. But instead, with clear emotional understanding, tact and placing boundaries where & when necessary. The Full Moon is hence also preparing you for the upcoming Saturn’s transit in Aries (leaving Pisces) through the changes and realignments that you are intuitively guided to make now. Meticulously clearing the path for your future Goals & Ambitions, to realize your Grand Dreams & Visions.

Make the most of this serendipitous Full Moon that nourishes you and your cherished loved ones along with your most Ambitious Goals. Cherishing relationships that are reciprocal, where you feel the love, support, effort, care & protection. Kindred Spiritual connections can be both manifested and nourished during this time.Long term plans, determined action & consistent effort will inevitably lead to Big Achievements in the coming weeks. Built a great foundation for your Grand Future unafraid and proud. Happy, Prosperous & Blessed New Year.
