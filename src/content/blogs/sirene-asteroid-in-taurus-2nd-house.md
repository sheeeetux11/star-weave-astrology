Sirene Asteroid in Taurus  +  2H  +  Venus Aspects  +  2° 14° 26°

LIGHT ATTRIBUTES OF SIRENE
Captivating personality (even physical appearance & body language). Your strong Character coupled with your Natural Sensuality can attract a lot of admirers. They can easily become quite possessive of you if you ever give them the honor of being in your inner circle. Your Value Systems & High Standards can intimidate a lot of people but can also inspire them to do the best for themselves the way that you do. You can tap into the abundance of your family lineage (even if you didn’t come from a rich family). 

You have a humble quality to you that can elude you from even your own magnetism, so having a strong Sense of Self and a High Self-esteem is essential with this placement. You can attract people who are equally Trust worthy, with High Moral Standards and Praise-worthy Character. Your romantic life can also bring wealth & success, either through Income/Inheritance and/or through Successful Financial ventures. Your Personal Style, Sense of Fashion, Self-Care, Body-Image & Fitness, etc. can be extremely alluring to others. 

You might have a captivating Voice as well, and coupled with excellent communication skills you can maintain good Social Networks of Resourceful people. You can also attract opportunities that will help you build the foundation of Security & Stability in all areas of your life. Focusing on building a Legacy & an Abundant Life can bring you many Achievements in Life. Creating wealth, security and comfort in life for both yourself and your loved ones can also make you well-adored and respected by others. You might even recognize how you have certain innate qualities, virtues, nobility & talents that are invaluable, which can bring you both Monetary Success and High Social Status.  

DARK ATTRIBUTES OF SIRENE
With this placement, it can be very easy to fall victim to fraudulent people, in romance, finance & life in general. You encounter those that try to portray themselves as someone quite Important & Valuable when in real they may likely have severe Low Self-esteem issues. The same way you can also come across people who are romantically (or even just sexually attracted to you) but deep within they don’t feel deserving of you. Financially, you can encounter con-artists here and there of course, the biggest Red Flags will however come from those around you, meaning your family, friends & associates who can take advantage of either your Lack of Self-worth in order to steal your hard-earned rewards in life, or to simply steal what you have. 

If you have Self-esteem issues, you can also become your own tough critique (especially of your body), this can either lead you into depressive/excessive eating or dieting beyond necessary, both can lead you into a Vicious Cycle if you are not careful. You might also encounter people who wish to ‘project their own issues’ onto you. If you are not careful, Sirene here can also make you extremely Money-minded, or you might meet people who are Money-obsessed and Shallow in personality. 

Being grounded and allowing space for gratitude will be very essential in avoiding the Negative side of this placement, hence avoiding people who don’t have such virtues will also be important. Seeing the Red Flags in others and standing firmly in your beliefs, values & standards can help you negate unpleasant experiences and avoid distractions (which may be created by both others & yourself).
