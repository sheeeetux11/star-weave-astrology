💞 A little respite from internal frustration, and instead taking the time for some Self-Love.

Whether they be about coming to terms with your True values, needs, wants & desires or what standards you wish to set for yourself, lifestyle, relationships & career, you will have a little “quite after the storm” moment to fully allow these changes to take effect internally and in your life.
