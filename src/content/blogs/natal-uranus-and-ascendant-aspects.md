Uranus Conjunct Ascendant 

☌ You’re a creative genius, with many talents & skills, full of great potential. Hence, from a young age you must have been able to accept your uniqueness and embrace all its gifts.
☌ You have an intense need for freedom to express yourself to the fullest. You rarely stay in restrictive places/situations/relationships.
☌ You have a vision in mind, and you likely won’t settle for anything less than extra-ordinary. You are also more inclined on working alone/for yourself than in groups/teams, although you’ve the potential to lead people and bring them together for a common cause, especially for social change & charity.
☌ You are knowledgeable, adventurous, and restless; always seeking new frontiers. You rarely ever settle for mundane routines, or jobs that don’t let you explore your ever expansive imagination. 
☌ You have an electric personality, you might tick people off with your brutal honesty, or your eccentric lifestyle. This is also why people love you, they might see you as a bold trendsetter, with a unique perspective on life, gaining you notoriety & popularity accordingly.
☌ Your ideas and visions are usually futuristic, making you think many steps ahead of the crowd. Most people might not be able to accept your opinions readily, but you’ll eventually gain support from those who have a similar mindset as you.
☌ You live life by a unique set of rules & morals, since you don’t necessarily follow traditions & norms, nor do you completely embrace them. You change with the tides of time, and so do your values.
☌ Your life is likely not normal. You might have experienced incredible things & events out of the blue, both good & bad. You gain maturity & wisdom through such incomprehensible (to others) situations. You might also actively seek exciting experiences, although you can easily be swept away into dangerous situations if you’re not being cautious. This can also mean that you need to be careful not to over-stimulate or under-stimulate your mind, since both extreme cases can cause serious damage to your self-confidence and mental health. 
☌ You’re in tune with your intuition (especially if Uranus is in 12H), this can prove to be your greatest asset. Since you know the importance of change and evolution, so you strongly discourage people to stay stuck & stagnant in comfortable places/situations. Your adaptability also gives you the ability to go with the flow and accept much needed changes often leading to better & brighter times. This can also be on a physical level, where you might suddenly change aspects of your appearance, lifestyle, attitude, etc. You might even be psychic with many spiritual gifts that you value a lot as you grow older.
☌ You’ll likely be a conduit for great change, both in your life and that of others; create incredible things/works/projects that help you showcase your intuitive/creative gifts; inspire many to embrace their uniqueness and to think from a different perspective; gain & share knowledge, wisdom, compassion, happiness and positivity.

Uranus Inconjunct/Qiuncunx Ascendant

^ You might have also been pushed to follow rules/traditions, live a certain lifestyle, or behave a certain way to suit your family/societal expectations, etc.
^ All those early life experiences, whether big or small, had a significant impact on your personality, and hence on your self-confidence.
^ You might have learnt a lot of lessons as the result of certain restrictions, that proved to be valuable to you as you grew older. (If not careful, you might rebel against all odds and welcome many troubles into your life, possibly even well into your adulthood, gaining notoriety for recklessness or irresponsible/disrespectful/unapologetic behaviors.)
^ You might have learnt the importance of patience, endurance, discipline, self-control and solemnness as part of your maturity. 
^ As you grew into adulthood, you might slowly get the chance to express yourself and explore all your untapped potentials.
^ All your early experiences & wisdom help you to stay grounded & humble enough to harness your latent talents and hence create many incredible changes in your life through the intentional use of your unique gifts.
^ You are once again able to embrace your personal intensity and present it in a more relaxed, controlled and acceptable way, which can gain you a lot of respect & popularity.
^ You’ll likely accept the need to harness your intense inner emotions & needs to direct them in a more responsible & productive way; create inspiring works/services that can make a positive impact on the world; use your incredible gifts to create necessary changes in your life and that of the others; gain & share wisdom, innovative ideas, happiness & positivity using your unique charm.

Uranus Opposite Ascendant (Conjunct Descendant)

☍ You might constantly feel the need to be defensive. You might have rebelled as a child, and those experiences might have left you feeling that’s the only way to react to people who have different opinions than yours.
☍ You might easily feel like you’re being pulled into a conflict even when others are stating their opinions, this is especially true if they’re talking about you. This can be vice versa, where you are the one constantly pushing people’s buttons & picking fights, even if it's just for thrills.
☍ You might have learnt however, that unnecessary confrontations, especially with those you love, never works out in the long run, since you might lose them over seemingly unimportant reasons.
☍ You might have learnt the importance of maturity, consistency, compromise and compassion through painful endings or situations.
☍ You might also be prone to justifying your actions by blaming others, or claiming to be unbothered but in reality, you might just be holding onto your ego and being stubborn.
☍ Your relationships in general may be a big, unusual & exciting chapter of your life, with many twists & turns. You might not really like some of the revelations that you get to find out or others may find out about you.
☍ You have an insatiable need for independence, yet you might want people who can keep up with you. This might not always work out in your favor unless the other person (especially in romantic terms) is also equally eccentric & adventurous as you’re.
☍ There may be both good and bad situations that can pop-up in your personal life with this aspect. You might either be interested in non-traditional kinds of relationships or find the perfect match who can be your happily-ever-after. Your friend's circle is also an important part of your life. 
☍ The more you engage in unsettling & unorthodox relationship dynamics (even in work partnerships, etc.) the more you might find yourself feeling ungrounded and prone to risk-taking behaviors to cope with such highly stimulating experiences. There may be a need to pace yourself, your eccentricity and your intense desire for freedom; since you might already be all that you ever wanted to be and have all that you hoped for, so now you might just have to take time to enjoy your blessings & hard work. 
☍ You’ll likely understand the importance of acknowledging others’ opinions just the way you value yours; learning how to pick your battles instead of living in constant conflict; cherishing significant connections and balancing your needs with that of theirs; allowing life’s lessons to humble your eccentricity (sometimes erratic side) to ground you and hence give you the opportunity to present your uniqueness with integrity & modesty; gaining & sharing wisdom, positivity, happiness and respect.
