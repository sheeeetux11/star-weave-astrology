Fresh Starts  •  Tough Decisions  •  Optimistic Outlooks

With the 🌑 Conjunct Sun & Jupiter in ♋️, the Summer starts with a Positive & Renewed Spirit. Along with a Sextile to Mars in Virgo, this is a Wonderful time for some much needed Energy Boost in both your day-to-day Life and your Career. You are likely to feel extra motivated to get started with Projects, Business Ideas & Ventures. Especially w.r.t. your Passions & Ambitions that also consequently elevate your Professional & Social Status. 

This brief period can be extremely stressful, with Head Aches and/or Stomach related issues. A sense of uneasiness and spikes in anxiety levels for seemingly no apparent reason. You are likely to make Important Life Style Changes that greatly benefit you. This can be true for your loved ones as well, where you may become more focused on their well-being and happiness. So, a good time to start healthy habits and cut back on unhealthy ones. Careful with seasonal or chronic conditions. Excellent time to learn more about nutrition & improvise on your recipes. Also, make time for some R&R 🥰.

There may however be some serious disruptions in your Home Life, where there can be conflicts or even big revelations. With Neptune & Saturn Squaring Moon, Jupiter & Sun, this New Moon will indeed be quite a Life-Changing one, with ripples of change becoming more & more obvious in the coming months. Take your time when making Serious Decisions as you may have to navigate through some Tough Choices & Uncertainty for a little while (say till the Next Full Moon). New Responsibilities whether concerning your own Personal Life or w.r.t. your Family can gently nudge you towards making Mature Decisions. 🐻

This is also an excellent time for some Deep Cleaning both in your Physical Environment and your Emotional Inner World. 🧸 Heal from Emotional and Psychological Mindsets, Thought-Patterns & Cycles that no longer serve your Highest Good. These Inner Changes will reflect on Key Relationship-dynamics making the bond stronger & more nurturing. The same can be true with cutting out relationships that bring only Emotional Turmoil & Instability. 

Overall a tremendously Intuitive & Enlightening New Moon. With your Emotional World revitalized and renewed, you will discover Newer Depths of your Inner World, an expansive space for more Growth, Compassion, Nurturing and Love. 🌱✨
