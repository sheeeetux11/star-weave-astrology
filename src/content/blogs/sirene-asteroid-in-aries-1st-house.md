Sirene (1009) Asteroid in Aries + 1st House + Conjunct Ascendant  + Mars Aspects + 1° 13° 25° 🐯✨

LIGHT ATTRIBUTES OF SIRENE
Your personality & presence are extremely alluring to others. Your self-confidence & self-assurance can mesmerize them, so your assertiveness can draw in a lot of people towards you. You will need a strong Sense of Self in order to make the best use of this placement, since this can be one of the most appealing qualities that can lead you to success & recognition. Your body-image, looks & body-language can be quite seductive as well, even if you don’t intend to, others may perceive you that way. Your Life Ambitions & Zealous approach can bring you numerous opportunities, since others can easily see the great potential within you. Although, patience & maturity can take you much further. 

Your independent & strong personality will attract challenges that will encourage you to win and stay dedicated to your passions. Your daring personality will attract the best of opportunities towards you. Having a clear sense of direction in life, steadily moving towards your personal ambitions & not backing down from your goals will bring many rewards. Invest your time & effort into self-care, physical fitness & sports, refining your body & personality. Your authentic and original personality will bring you Popularity and Immense Success. 

Your self-assuredness can also be infectious to others. You can draw in a lot of opportunities & admirers when you take a decisive stand and become a virtuous leader. Your fiery attitude and charisma can hence naturally attract those who believe in you & in your capabilities, and wish to follow you. Your assertive personality will bring the best out of you. Your self-confidence will keep you moving forward even after many setbacks. This Sirene Placement can hence help you create a life that can be truly fulfilling. With clear intentions & positive expectations, you can make the most of this placement indeed.

DARK ATTRIBUTES OF SIRENE
You can encounter people who feel compelled to steal your identity. Your character & personal charisma can intimidate these people, where “First they hate you, then they copy you” becomes quite a real scenario in your life. Hence, you can attract competition very often, some that are even very confrontational. This can also be in your personal relationships, so there may be a risk of intimate relationships that become dangerous or abusive on the extreme negative side of this placement. You might even encounter people who may be eager to fight you, or provoke you into physical altercations. 

This can also indicate that you can deceive yourself about your true-identity, where you can create a delusion of a personality that will bring you the things that you think you wish to conquer in life, which can be detrimental to both your public image and your mental health in the long run. It is also important not to let self-doubt to deter you from opportunities that can lead you towards your ambitions. Recklessness can also cost you your public image. If you are not careful, you can inadvertently create a self-fulfilling prophecy of not being enough, not being ready and/or not being capable. 

This Sirene placement can also indicate that either circumstances or your own fear of inadequacies can become major distractions, attracting people/places/situations that lead you further and further away from your Higher Ambitions. You might even miss out on many important opportunities to achieve your Goals as a result. Try not to take short cuts in life, especially if you feel impulsively drawn towards certain things (relationships, status, career goals, etc.). Complete your chosen journeys without feeling discouraged, since going the extra mile despite disappointments will lead to exceptional rewards.
